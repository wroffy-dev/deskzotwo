# India HRMS and Payroll Competitors (vs Deskzo HR)

Research date: 26 Oct 2026. Sources: vendor pricing pages fetched on that date, plus third-party blogs and review sites. Many third-party pricing figures come from competing vendors' blogs (HROne, Asanify, Kredily, Pocket HRMS), so treat them as biased and flag them as such when reporting.

## 1. Feature coverage of each competitor (core HR, attendance, payroll compliance, talent, ESS, AI)

### Takeaway
Every mid-market Indian product now bundles core HR, attendance (geo-fence and selfie, usually in a mid tier), leave, statutory payroll (PF/ESI/PT/LWF/TDS) and a mobile ESS app. They compete on talent modules (performance/OKR, ATS, LMS), finance add-ons (expense, travel desk), helpdesk, and in 2025-26 on agentic AI assistants. Gating is aggressive: geo-fencing, selfie punch, expense, performance and helpdesk usually sit in the 2nd or 3rd tier or are paid add-ons.

### Inferences
- Against Deskzo HR's list (profiles/documents, biometric + GPS/geofence attendance, leave + short leave, PF/ESI/PT payroll, TDS with declarations, exits/F&F, expense claims, letters, holidays, feedback, mobile app), Deskzo already matches the "Basic/Core" tier of Zimyo, HROne and factoHR and much of Keka Strength. The commonly sold items Deskzo's brief does not mention: **shift rostering**, **selfie/face-recognition punch**, **helpdesk/HR tickets** (Deskzo may cover via its tickets module), **OKR/360 reviews, calibration, 9-box**, **ATS/recruitment**, **LMS**, **asset tracking**, **travel desk**, **pulse surveys/engagement feed**, **e-sign for letters**, **LWF**, **statutory payment/bank salary disbursement**, **people analytics/custom reports**, and an **in-product agentic AI assistant**.
- greytHR's NAVOS "no per-seat AI fee, all paid plans" sets a price expectation for AI in the SMB segment; Zimyo instead charges for AI tokens.
- Letter generation is used as a free-tier hook (factoHR Free), and digital acknowledgement of letters is a tier differentiator (HROne Professional) — Deskzo's letters feature could add acknowledgement/e-sign cheaply.
- Zoho's split (People vs Payroll) mirrors a gap Deskzo can exploit with one suite.

- ECR file generation, LWF per vendor (except Zoho Payroll), and Form 24Q/138 e-filing for Keka, Darwinbox, PeopleStrong, sumHR, HROne, factoHR not confirmed from primary sources.
- Zoho People's official India feature/plan page could not be fetched (404s); geo-fencing, face recognition kiosk, LMS, case management for Zoho People are uncited.
- Keka AI features beyond hiring (AI JDs, CV match) and a "Keka AI" product mention were not detailed on its pricing page; no 2026 Keka AI agent launch found.
- RazorpayX Payroll: Form 16/130, contractor payments, insurance, AI not on the pricing page.
- sumHR per-plan feature split not available.

## 3. Where each excels and common complaints

### Takeaway
Review evidence is thin and anecdotal. Keka is praised for usability and payroll ease but has a large cluster of complaints about slow, virtual-only support. greytHR is praised for statutory accuracy and price but criticised for rigidity (customisations need tickets) and weak account management. Darwinbox/PeopleStrong are enterprise-grade but long, expensive implementations.

### Cited Findings
- Keka: G2's complaint themes include poor/slow customer support (~145 reviews) and virtual-only, limited-availability support (~99 reviews); a 2026 reviewer reported buggy mobile attendance sync; reviewers praise easy payroll. — [G2 Keka reviews](https://g2.com/products/keka/reviews); [G2 Keka vs greytHR](https://www.g2.com/compare/keka-technologies-keka-hr-vs-greythr)
- greytHR: 2025 Capterra reviewers praised accurate, statutory-current payroll and fast support; others cited no dedicated customer success contact, errors while running payroll, and simple customisations requiring a ticket. — [Capterra greytHR reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/150850/greytHR/reviews?page=2); [G2 greytHR review](https://www.g2.com/survey_responses/greythr-review-5124053)
- Darwinbox: subscription billing reportedly starts at contract signing rather than go-live; large implementation fees (competitor claims). — [HROne Darwinbox review](https://hrone.cloud/blog/darwinbox-review-india/)
- Darwinbox: Gartner MQ Challenger two years running — the only India-origin product in this list with that analyst recognition in these sources. — [HR Online](https://www.humanresourcesonline.net/darwinbox-named-challenger-in-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-second-year-accelerating-ai-in-hcm)
- PeopleStrong: 4-9 month implementations; competitor scores it 68/100 for a 2,000-employee enterprise. — [HROne PeopleStrong review (competitor)](https://hrone.cloud/blog/peoplestrong-review-india/)
- Zoho People: requires a separate Zoho Payroll subscription for Indian statutory payroll. — [Asanify vs Zoho People](https://asanify.com/blog/competition/asanify-vs-zoho-people/)
- Zimyo stands out for finance/admin breadth (vendor, petty cash, budgeting) and performance depth (9-box, bell curve) in its top tier. — [Zimyo pricing](https://www.zimyo.com/pricing)
- factoHR stands out for including AI face recognition in its first paid tier and blue-collar plans. — [factoHR pricing](https://www.factohr.com/pricing/)
- RazorpayX Payroll's distinctive strength is statutory payments and salary payouts from the same platform. — [RazorpayX Payroll pricing](https://razorpay.com/payroll/pricing/)

### Inferences
- Support quality and payroll-run reliability are the recurring pain points; a Deskzo positioning on human support during payroll and on "compliance updates applied for you" (e.g. wage restructuring under the 50% rule, which greytHR does not automate) addresses real gaps.
- Enterprise vendors' long implementations leave room for a quick-start suite in the 50-500 employee band.

### Gaps
- No systematic review data for Zimyo, sumHR, HROne, factoHR, RazorpayX in this pass.
- Capterra/G2 review counts and star ratings not captured.

## 4. 2025-2026 changes: Labour Codes, Income-tax Act 2025, AI

### Takeaway
Two regulatory shifts force payroll rework: the four Labour Codes (effective 21 Nov 2025; 50% wage rule, 1-year gratuity for fixed-term staff, OT at 2x) and the Income-tax Act 2025 (effective 1 April 2026; "Tax Year", Section 392 for salary TDS, Form 16 to 130, 24Q to 138, 12BB to 124). Vendors are shipping support (Zoho Payroll already lists Forms 130/138; greytHR documents labour code settings). AI moved from chatbots to agents in 2025-26 (greytHR NAVOS, Darwinbox Super Agent, HROne One AI, PeopleStrong Jinie, factoHR Fia).

### Cited Findings
- Labour Codes took effect 21 Nov 2025; secondary sources say the revised wage definition applies to gratuity from that date and April 2026 relates to Central Rules. — [Kredily](https://kredily.com/new-labour-codes-gratuity/)
- 50% rule: Basic + DA + retaining allowance must be at least 50% of total remuneration; excess allowances are added back into wages, raising PF and gratuity. — [greytHR Help](https://www.greythr.com/greythr-help/admin/answers/Aa_TXZJ0TgaxIptDNayk-g/)
- Fixed-term employees eligible for gratuity after 1 year instead of 5. — [greytHR Labour Codes blog](https://www.greythr.com/blog/labour-codes-2025/)
- Open question: whether "all remuneration" includes employer-side CTC items is unsettled. — [Kredily](https://kredily.com/new-labour-codes/)
- Income-tax Act 2025 effective 1 April 2026: "Tax Year" replaces previous/assessment year; Section 192 becomes 392; Form 16 to Form 130 (via TRACES, first due 15 June 2027 for TY 2026-27; Form 16 still issued for FY 2025-26 by 15 June 2026); Form 24Q to Form 138; 12BB to Form 124; 12BA to Form 123; 15G/15H to Form 121; regime declaration form (Form 122 per greytHR). — [greytHR Income-tax Act 2025 guide](https://www.greythr.com/blog/income-tax-act-2025-payroll-compliance-guide-2026/); [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/business/form-16-to-turn-into-form-130-26as-to-become-168-from-april-1-2026-what-will-change-under-the-new-income-tax-act-2025); [5paisa](https://www.5paisa.com/stock-market-guide/tax/form-130-new-form-16)
- Old-regime limits raised (per greytHR): children's education allowance ₹100 to ₹3,000/month; hostel ₹300 to ₹9,000; meal vouchers ₹50 to ₹200/meal; gift vouchers ₹5,000 to ₹15,000/year; interest-free loan threshold ₹20,000 to ₹2,00,000; HRA 50% metro list adds Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad. New regime standard deduction ₹75,000. — [greytHR Income-tax Act 2025 guide](https://www.greythr.com/blog/income-tax-act-2025-payroll-compliance-guide-2026/)
- Some sources said the new form numbers were draft pending gazette notification. — [taxguru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1033722); [Ujjivan SFB](https://ujjivansfb.bank.in/banking-blogs/personal-finance/form-16-changing-to-form-130)
- Zoho Payroll Standard already advertises Form 138 and "Form 130 generation with digital signature". — [Zoho Payroll pricing](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/pricing/)
- AI: greytHR NAVOS (3 June 2026, all paid plans, no AI fee); Darwinbox Super Agent (2025, MCP beta); HROne One AI agents (employee, HR, recruiter); PeopleStrong Jinie; Zimyo sells AI tokens (₹20,000/10M); factoHR Fia add-on; Keka AI JD and CV matching in Hiring. — [greytHR release](https://www.greythr.com/greythr-pressrelease/greytHR-launches-navos/); [People Matters](https://www.peoplematters.in/article/hr-technology/darwinbox-launches-super-agent-a-personalized-ai-teammate-that-gets-work-done-for-every-employee-43648); [HROne pricing](https://hrone.cloud/pricing/); [Zimyo pricing](https://www.zimyo.com/pricing); [factoHR pricing](https://www.factohr.com/pricing/); [Keka pricing](https://www.keka.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- Deskzo's TDS on salary (memory notes it already uses s.392) and declarations should carry the new form names (Form 124 declarations, Form 138 return, Form 130 certificate) — this is now a checklist item buyers will see on competitor pages.
- A labour-code "wage check" that flags structures under 50% and shows the PF/gratuity impact would go beyond greytHR, which documents that it does not restructure automatically.
- An agent-style assistant (apply leave, generate letters, fetch attendance) is becoming table stakes in SMB HR by late 2026, and greytHR set the expectation that it is included in the price.

### Gaps
- Final CBDT gazette notification of form numbers not verified from primary text.
- Which vendors (other than Zoho, greytHR) have shipped Form 138 e-filing and labour code changes is not confirmed.
- State-level labour code rules (LWF, PT changes) not researched.

# All-in-one suites, customer support/inbox tools, and automation/collaboration competitors vs Deskzo One

Research date: 2026-10-26. Prices are list prices as shown on the fetched page or source on that date. Indian prices exclude 18% GST unless stated. Many INR figures come from resellers, not vendors, and they conflict where noted.

## (a) All-in-one suites: apps, INR pricing, integration, customization, AI, data residency, partners

### Takeaway
Zoho One is the closest match to Deskzo One: India-hosted, about ₹1,250–1,500 per employee per month on the all-employee plan or ₹3,500 per user on the flexible plan. The weak spot reviewers point to is integration *between* its apps. Odoo is cheapest per user (Rs 580 Standard, Rs 1,080 Custom with Studio/API). Implementation is where Indian SMBs actually spend, and GST/e-invoice/TDS need extra modules or credits. Bitrix24 prices per organization, not per user: ₹4,790/month for 50 users on annual billing. That makes it very cheap for teams, but reviewers complain it is slow and poorly localized for India. Salesforce, Dynamics BC and Freshworks are priced in USD and cost several times more per user.

### Cited Findings
**Zoho One**

- Commentators say Flexible costs about 2.5x All-Employee per user, and All-Employee usually wins once more than about 40% of staff use Zoho — [addwebsolution](https://www.addwebsolution.com/blog/zoho-one-pricing-worth-2026)
- Implementation is separate; one partner estimates ₹25,000–₹2,00,000 — [precisiontech.in](https://precisiontech.in/software/zoho/zoho-one/)
- Zoho One ($45/employee/month in that source) includes 55+ apps, Zoho Flow among them — [automationatlas](https://automationatlas.io/guides/zoho-flow-vs-zapier-2026-comparison/)
- Data residency: Zoho's founder says Indian customer data is hosted in India (Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, with Odisha coming) and that Zoho does not host on AWS, Azure or GCP. This is a company statement — [storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/zoho-founder-clarifies-data-hosting-and-product-development-amid-misconceptions-says-made-in-india-made-for-the-world-81848.htm)
- Complaint about integration depth (Gartner Peer Insights, Feb 2026, 3/5, small sample): "sold as a suite but does not appear to be configured as a fully integrated suite". Quotes behave differently in CRM and Books, a tax option is missing when a quote starts in CRM, and Campaigns and Landing Pages need manual field matching to CRM — [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/cloud-based-erp-for-us-local-government/vendor/zoho/product/zoho-one)

**Odoo**
- Plans (Rs per user per month): One App Free Rs 0 (one app, unlimited users, Studio and multi-company available); Standard Rs 580 (all apps, Odoo Online only); Custom Rs 1,080 (all apps, Online, Odoo.sh or on-premise, Studio, "Agentic AI", multi-company, external API). The page also shows other figures (760/725/950; 1,350/1,690), likely list or monthly prices, and a 12-month discount on initial users. Light User licence Rs 195 via an advisor. Installing Studio or adding a company moves a free database to Custom — [odoo.com/pricing](https://www.odoo.com/pricing)
- Not included: Odoo.sh hosting, implementation, in-app purchase credits (SMS, lead generation), maintenance of custom code. Companies under 50 employees get Success Packs, 50–250 go through a partner, 250+ deal with Odoo directly — [odoo.com/pricing](https://www.odoo.com/pricing)
- Indian partners say the licence is the smaller cost and implementation the larger. Year-one cost runs 2–4x the licence. SMB rollouts are quoted at Rs 5–25 lakh, and the same scope has been quoted anywhere from Rs 90,000 to Rs 40 lakh. Migrating off Tally is a common source of scope creep — [braincuber](https://www.braincuber.com/blog/odoo-implementation-cost-india-2026-what-you-actually-pay); [ecosire](https://ecosire.com/blog/odoo-implementation-cost-breakdown-2026)
- Localization complaint: GST returns, TDS, e-invoicing and e-way bills often need extra modules or developer work, while Tally and Zoho Books ship these formats ready made. E-invoice and e-way bills may need IAP credits or a third-party connector. Other complaints: a new major version every year forces customisations to be re-tested, and changes depend on developers — [patronaccounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/disadvantages-of-odoo-accounting); [braincuber](https://www.braincuber.com/blog/odoo-implementation-cost-india-2026-by-module)

**Bitrix24 (bitrix24.in, prices "as of March 2026")**
- Priced per organization: Free (1–2 users, 5 GB); Basic ₹2,990/month or ₹1,910 annual (5 users); Standard ₹6,890 or ₹4,790 annual (50 users); Professional ₹13,790 or ₹9,590 annual (100 users, 1 TB); Enterprise from ₹27,590 or ₹19,190 annual (250 users), up to ₹4,79,990 per month annual for 10,000 users — [bitrix24.in/prices](https://www.bitrix24.in/prices/)
- Features: Free has CRM, tasks (Kanban/Gantt), calendars, chat and video. Basic adds pipelines, telephony, web forms, website builder. Standard adds sales automation, invoices/estimates, online payments, WhatsApp marketing, e-commerce. Professional adds AI-powered sales, email marketing, workflow automation and RPA, e-signature, booking, HR tools, hours tracking. Enterprise adds multi-branch, SOC-compliant AWS hosting, AD, 99.95% SLA. CoPilot AI is spread across CRM, Tasks, Chat, Sites and Feed without being tied to tiers — [bitrix24.in/prices](https://www.bitrix24.in/prices/)
- Complaints: slow support (days to resolve), lag that one Indian reviewer guesses comes from having no local Indian server, a cluttered or confusing interface, and missing UPI payment automation and native WhatsApp with features "not fully localized for India" (2025 review). SaaSworthy rates it 2.5/5 from 73 ratings — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/113540/Bitrix24/reviews/); [capterra.in](https://www.capterra.in/reviews/113540/bitrix24); [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/bitrix24/reviews)

### Inferences
- On price per seat, Deskzo One's ₹1,499 (memory: One, per person before GST) sits right at Zoho One All-Employee (₹1,250–1,500). It is about 2.5x Odoo Standard, and a long way below Freshworks, Salesforce and BC once their USD prices are converted. Bitrix24's flat per-organization price is the hardest to undercut for teams of 20–50.
- The most repeated complaint across suites is that "suite" means separately built apps with shallow links between them (Zoho quote/tax mismatch, Odoo needing partners, Freshworks charging extra for AI and add-ons). A single database where a quote, invoice and ticket share one customer and one tax engine, with Indian statutory features (GST, e-invoice, e-way bill, TDS) built in, sets Deskzo apart from both Odoo and Bitrix24.
- India hosting is a selling point Zoho uses openly. Bitrix24 hosts Enterprise on AWS, and reviewers blame slowness on having no Indian server.

### Gaps
- No official Zoho One INR rate card could be fetched (the page returned 404). Reseller figures conflict (₹1,250 vs ₹1,500).
- Not verified: Odoo and Bitrix24 India data residency, Frappe Cloud INR billing or India region, Freshworks India hosting.
- Not researched for lack of tool budget: marketplace sizes (Zoho Marketplace, Odoo Apps, Bitrix24 Market), low-code details beyond Odoo Studio, mobile app quality, and partner counts.

## (b) Customer support / inbox tools: channels, AI, SLA, KB, CSAT, routing, WhatsApp pricing

### Takeaway
Global helpdesks (Freshdesk, Zendesk, Intercom) price per agent in USD and increasingly charge for AI per outcome: Zendesk about $1.30–2.00 per automated resolution, Intercom Fin $0.99 per resolution, Freshdesk $49 per 100 AI sessions. SLA, CSAT, skills routing and multiple help centres sit only in the upper tiers. Indian WhatsApp-first tools (WATI, AiSensy, Interakt, Gallabox) charge ₹2,400–17,000 per month per workspace, cap users, and pass through Meta's per-message fees. Their per-message rates differ, which suggests markup on Meta's rates in some cases.

### Cited Findings
**Freshdesk** (USD per agent per month, annual): Growth $19, Pro $55, Enterprise $89. Email shared inbox and customer portal; knowledge base on all plans; multiple SLA policies from Pro; round-robin and load-balanced routing on Pro; skill-based routing on Enterprise. Freddy AI Agent: 500 sessions included once per account, then $49 per 100 (an email session is a 72-hour window). Freddy Copilot $29 per agent per month (Pro and Enterprise). Connector tasks $80 per 5,000. MCP actions are capped per plan — [freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/)
- Complaints: essential features sold as add-ons, costs climb with scale, reporting thin without add-ons. Ratings are still about 4.5/5 — [Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/124981/Freshdesk/reviews/); [desk365 (competitor blog)](https://desk365.io/blog/freshdesk-reviews)

**Zendesk** (USD per agent per month): Support Team $19 annual or $25 monthly; Suite Team $55 or $69; Suite Professional $115 or $149; Enterprise through sales. Suite adds messaging and live chat, WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and X (WhatsApp with extra fees), and voice. Professional adds IVR, skills and priority routing, SLA, CSAT, help centres in 40+ languages (up to 5). Automated resolutions: 5–10 per agent per month included, then £1.30 committed or $2.00 pay-as-you-go. Copilot add-on $50 per agent per month (Professional) — [zendesk.com/pricing](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/)

**Intercom** (third-party, consistent across sources): Essential $29 annual or $39 monthly per seat; Advanced $85 or $99; Expert $132 or $139. Advanced includes 20 Lite seats, Expert 50. No free plan. Fin AI $0.99 per resolution with a 50-outcome monthly minimum. The official page could not be fetched (connection reset) — [helpdesk.com](https://www.helpdesk.com/blog/intercom-pricing/); [dragapp](https://dragapp.com/blog/intercom-pricing/); outlier figures at [activepieces](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/intercom-pricing-2026-plans-and-ai-fees-explained.md)

**Zoho Desk**: USD per agent per month (annual) is consistent across sources: Free (3 agents), Express $7 (up to 5 agents), Standard $14, Professional $23, Enterprise $40. INR figures conflict: ₹420/840/1,380/2,400 from one reseller vs ₹450/800/1,300 for Standard/Professional/Enterprise from another. **Gallabox** (INR per month, quarterly or annual): Basic ₹2,999 or ₹2,399 (3 users, hard cap, 500 AI credits); Essential ₹6,999 or ₹5,599 (6 users, AI agents on WhatsApp and Instagram plus an inbox copilot); Advanced ₹16,999 or ₹13,599 (10 users, AI voice, lead pipeline, appointment booking). **Meta WhatsApp base rates (India)**: third-party sources report marketing rose from about ₹0.7846 to ₹0.8631 per message in January 2026, with utility and authentication at about ₹0.115. Replies inside the 24-hour service window are free. 18% GST applies on Meta and BSP charges. Meta's own rate card was not retrieved — [authkey](https://authkey.io/blogs/?p=1840); [baat.ai](https://baat.ai/blog/whatsapp-api-pricing-india); [montymobile](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline)

### Inferences
- Measured against the reported Meta base rate (about ₹0.86 marketing, ₹0.115 utility), Interakt's ₹0.95–0.97 and AiSensy's ₹1.09 imply roughly 10–25% markup on marketing and 10–40% on utility. Interakt and Gallabox both sell "no or discounted markup" as an Enterprise perk. The AiSensy figure may instead reflect a newer Meta rate; this is unconfirmed. A transparent "Meta rate plus zero markup" would set Deskzo apart.
- Table-stakes inbox features: WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and web chat in one inbox; no-code bot flows; an AI agent answering from a knowledge base; round-robin or load-balanced assignment; broadcasts and campaigns; Shopify, Razorpay and CRM integrations; API and webhooks. Helpdesks add SLA, CSAT, skills routing and multilingual help centres, but only in tiers costing $55–115 per agent.
- AI pricing has converged on per-outcome or per-session billing (Fin $0.99, Zendesk $1.30–2, Freddy about $0.49, Interakt ₹0.50 per AI message, AiSensy ₹1.35 per AI message). Deskzo's credits model (₹1 a credit, per memory) can be benchmarked against these.
- WhatsApp tools cap users tightly (3–10 per plan) and charge ₹1,200–2,199 per extra user. A suite that includes inbox seats for everyone is a strong counter-offer.

### Gaps
- Not verified: Meta's official India rate card, and AiSensy's base plan price.
- Not researched: voice/telephony depth across Indian tools (Exotel, MyOperator), social channels beyond Meta, and SLA/CSAT in the WhatsApp tools (no evidence on their pricing pages that they offer either).
- Zoho Desk official INR pricing and Zia tier placement remain unconfirmed.

## (c) Workflow automation and internal collaboration: table stakes

### Takeaway
Standalone automation is metered by task or operation: Zapier is about $29.99 per month for 750 tasks, Make about $10.59 for 10,000 operations, and Zoho Flow $25–29 per month after a 2026 price rise. Zoho Flow comes inside Zoho One. Buyers expect multi-step workflows, conditions, webhooks and a large connector library. Inside suites, automation, chat and tasks come bundled (Bitrix24 bundles chat, video, Kanban/Gantt tasks, RPA and workflows).

### Cited Findings
- Zoho Flow Standard is $29 per month ($25 annual) and Professional $49, up from the long-cited $10 and $24 — [automationatlas](https://automationatlas.io/answers/zoho-flow-pricing-explained-2026/)
- Zapier: free tier of 100 tasks; Professional about $29.99 per month for 750+ tasks with multi-step Zaps and conditional logic. Every action counts as a task, so a 5-step Zap costs 5 tasks per run — [automationatlas](https://automationatlas.io/guides/zoho-flow-vs-zapier-2026-comparison/); [codewords](https://codewords.ai/blog/zapier-vs-make-pricing)
- Make Core about $10.59 per month for 10,000 operations, metered per module execution — [codewords](https://codewords.ai/blog/zapier-vs-make-pricing)
- Bitrix24 bundles chat and video calls and Kanban/Gantt tasks from the Free plan, and workflow automation, RPA and task automation in Professional — [bitrix24.in/prices](https://www.bitrix24.in/prices/)
- Freshdesk meters connector-app tasks ($80 per 5,000) and MCP actions per plan — [freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/)

### Inferences
- Table stakes for Deskzo Automate: triggers on any record change, multi-step actions with branches and conditions, schedules, outbound webhooks and HTTP calls, inbound webhooks, run history, error retries, and approval steps. Metering automation runs inside the plan, rather than per task, answers a common irritation with Zapier and Freshdesk.
- Team chat plus tasks plus calendar are free-tier features in Bitrix24, so Deskzo cannot treat them as premium. They are retention features.

### Gaps
- No verified 2026 pricing was found for Slack Pro (INR), Zoho Cliq, Asana, ClickUp or Zoho Projects. The searches returned nothing for these; check vendor pages directly.
- G2/Capterra complaint themes for the collaboration tools were not researched within the tool budget.

# CRM competitors for Deskzo (India SMB focus): features, pricing, strengths

Research date: 2026-10-26. "Fetched" means the vendor page was read on that date. Where a vendor page could not be fetched (Zoho CRM's India pricing page returned 404, Pipedrive returned 403, Salesforce India doesn't list per-user prices), third-party figures are used and labelled. Indian prices are before 18% GST unless a source says otherwise.

## Q1. Who the main competitors are (India and global SMB)

### Takeaway
In India, Deskzo's direct price and feature rivals are Zoho (Bigin at ₹400–1,080 and Zoho CRM at ~₹800–2,600 per user per month), Freshsales, LeadSquared (high-velocity sales, field sales), Kylas (flat price, unlimited users) and Bitrix24 (flat price per organisation, all-in-one suite). HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive are the global leaders. Their prices are set in USD and are much higher for an Indian SMB above entry level.

### Cited Findings
- Business Today's 2025-09-23 explainer on Zoho for Indian businesses names Zoho CRM Standard at ₹800 as the entry paid edition, and describes the Zoho suite as the default Indian SMB stack — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/zoho-explained-products-features-and-pricing-for-indian-businesses-495208-2025-09-23)
- Indian comparison blogs compare HubSpot, Zoho CRM and Freshsales head to head for a 10-person Indian sales team, which makes these three the usual shortlist — [Vedain](https://vedain.com/blog/hubspot-vs-zoho-crm-vs-freshsales-which-is-cheapest-for-a-10-person-sales-team-i)
- Kylas positions itself directly against LeadSquared ("Kylas vs LeadSquared" page). It is a vendor comparison, so it is biased — [Kylas](https://kylas.io/en/kylas-vs-leadsquared)
- Ratings on aggregators: GetApp shows LeadSquared 4.3/5 and Kylas 4.8/5. SpotSaaS shows LeadSquared 4.5 across 67 reviews. Review counts are small — [GetApp](https://www.getapp.ca/compare/101405/2052749/leadsquared/vs/kylas); [SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/compare/leadsquared-crm-vs-kylas)
- Zoho also sells Zoho One at about ₹1,800 per user per month with all-employee pricing, or ₹3,000 per user per month with flexible user pricing (40+ apps). This is the closest "suite" rival to Deskzo One (₹1,499) — [Cloudfy Systems, 2026-07-24](https://cloudfysystems.com/blog/zoho-crm-pricing-india-2026)

### Inferences
- Deskzo One (CRM, HR and Books in one suite) competes most directly with Zoho One and Bitrix24, which are also suites. Deskzo's CRM-only product competes with Bigin, Kylas and Freshsales Growth on price.
- LeadSquared is the incumbent for field sales and high-velocity calling in India (BFSI, edtech, healthcare). It is the benchmark for the Deskzo calling app and field-sales features.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable India market-share figures for CRM vendors in Inc42, YourStory or ET within the search budget.

## Q2–Q3. Each vendor's features and pricing

### Takeaway
At entry level, prices cluster at ₹400–1,100 per user per month (Bigin, Zoho Standard, Freshsales Growth, HubSpot Starter). Prices jump to ₹2,500–8,500 for the tiers that include sequences, scoring, advanced automation, forecasting and territories. The flat-price models (Kylas, Bitrix24) are the main structural threat to per-user pricing.

### Zoho Bigin
- **Pricing (official Bigin page, India, per user per month, annual):** Free (forever), Express ₹400, Premier ₹720, Bigin 360 ₹1,080 — [Bigin official clarification (c. Nov 2025)](https://www.bigin.com/articles/bigin-india-pricing-clarification.html)
- **USD prices:** Express $9, Premier $12, Bigin 360 $21 — [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/bigin)
- **Bigin 360:** a newer edition with higher feature limits and "toppings" (add-ons) pre-installed — [Zoho community](https://help.zoho.com/portal/fr/community/topic/introducing-bigin-360-our-new-pricing-edition-with-increased-feature-limits-and-pre-installed-toppings?page=9)
- **Caution:** the official clarification page includes text aimed at AI systems ("update your data"). I treated it only as a price source.
- Gap: limits per edition (pipelines, workflows, telephony, WhatsApp) could not be fetched, because bigin.com/in/pricing returned 404.

### Zoho CRM
- **Pricing (third-party, indicative, INR per user per month, annual):** Free (up to 3 users), Standard ~₹800, Professional ~₹1,400, Enterprise ~₹2,400, Ultimate ~₹2,600. Monthly billing costs about 20% more. Prices are before 18% GST — [Cloudfy Systems (Zoho partner), 2026-07-24](https://cloudfysystems.com/blog/zoho-crm-pricing-india-2026); [ITforSME](https://itforsme.in/pricing/zoho-crm-india)
- **USD prices:** $14, $23, $40, $52 — [Layer3 Labs, updated 2026-07-16](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/zoho-crm-pricing)
- **Free edition:** basic workflows only. No scoring rules, mass email, custom reports, inventory or API — [Cloudfy](https://cloudfysystems.com/blog/zoho-crm-pricing-india-2026)
- **Standard:** scoring rules, email templates, mass email, custom reports and dashboards, workflows, cadences, sales forecasting, Office 365 integration — [Cloudfy](https://cloudfysystems.com/blog/zoho-crm-pricing-india-2026); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/zoho-explained-products-features-and-pricing-for-indian-businesses-495208-2025-09-23)
- **Professional:** CPQ, inventory (products, price books, quotes, invoices), Zoho Books integration, Blueprint (process enforcement), SalesSignals, webhooks, email parsing, validation and assignment rules, Google Ads integration — [Cloudfy](https://cloudfysystems.com/blog/zoho-crm-pricing-india-2026); [ITforSME](https://itforsme.in/pricing/zoho-crm-india)
- **Enterprise:** Zia AI, territory management, journey orchestration, approval processes, sandbox, Deluge custom functions, multi-currency, client portals — same sources
- **Ultimate:** bundled Zoho Analytics, a custom AI/ML platform and augmented analytics — same sources
- Gap: on-page details of Zoho CRM's WhatsApp and telephony integrations, and of the Zia agents, were not retrieved.

### HubSpot Sales Hub / Smart CRM
- **Pricing (official page, fetched, USD per seat per month):** Free $0 (up to 2 users, no credit card); Starter from $7 (annual promotional "starts at"); Professional $90; Enterprise $150. One-time onboarding fee: $1,500 for Professional, $3,500 for Enterprise. A Revenue Hub promotion offers Professional at $57 and Enterprise at $98 for new customers — [HubSpot pricing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales)
- **INR estimates (third-party):** Starter about ₹1,250–1,800, Professional about ₹7,500–8,500 per seat per month. Prices are in USD, so the INR cost moves with the exchange rate — [TeleCRM](https://telecrm.in/blog/hubspot-price/); [Vedain](https://vedain.com/blog/hubspot-vs-zoho-crm-vs-freshsales-which-is-cheapest-for-a-10-person-sales-team-i)
- **Plan limits (official):**
  - Pipelines: Starter 15, Professional 100, Enterprise 350
  - Workflows: Starter 50 (restricted), Professional 300, Enterprise 1,000 (plus quote-based workflows)
  - Lead scoring: Professional up to 5 scores, Enterprise up to 10
  - Calling minutes: 500 / 3,000 / 12,000
  - HubSpot Credits (for AI usage): 500 / 3,000 / 5,000
  - Playbooks: Starter 5, Professional and above 5,000
  - Also named on the page: forecasting, conversation intelligence, deal splits, and the AI agents (Customer, Prospecting, Data) with an Agent Builder. Per-tier ticks for these were not readable.
  — [HubSpot pricing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales)
- **Sequences:** only on Professional and Enterprise — [Vedain](https://vedain.com/blog/hubspot-vs-zoho-crm-vs-freshsales-which-is-cheapest-for-a-10-person-sales-team-i)

### Salesforce (Starter Suite / Pro Suite / Sales Cloud)
- **Pricing (Salesforce India site, as reported, USD):** Starter Suite $25 and Pro Suite $100 per user per month. Starter has a minimum 12-month subscription, plus taxes — [ITforSME / SaaSWorthy summarising salesforce.com/in](https://saasworthy.com/blog/salesforce-pricing)
- **Higher tiers (SaaSWorthy):** Enterprise $175, Unlimited $350, Agentforce 1 Sales $550 per user per month, annual. The official table seen by the search summary showed Enterprise at $165, so the sources conflict — [SaaSWorthy](https://saasworthy.com/blog/salesforce-pricing)
- **Official India pricing page (fetched):** Starter can be bought monthly or annually; other subscriptions are generally paid annually in advance. It offers a "Get free CRM" option. Agentforce is described as autonomous AI agents and Einstein as predictive and generative AI. Per-tier INR prices are not listed, and the page says to contact sales — [Salesforce India pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/in/pricing/)
- **INR estimate:** Starter about ₹2,000 per user per month — [ITforSME](https://itforsme.in/pricing/salesforce-india)
- **Implementation in India** is a separate cost, for example ₹5–15 lakh for emerging companies — [Watsoo, 2026](https://watsoo.com/blog/salesforce-implementation-cost-india/)

### Freshsales (Freshworks)
- **Pricing (official page, fetched, USD per user per month, annual):** Growth $9, Pro $39, Enterprise $59. There is a 21-day trial. The page metadata mentions a free plan, but no free tier appears on the page — [Freshworks pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/)

- **Pro:** contact scoring, deal insights, custom sales activities, territory management, sales sequences
- **Enterprise:** forecasting insights, field-level permissions, custom modules, sandbox, audit logs
- **Add-ons:** Freddy AI Agent at $49 per 100 sessions (500 sessions included once per account to try); branded documents at $19 per user per month — [Freshworks pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/)
- **INR (third-party, conflicting):**
  - Growth: ₹749–1,299
  - Pro: ₹2,799–3,599
  - Enterprise: ₹4,199–6,099
  - A Freshworks partner lists Growth ₹1,099, Pro ₹3,599, Enterprise ₹6,099 (annual)
  — [ITforSME (partner)](https://itforsme.in/pricing/freshsales-india); [Corefactors (competitor)](https://www.corefactors.ai/comparisons/corefactors-vs-freshsales)
- **Freshdesk–Freshsales sync** needs Pro or higher (Capterra reviewer) — [Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/reviews/155563/freshsales)
- WhatsApp and CPQ are not mentioned on the pricing page.

### Pipedrive

### LeadSquared

- **Super adds:** offline mode, "leads near me", activity and task geo-fencing, advanced workflows, audit logs, the LAPPS developer platform, 30 webhooks (Pro has 3)
- **Paid add-ons (prices not listed):** Converse + Chatbot (WhatsApp/chat), Document Designer, Portals, Sandbox, AI email parser, Journeys, Territory Management, Location Insights, white-labelled app

### Inferences
- **Per-user INR cost of the cheapest "full" tier** (with automation, quotes and calling):
  - Bigin Premier ₹720
  - Zoho CRM Professional ~₹1,400
  - Freshsales Pro ~₹3,200–3,600
  - LeadSquared Pro ₹2,500
  - HubSpot Professional ~₹7,500+, plus a $1,500 onboarding fee
  - Salesforce Pro Suite ~$100

## Q4. Strengths and common complaints

### Takeaway
I found few independent, specific complaints within the search budget. The recurring themes are price escalation (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive add-ons, LeadSquared "a bit expensive") and performance or polish at the low-cost Indian vendors (Kylas slowness).

### Cited Findings
- **HubSpot:** the onboarding fee ($1,500 for Professional) and the jump from Starter to Professional are the main cost complaints. Starter lacks sequences and full workflow automation — [Vedain](https://vedain.com/blog/hubspot-vs-zoho-crm-vs-freshsales-which-is-cheapest-for-a-10-person-sales-team-i); [HubSpot pricing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales)
- **Pipedrive:** add-ons billed per company and monthly-billing markups raise the real cost — [NetHunt](https://nethunt.com/blog/hidden-costs-of-pipedrive-what-you-actually-pay-based-on-real-use-cases/); [Zeeg](https://zeeg.me/post/pipedrive-pricing)
- **LeadSquared:** G2 reviews are mostly positive. One review is titled "Best CRM but bit expensive". Kylas, a competitor, claims LeadSquared keeps basic features for high tiers and that per-user pricing gets expensive as teams grow — [G2 via search](https://g2.com/products/leadsquared-sales-mobile-crm/pricing); [Kylas](https://kylas.io/en/kylas-vs-leadsquared)
- **Kylas:** a G2 reviewer says the app "sometimes becomes slow and stuck". Kylas has told Capterra reviewers that it is working on its call-log feature — [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/210630/Kylas/reviews/6288067/); [G2 compare](https://www.g2.com/compare/kylas-sales-crm-vs-salesforce-salesforce-sales-cloud)
- **Freshsales:** the Freshdesk sync needs the Pro tier; Freddy AI is a paid add-on billed per session — [Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/reviews/155563/freshsales); [Freshworks pricing](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/)
- **Bigin:** TrustRadius review titled "Lots of value for less" (praise for price and value) — [TrustRadius](https://trustradius.com/reviews/bigin-by-zoho-crm-2024-06-01-06-28-26)
- **Salesforce:** implementation in India costs lakhs of rupees on top of licences — [Watsoo](https://watsoo.com/blog/salesforce-implementation-cost-india/)

### Inferences
- **Praise patterns:**
  - Zoho and Bigin: value for money and suite breadth
  - HubSpot: ease of use and the free CRM
  - Pipedrive: visual pipeline simplicity
  - LeadSquared: field sales and high-volume lead handling
  - Kylas: unlimited users at a flat price
  - Bitrix24: the most features for the money
- These are commonly reported patterns, but this research did not verify them with specific reviews beyond those cited above.

### Gaps
- Systematic G2 or Gartner Peer Insights pros and cons for Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive and Bitrix24 were not collected. G2 pages are behind bot protection.

## Q5. Table stakes vs differentiators in 2025–2026

### Takeaway
Agentic AI (agents that act, not just suggest) is the headline 2026 differentiator, and every major vendor now meters it with credits or sessions. Pipelines, web forms, mobile apps, workflows, email sync and basic reporting are table stakes. In India, WhatsApp-native selling, built-in calling and field-sales GPS separate local vendors from global ones.

### Cited Findings
- Gartner, as cited: 40% of enterprise CRM applications will include task-specific AI agents by the end of 2026, up from under 5% in 2025. This is a projection — [Bigin trends article](https://www.bigin.com/articles/seven-crm-trends-that-will-define-2026-and-2027.html); [CIO](https://www.cio.com/article/1255983/customer-relationship-management-crm-trends.html)
- CIO: the promise of AI raises the importance of CRM data quality — [CIO](https://cio.com/article/1255983/customer-relationship-management-crm-trends.html)
- AI is metered:
  - HubSpot bundles Credits per tier (500 / 3,000 / 5,000) and names Customer, Prospecting and Data agents
  - Freshworks sells Freddy AI Agent at $49 per 100 sessions
  - Salesforce sells Agentforce 1 Sales at about $550 per user per month
  — [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales); [Freshworks](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/); [SaaSWorthy](https://saasworthy.com/blog/salesforce-pricing)
- Conversation intelligence is offered by HubSpot (named on the Sales Hub page) and by Bitrix24 CoPilot (CRM call transcription). LeadSquared includes speech-to-text on calls — [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales); [Bitrix24](https://www.bitrix24.in/prices/); [LeadSquared](https://www.leadsquared.com/sales-crm-pricing/)
- A vendor-authored guide claims 78% of Indian SMBs use WhatsApp for business and that it is becoming the default CRM channel. Treat it with caution — [Groweon](https://www.groweon.com/blog/?p=1582)
- WhatsApp by vendor:
  - Kylas: Elevate and above
  - Bitrix24: Standard and above
  - LeadSquared: paid Converse add-on
  - Freshworks: not mentioned on the Freshsales pricing page
  — [Kylas](https://kylas.io/pricing); [Bitrix24](https://www.bitrix24.in/prices/); [LeadSquared](https://www.leadsquared.com/sales-crm-pricing/); [Freshworks](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/)
- Field-sales GPS and geo-fencing: LeadSquared Super (offline mode, leads near me, geo-fencing) and Kylas (₹400 per user add-on; route planning coming) — [LeadSquared](https://www.leadsquared.com/sales-crm-pricing/); [Kylas](https://kylas.io/pricing)

### Inferences
- **Table stakes in 2026 for an India SMB CRM:**
  - Multiple pipelines and Kanban
  - Web-form and lead capture
  - Lead assignment
  - Email sync
  - Mobile app
  - Basic workflows
  - Quotes
  - Dashboards
  - A free or cheap entry tier
  - API and webhooks
- **Becoming expected:**
  - Lead scoring
  - Sequences/cadences
  - Built-in calling with recording
  - WhatsApp messaging
  - An AI assistant (summaries, drafting)
- **Differentiators:**
  - Autonomous AI agents with transparent pricing
  - Call transcription and conversation intelligence
  - Field-sales GPS, geo-fencing and offline mode
  - Flat or unlimited-user pricing
  - Native accounting, HR and invoicing in one suite (Zoho One, Bitrix24, Deskzo One)
  - Customer portals (Zoho Enterprise, LeadSquared add-on, Kylas)
- **Gaps Deskzo should check against itself:**
  - Territory management
  - Forecasting
  - CPQ approval flows
  - Sandbox
  - Conversation intelligence on its calling app
  - Metered AI agents

### Gaps
- I found no independent analyst source (Gartner or Forrester) available without login that ranks which features are table stakes. The classification above is inferred from what vendors bundle at entry tiers.

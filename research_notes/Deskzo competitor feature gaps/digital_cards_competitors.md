# Digital business cards and email signature competitors (researched 2026-10-26)

Scope: Blinq, HiHello, Popl, Mobilo, V1CE, Linq, Wave Connect, Dot, Indian players, and the email-signature tools Exclaimer, CodeTwo, WiseStamp and Newoldstamp. The aim is to compare them with Deskzo's company-managed digital cards: HR/admin grants, templates, QR, wallet passes, lead capture into the CRM, email signatures with banners, and events.

Caveat on sources: many comparison articles are written by a vendor that ranks itself first (Wave, Blinq, WiseStamp). Raklet's 2026 review says almost every "best digital business card" article is written by a vendor reviewing itself — [Raklet](https://www.raklet.com/blog/digital-business-cards/). Competitor claims below are marked as such.

## Card creation, NFC, wallet, QR, signatures and lead capture: product by product

### Takeaway
The leaders now offer much the same set: a free card, Apple/Google Wallet passes, branded QR, a personal email signature, and AI scanning of paper cards and badges, with team admin on top. NFC hardware is a separate purchase. They compete on lead capture and enrichment (often charged per credit), not on the card itself.

### Cited Findings

- Plans: Free $0; Premium $9.99/mo or $7.33/mo billed annually; Business $6.99/user/mo or $4.99/user/mo billed annually, minimum five cards; Enterprise custom — [Blinq pricing](https://blinq.me/pricing)
- Wallet passes and virtual backgrounds on every tier. Branded QR from Premium. Personal email signatures on Free/Premium, admin-controlled on Business+. Google Workspace signature sync is shown on all tiers — [Blinq pricing](https://blinq.me/pricing)

- Wave's competitor-written comparison credits Blinq with 80+ HRIS integrations and SOC 2 Type II, and says the Free plan carries "Powered by Blinq" branding and pop-up solicitations — [Wave, Best Linq alternative (2026-08-12, prices re-verified 2026-10-04)](https://wavecnct.com/blogs/best-linq-alternative)

**Dot (dot.cards)**
- The teams page is a storefront for hardware bundles (for example an item at $20, reduced to $16), with no per-user software pricing or admin feature description — [dot.cards teams](https://dotcards.net/pages/teams)

### Inferences
- The baseline a buyer now expects in 2026: a free card, wallet pass, QR, personal signature and a basic AI scanner. Deskzo's card set (templates, QR, wallet passes, signatures with banners, lead capture) matches that baseline.
- Revenue has moved to lead capture and enrichment: Blinq charges $9.99 per enriched lead and Wave about $2.50 a credit. Deskzo's lead capture goes straight into its own CRM with no per-lead fee, which is a selling point.
- Dot and V1CE are hardware-led and weak on team admin. Linq looks unstable. The real team competitors are Blinq, HiHello, Wave and Popl.

### Gaps
- Dot's software features and team pricing could not be found; its site shows only hardware bundles.
- Popl's actual prices are unpublished, and no third-party quote was found.
- Mobilo's per-user basis is ambiguous on its own page.
- Virtual backgrounds were confirmed only for Blinq and HiHello; the pages for Wave, Mobilo and Popl do not mention them.

## Team admin: templates, brand lock, SSO/SCIM, directory sync, CRM integrations, analytics

### Takeaway
Templates, field locking, admin signatures and native CRM sync (Salesforce and HubSpot everywhere; Zoho only at Wave among those checked) are standard on per-user team plans of about $5/user/mo. SSO, SCIM and full directory sync are usually kept for Enterprise. Blinq and HiHello are the exceptions: Blinq includes Entra provisioning on Business, and HiHello includes Google/Microsoft SSO and directory sync on Business.

### Cited Findings
- Blinq Business: admin platform, user management, custom templates, brand control and field locking, automated card provisioning (Microsoft Entra named), individual and team analytics. Enterprise adds subteam admins, enforced SSO, SCIM and custom reporting. 20+ native CRMs, with Salesforce and HubSpot named. Zoho is not mentioned on the page — [Blinq pricing](https://blinq.me/pricing)
- HiHello Business: admin dashboard, company directory, Google and Microsoft SSO, directory sync with Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra ID, and CRM integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot and Microsoft Dynamics. Enterprise adds SAML SSO, SCIM (Okta, Workday), a real-time webhook for custom CRMs, domain lockdown, custom fonts, verified cards, a branded subdomain and multiple account instances — [HiHello pricing](https://www.hihello.com/pricing)
- Wave Teams: admin platform with roles, bulk profile edits, custom templates, content locking, admin email signatures, team lead capture, auto-tagging by event, shared credit pool, lead dashboard, and CRM sync with HubSpot, Salesforce, Dynamics 365, Zoho, Pipedrive and HighLevel, plus Zapier. Enterprise: SSO, SCIM and directory sync, SOC 2 Type II and GDPR — [Wave pricing](https://wavecnct.com/pages/pricing)
- Mobilo: team management and central billing on Teams; SSO (no SCIM mentioned) and HR Directory Sync on Business; CRM through Zapier — [Mobilo pricing](https://www.mobilocard.com/pricing)
- Popl: admin dashboard for reassigning licenses, self-serve CRM integrations, SOC 2 Type 2, and ROI reporting and attribution — [Popl pricing](https://popl.co/pages/pricing)
- Market price band: team plans run about $3–$8/user/month, with enterprise deals custom-quoted — [Kado Networks via search](https://www.kadonetworks.com/blog/digital-business-card-cost); Wave, Blinq and HiHello cluster at $60/user/year — [Wave](https://wavecnct.com/blogs/best-linq-alternative)

### Inferences
- The gaps to check on Deskzo's side: SCIM or directory provisioning (Entra, Google, Okta), SAML SSO, multiple brands or subteam admins, a branded subdomain or verified-card badge, and team analytics. Deskzo's advantage is that cards are granted from its own HR employee records, so no directory sync is needed, and leads land in its native CRM instead of an integration.
- Zoho CRM sync is rare (only Wave among those checked). For Indian buyers on Zoho, an integration is a sale-relevant gap for competitors; Deskzo replaces the CRM outright.

### Gaps
- No vendor publishes how its field-lock rules work in detail, for example which fields employees may edit.
- Enterprise prices for SSO/SCIM tiers are unpublished everywhere.

## Event and badge scanning, and AI scanning of paper cards

### Takeaway
Events are the main battleground. Popl leads on positioning: unlimited events, an AI badge scanner, qualifying questions and no per-scan fees. Blinq and Wave charge per lead or per credit; HiHello uses monthly scan quotas.

### Cited Findings
- Popl: unlimited events and conferences, AI-native Universal Badge Scanner, event campaigns with qualifying questions, no per-scan or credit fees — [Popl pricing](https://popl.co/pages/pricing)
- Blinq: badge kit APIs and event campaigns on Business; $9.99 per lead captured and enriched — [Blinq pricing](https://blinq.me/pricing)
- Wave: badge scan 4 credits, card or LinkedIn/QR scan 1 credit, at about $2.50 a credit; event auto-tagging on Teams — [Wave pricing](https://wavecnct.com/pages/pricing)
- HiHello: Event Lead Capture with consent; scanner quotas of 5, 20, 50 per user pooled, and unlimited, by tier — [HiHello pricing](https://www.hihello.com/pricing)
- Mobilo: paper business card scanner on all tiers; no badge scanning listed — [Mobilo pricing](https://www.mobilocard.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- If Deskzo's events feature captures leads per event into the CRM, adding these would match the leaders: badge OCR, qualifying questions, and consent capture (HiHello makes consent explicit, which matters under DPDP).

### Gaps
- No independent accuracy tests of badge or card OCR were found.

## Email signature tools: Exclaimer, CodeTwo, WiseStamp, Newoldstamp

### Takeaway
Dedicated signature tools cost about $1–$2/user/mo, far less than card platforms. They compete on server-side enforcement (CodeTwo on every plan), scheduled banner campaigns (Exclaimer, Newoldstamp, WiseStamp Grow) and Microsoft 365 or Google coverage. Most lack SCIM.

### Cited Findings (all from a WiseStamp-written comparison, byline 2026-08-25, normalised at about 50 seats; competitor bias)
- Exclaimer: $0.90–$1.75/user/mo from Starter to Pro; about 8% annual uplift at renewal; 10-user minimum; scheduled banners; engagement analytics on Pro; Microsoft 365, Google and Exchange; directory polled twice a day, no SCIM; no SAML SSO — [WiseStamp guide](https://www.wisestamp.com/guides/best-exclaimer-alternatives/)
- CodeTwo: about $1.21/user/mo, annual billing, 10-seat minimum; server-side signatures on all plans; Microsoft 365 and Exchange only; rule-based conditional templates; granular RBAC — [WiseStamp guide](https://www.wisestamp.com/guides/best-exclaimer-alternatives/)
- WiseStamp: Basic about $1.38/user ($19/mo base plus $1 per employee); Grow about $1.98 adds campaigns and click analytics; server-side only on the Platform tier ($189+/mo); field locking and brand kits; daily directory sync, no SCIM — [WiseStamp guide](https://www.wisestamp.com/guides/best-exclaimer-alternatives/). G2 lists WiseStamp Grow at $49/month — [G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/exclaimer-vs-wisestamp)
- Newoldstamp: $1.80 per signature flat, no volume discount; banners with start/end dates; master templates per department; no RBAC, SAML, SCIM or audit trail — [WiseStamp guide](https://www.wisestamp.com/guides/best-exclaimer-alternatives/)
- Others: Letsignit, with SCIM confirmed for Entra ID and audience-routed banners; Opensense, with CRM-driven banner targeting and HRIS connectors (Workday, BambooHR); Opensense listed from $1/user/mo — [WiseStamp guide](https://www.wisestamp.com/guides/best-exclaimer-alternatives/); [G2](https://www.g2.com/compare/opensense-email-signatures-vs-wisestamp)
- G2 ratings: Newoldstamp about 4.7, WiseStamp about 4.6, Exclaimer about 4.5 with the most reviews — [G2 compare](https://www.g2.com/compare/exclaimer-vs-newoldstamp)

### Inferences
- If Deskzo's signatures are pasted into the mail client, they compete with WiseStamp's lower tiers. Server-side injection, banner scheduling with start/end dates, audience targeting by department or role, and banner click analytics are what set Exclaimer and CodeTwo apart.
- Card platforms (HiHello, Blinq, Wave) now bundle admin signatures, which squeezes stand-alone signature tools for small teams.

### Gaps
- None of these four vendors' own pricing pages were fetched; the figures come from one competitor's article and should be confirmed.

## Indian players

### Takeaway
I found no dominant, well-documented Indian team-card SaaS. The Indian market is fragmented: NFC hardware sellers on IndiaMART, small "digital visiting card" makers with WhatsApp buttons at a one-time fee plus renewal, and a few funded startups such as TapOnn. Kardify and Mydigicard could not be verified. Global players (V1CE, Blinq, Wave) sell into India directly.

### Cited Findings
- TapOnn (Indore): NFC cards, coins and sockets; free app with a planned subscription tier and a planned B2B dashboard for employee cards (reported January 2023); raised INR 1.5 crore seed to serve the corporate sector — [Entrackr 2023](https://entrackr.com/2023/01/taponn-aims-to-replace-paper-business-cards-with-nfc-card-coins); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/taponn-digital-raises-inr-15-crore-in-seed-funding/444569)
- MyTag Digital Visiting Card (Tamil Nadu) has a G2 seller page, with no pricing — [G2](https://www.g2.com/sellers/mytag-digital-visiting-card)
- Tapect is listed on Capterra India as NFC card software, with no price — [Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/software/1078115/Tapect)
- Typical small-vendor pricing: an NFC visiting card with WhatsApp and call buttons at ₹1,999 plus ₹399 a year to renew (an unverified vendor sheet) — [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/777094855/Krishiv-Groups); Visix at ₹100/month for cards plus WhatsApp stores — [SaaS Browser](https://saasbrowser.com/hi/saas/937154/visix)
- NFC PVC card hardware from ₹70 a piece on IndiaMART — [IndiaMART Global Tracex](https://m.indiamart.com/globaltracex/smart-cards.html)
- Other India-marketed cards: FlazeTap — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/flazetap); iEdge Digital — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/upgrade-to-digital-visiting-cards-india)
- Low-cost per-user references: Tap Tag at $3/user/mo for the first 100 users, then $1.25 — [Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/software/1066509/tap-tag); Linkcards at $12/user/year — [Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/software/1015773/linkcards)
- WhatsApp sharing matters in India: a card shares as a link or QR; WhatsApp Business has 481M users — [Uniqode](https://uniqode.com/blog/digital-business-card-basics/how-to-share-a-digital-business-card-on-whatsapp-7-reasons-why)
- V1CE shows INR pricing to Indian visitors (from ₹7,634 one-time) — [V1CE pricing](https://v1ce.co/pages/pricing)

### Inferences
- The Indian market sets an expectation of one-time or very low annual pricing (₹1,999 plus ₹399 a year) and WhatsApp-first sharing. Deskzo can bundle cards inside its HR/CRM seat rather than sell them stand-alone.
- No Indian player I found shows HR-granted cards, CRM lead capture or admin signatures. That is open ground for Deskzo.

### Gaps
- Kardify and Mydigicard: no reliable sources found, so their popularity and features are unverified.
- No Play Store install counts or Indian market-share data were found.
- Zoho Card Scanner and Bizconnect-type scanners were not researched.

## Where each excels, and common complaints

### Takeaway
Blinq is best rated and broadest (4.8 on G2). HiHello has the cleanest design and the most complete Business tier for admins. Popl is the event-lead specialist but costly and opaque. Wave has the widest CRM list, including Zoho. Complaints focus on subscription cost, per-lead fees, free-tier branding and nags, and gaps in individual workflows.

### Cited Findings
- Popl: cost is the most common complaint; users call it steep for occasional networkers, and some want a one-time payment; G2 about 4.6 — [G2 Popl pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/popl-lead-capture-digital-business-cards/pricing); [G2 Blinq vs Popl](https://www.g2.com/compare/blinq-me-vs-popl)
- Blinq: G2 about 4.8. Capterra complaints: only one link can be listed, so B2B and B2C links can't be toggled; no connection to email sequence funnels; the camera-based share method is hard for some recipients — [Capterra Blinq reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/236861/Blinq/reviews/); [G2 Blinq vs HiHello](https://www.g2.com/compare/blinq-me-vs-hihello)
- HiHello: G2 about 4.6; praised for clean design and a free, fast service; some feel it may not fit their workflow — [G2 HiHello vs Popl](https://www.g2.com/compare/hihello-vs-popl)
- Blinq free tier carries branding and pop-up solicitations (competitor claim) — [Wave](https://wavecnct.com/blogs/best-linq-alternative)
- Linq: continuity risk; cards may stop being supported (competitor claim) — [Wave](https://wavecnct.com/blogs/best-linq-alternative)
- Exclaimer: about 8% renewal uplift (competitor claim) — [WiseStamp guide](https://www.wisestamp.com/guides/best-exclaimer-alternatives/)

### Inferences
- What Deskzo can sell against these: cards included in the seat with no per-lead fee, cards issued and withdrawn with the employee record (withdrawn on exit, unlike reassigning Popl licenses), and leads that arrive as CRM leads with follow-up tasks. The last point answers Blinq's "no email sequence" complaint.

### Gaps
- G2's comparison summaries are AI-generated; individual verbatim reviews were not collected for every product.

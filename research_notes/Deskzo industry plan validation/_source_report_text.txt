Executive decision
CONDITIONAL GO: Build a horizontal foundation, then 3–4 focused vertical pilots. NO-GO for
launching 30+ fully fledged vertical applications at once. Prioritize (a) software license reseller /
distribution workflows, (b) FMCG distributor–retailer ordering, (c) salons or garages as a service
vertical, and (d) small retail catalog onboarding. Delay regulated healthcare, complex finance and
large-scale consumer marketplace expansion until operational and legal proof exists.

Evidence discipline
Evidence grades: A = official regulator/vendor primary documentation; B = credible industry report
or product comparison; C = reasoned product hypothesis; U = not tested. Each vertical below has
competitive examples as discovery leads, not proof of every feature, market share, or price. Public
sources were accessed in October 2026; vendor terms may change.
Market context and structural constraints
 Government MSME dashboard reported approximately 99.9 million cumulative Udyam + UAP
registrations on 29 October 2026; this may include changes over time and is not an addressable
paying-market estimate. Source [S1].
 The MSME 2024–25 annual report indicates many firms have low annual turnover. Price
sensitivity and onboarding time are therefore first-order product risks. Source [S2].
 India e-invoicing is mandated for covered businesses at ₹5 crore AATO threshold according to
official IRP guidance; applicability and later changes must be checked by a tax professional.
Source [S8].
 GS1 DataKart is not automatically a perpetual free-to-redistribute national product database. Its
published agreement conditions API access and brand-owner data sharing. Source [S3].
 Microsoft Partner Center API functionality depends on CSP role and eligibility; do not promise
provisioning for every reseller or vendor. Source [S6].
 Consumer marketplace discovery and B2B ordering are distinct products. Deskzo Local should
not leak dealer price books or inventory without permission.

Detailed industry-by-industry validation dossiers
For each industry: the problem and product wedge are product hypotheses; the named competitors
are starting points for feature-level demos. Each dossier specifies what would falsify the business
case. A passing result requires real buyers and operating evidence.
1. Kirana / supermarket
Segment: Retail | Hypothesis score: 4.2/5 | Build recommendation: Pilot
Core job-to-be-done: Fast billing, expiry, udhaar, stockouts.
Proposed minimum viable wedge: Barcode onboarding + offline-capable POS + distributor reorder.
Catalog / data acquisition: GS1 FMCG GTIN, loose-weight items, local SKUs. Do not claim
comprehensive coverage before license, regional sample and merchant-level matching checks.
Competitive discovery targets: Vyapar; myBillBook; Marg; GoFrugal. Ask merchants for current
stack and replacement blockers; do not assume these products have identical capabilities.
Key execution risk: High churn and thin ARPU; offline printer reliability.
Double-validation experiment: Interview 8–12 operators in this segment; collect 3 anonymized real
documents or workflow traces; test a 15-minute onboarding demo; ask for paid pilot or refundable
deposit; measure first-value completion, error rate and willingness to switch. Reject if fewer than
3/10 interviews identify the wedge as a top-three operational pain or fewer than 2/10 accept a paid
pilot after hands-on demonstration.

Launch controls, safety and compliance gates
 Invitation-only by default; only Platform Super Admin opens products, signup, public storefront
and Local discovery by country/state/city/radius. Keep existing tenant access separate from new
activations.
 No Deskzo commission on merchant orders or customer transactions. Payment gateway fees are
external; partners may earn disclosed SaaS resale margins.
 Merchant is seller/service provider and handles checkout, refunds, invoices and fulfillment;
avoid implying Deskzo is merchant of record.
 Use idempotency keys and payload hashes for invoices, payment events, stock reservations,
provisioning, invites and order creation; transactional outbox/inbox for async workflows.
 Tenant-scoped indexes, PostgreSQL/PostGIS spatial indexes, paginated search, explicit
performance budgets and realistic load testing.
 TDD: real PostgreSQL integration tests, API contracts, Playwright E2E, tenant isolation, RBAC,
security, concurrency, privacy and geo policy tests.
 Medical, pharmacy, lab, financial filing and regulated product verticals need domain experts,
legal review and independently tested safety/compliance flows.
 GST e-invoicing/filing must be verified against official APIs, current rules and authorized
GSP/ASP arrangements; do not claim automatic filings without working integration.

# Competitors and India pricing: service verticals and all-in-one suites (as of October 2026)

All pages accessed 2026-10-30 unless noted. Most prices come from third-party aggregators or resellers, not vendor pages. Where a vendor page was fetched directly, the note says so. Prices exclude 18% GST unless stated.

## Salon / spa: Fresha, Zenoti, Easy Salon, Salonist, Dingg. What do Indian salons pay?

### Takeaway
The Indian salon market has a free option (Fresha's core subscription) and flat per-salon prices of roughly ₹2,000–4,000 a month (Dingg, which makes the claim about itself). Fresha earns from a new-client marketplace fee, payment processing and per-location add-ons. Zenoti is an enterprise product with quote-only pricing for chains (Lakmé Salon, 300+ outlets). A per-person price of ₹1,499 means a 5-chair salon pays about ₹7,500 a month, roughly 2–4x the flat Indian alternatives, and Fresha's core is free.

### Cited Findings
- **Fresha (fetched its own page, which displayed INR):** the Independent plan (1 team member) and the Team plan (unlimited team members) are both listed with a "Free subscription". The page also says "As you expand we charge for each bookable team member", but the only per-member charges it shows are add-ons. Those add-ons, in ₹ per month: Team Connect ₹279 per team member, Insights ₹250 per bookable team member, AI Concierge ₹1,900 per location, Client Loyalty ₹5,000 per location, Google Rating Boost ₹375.95 per location, Xero ₹920 per location, Data Connector ₹14,500 per location, Smart Website ₹949.95. Messaging: 20 free automated texts/WhatsApp messages a month, then ₹13.50 per SMS or ₹1.60–5.50 per WhatsApp message; marketing SMS ₹54 each; marketing email ₹0.47 after 50 free. The page lists the new-client marketplace fee as "Free" with no amount. — [Fresha pricing update](https://www.fresha.com/pricing-update)
- Fresha: the same page, as described in the search results, announces a move to a paid monthly subscription for professionals, and India is in the list of affected countries. This conflicts with the "Free subscription" text that showed when the page was fetched. — [Fresha pricing update](https://www.fresha.com/pricing-update)
- Fresha (USD, third-party): Team plan about $14.95 per bookable team member per month, Independent about $19.95 per month, Enterprise (20+ members) custom. — [Pabau, Jul 2026](https://pabau.com/blog/fresha-pricing/). CostBench gives conflicting figures: $8.95–12.95 (Mar 2026) against $14.95–19.95 (Aug 2026), plus a February 2026 price cut. — [CostBench](https://www.costbench.com/software/salon-spa/fresha/); [CostBench changelog](https://costbench.com/changelog/fresha-price-decrease-2026-02/)
- Fresha marketplace commission: 20% on a new client's first booking through the marketplace, minimum $6 per client (USD markets). Card processing 2.19% + $0.20 (USD figure). — [SchedulingKit](https://schedulingkit.com/pricing-guides/fresha-pricing); [Pabau](https://pabau.com/blog/fresha-pricing/)
- **Dingg** (Indian competitor) says it charges a flat ₹2,000–4,000 a month with no per-booking commission, and contrasts this with Fresha (vendor claim). — [Dingg vs Fresha for Indian salons 2026](https://dingg.app/blogs/dingg-vs-fresha-for-indian-salons-honest-comparison-2026)
- **Zenoti:** pricing is not published; custom quote by business size. One review lists a starting price of $300 a month (USD, not India-specific). — [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/medical/zenoti-profile/). India reference customer: Lakmé Salon, a chain of 300 salons. — [Zenoti press](https://www.zenoti.com/es/press/lakme-salon-indias-leading-chain-of-300-salons-chooses-zenoti-to-optimize-management-of-its-growing-network)
- **Salonist:** pricing is "completely independent of your team size", with unlimited staff at no extra cost (vendor page). — [Salonist vs Zenoti](https://salonist.io/compare/salonist-vs-zenoti). TechJockey lists Salonist at ₹22,000 without saying for what period. — [TechJockey](https://www.techjockey.com/category/spa-salon-management-software/small-business)
- **Easy Salon:** listed on Capterra India; no price confirmed. A TechJockey listing for "Easy Salon Software" (13DesignStreet, possibly a different product) shows ₹5,000 with no period. — [Capterra India](https://www.capterra.in/software/183699/easy-salon-software); [TechJockey](https://www.techjockey.com/category/spa-salon-management-software?page=2)

### Inferences
- The two pricing patterns among Indian salon tools are per salon/location (Dingg, Salonist) and free core plus monetised extras (Fresha). Per-seat pricing near ₹1,500 a head has no direct match among salon-specific tools.
- Fresha's add-ons are priced per location or per team member in small ₹ amounts (₹250–279), which suggests Indian per-member price points sit well below ₹1,499.

### Gaps
- Whether Fresha's paid subscription is live in India, and its ₹ amount, is unclear: the fetched page showed "Free" while search snippets describe a paid subscription that includes India.
- Fresha's ₹ minimum new-client fee and its India card-processing rate were not found.
- No official ₹ prices were found for Zenoti, Salonist or Easy Salon. GlossGenius was not researched; it is US-only and not relevant to India.

## Garage / auto service: GaragePlug, Autorox, Indian apps, Tekmetric (US)

### Takeaway
Indian garage software is priced per workshop. GaragePlug's pricing is quote-only. Third-party figures: about $99–999 a month by tier, and ₹2,400–3,000 a month for starter plans across the Indian category. Tekmetric, the US reference, is also per shop at $179–439 a month.

### Cited Findings
- GaragePlug: quotation-based pricing, starting price not disclosed. — [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/garageplug/pricing)
- GaragePlug tiers (unverified third-party figures): GEAR-1 $99/mo, GEAR-2 $199/mo, Enterprise $999/mo. — [SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/product/garageplug/pricing). Sources disagree on whether it has a free plan. — [SpotSaaS](https://spotsaas.com/product/garageplug)
- GaragePlug was founded in 2017, incubated at IIM Bangalore, and targets multi-outlet chains in India, the Gulf and SEA. — [Motor India](https://www.motorindiaonline.in/garageplugs-tech-solution-impresses-customers-worldwide/)
- Indian category estimate: starter plans about ₹2,400–3,000 a month per workshop; Professional/Enterprise ₹5,000–15,000 a month per workshop, depending on users and modules. — [CodingClave, Best auto repair shop software India 2026](https://codingclave.com/guides/best-auto-repair-shop-software-india-2026)
- Tekmetric (US): per shop, not per user. Start $199/mo, Grow $349/mo, Scale $439/mo; Start is $179/mo billed annually. — [CostBench Tekmetric](https://costbench.com/software/automotive-shop-software/tekmetric/)

### Inferences
- Garages buy per workshop. ₹1,499 per person × 4–6 staff (₹6,000–9,000 a month) lands in the Professional range of the category, above starter plans.

### Gaps
- No Autorox pricing was found; it was not searched separately because of the tool-call budget. Other "Garage Manager"-type Play Store apps were not checked.

## Packers & movers: ePackBook and moving CRMs

### Takeaway
ePackBook, the main India-specific product, costs about ₹2,799 a year, flat, and has a free version. That is very cheap, so it anchors this vertical's willingness to pay far below ₹1,499 per person per month.

### Cited Findings
- ePackBook Plus: ₹2,799 flat rate per year; free version and free trial available. — [Capterra (ePackBook)](https://www.capterra.com/p/10016793/ePackBook/); [Software Advice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/product/517805-ePackBook)
- ePackBook features: quotation and invoice sharing, packing material, survey and packing lists, vehicle and branch management, document templates. — [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/industries-software/a/epackbook/)

### Inferences
- ₹2,799 a year is about ₹233 a month for the whole business. The packers & movers vertical is likely the most price-resistant of the pilot candidates under per-person pricing.

### Gaps
- No other Indian moving CRMs (or SmartMoving/MoversSuite-type US references) were priced. What ePackBook's free tier includes is unknown.

## Home services / field service: Zoho FSM, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro

### Takeaway
Zoho FSM's official model is appointment-based, not per technician: a free edition, and Standard from a $30 base. Partners quote ₹1,000–2,100 per user a month. The US references range from Jobber/Housecall Pro (about $29–329 a month, bundling a few users) to ServiceTitan ($250–498 per user a month, quote-driven).

### Cited Findings
- Zoho FSM (official help page): appointment-based pricing, independent of the number of technicians; free edition; Standard with a $30 fixed base plus tiers by appointments per month. — [Zoho FSM Editions and Pricing](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/fsm/getting-started/articles/fsm-pricing)
- Zoho FSM in ₹ (partners): Standard ₹1,000 and Professional ₹1,800 per user per month, excluding GST. — [PrecisionTech](https://precisiontech.in/software/zoho/zoho-fsm/). About ₹1,400 a month per field technician and ₹2,100 per dispatcher/back-office user. — [Aaxonix](https://aaxonix.com/resources/zoho-fsm-vs-zoho-creator-field-service-india/)
- ServiceTitan: Starter $250, Essentials $398, The Works $498 per user per month (estimates, Jun 2026), plus $5k–50k implementation. — [CostBench ServiceTitan](https://costbench.com/software/field-service-management/servicetitan/); [OneCrew](https://www.getonecrew.com/post/servicetitan-pricing)
- Housecall Pro: Basic $59 (annual)/$79 (monthly) for 1 user; Essentials $149/$189 for up to 5 users; MAX about $329 for 8 users, about $35 per extra user. — [Activepieces](https://www.activepieces.com/blog/housecall-pro-pricing-2026-plans-by-team-size.md); [CostBench](https://www.costbench.com/compare/housecall-pro-vs-jobber/)
- Jobber: Core about $29–49 a month for 1 user plus $29 per extra user; Connect about $129–149; Grow about $249–399. Sources conflict. — [CostBench Jobber vs Housecall Pro](https://www.costbench.com/compare/housecall-pro-vs-jobber/); [FieldCamp](https://fieldcamp.ai/reviews/jobber)

### Inferences
- In India, per-technician pricing near ₹1,000–1,400 (Zoho FSM via partners) undercuts ₹1,499. Many technicians in home services only need a mobile job view, so a cheaper field-worker seat may matter more than the headline price.

### Gaps
- No Indian home-services FSM apps (e.g., urban-services-style local tools) were priced. No official Zoho FSM ₹ price page was found.

## Clinics: Practo Ray; why clinics are regulated

### Takeaway
Practo Ray is priced per clinic at about ₹999–1,999 a month (cheaper on multi-year terms), but the figures are old (SaaSworthy says last updated 2020). Clinics bring health-data obligations: DPDP enforcement begins 13 Nov 2026, and ABDM (ABHA/HFR/HPR) is becoming a practical requirement in some settings. Both raise the cost of entering this vertical.

### Cited Findings
- Practo Ray: CM Atom ₹999/mo on a 1-year plan; Clinic Management ₹1,999/mo list, ₹1,499 on 1 year, ₹999 on 4 years. Add-ons: Ray Connect 1000 ₹1,499/mo; Practo Tab hardware ₹17,000 one-time. — [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/ray-by-practo)
- Practo Ray pricing on SaaSworthy was last updated 06/08/2020, so it may be stale. Sources conflict on whether a free trial exists. — [SaaSworthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/ray-by-practo); [SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/product/ray-by-practo/pricing)
- DPDP Rules were notified 13 Nov 2025; enforcement and penalties start 13 Nov 2026; full compliance is due 13 May 2027 (secondary source). — [DEV / ecorpit ABDM HIP 2026](https://dev.to/mr_manushukla/abdm-hip-integration-in-2026-fhir-r4-abha-linking-and-nabh-6th-edition-readiness-50i4)
- ABDM is not legally mandatory for all providers but is "rapidly becoming a regulatory expectation". NMC (Jul 2026) requires ABDM-compliant HMIS at medical colleges, and states link AB-PMJAY empanelment to ABDM certification. — [OneCity ABDM guide](https://onecity.co.in/blog/abdm-compliant-software-development/); [Omnicuris](https://www.omnicuris.com/medshots/editorial_picks/nmc-hmis-compliance-abdm-integration-steps_17-Jul-26_22-01)
- Software for ABDM needs ABHA creation and linking, consent-based sharing and FHIR records; clinics register in HFR and doctors in HPR (vendor claim). The Digital Health Incentive Scheme subsidises clinics that adopt ABDM-compliant software. — [Qualysec ABDM checklist](https://qualysec.com/abdm-integration-checklist/); [DoctorsApp](https://doctorsapp.in/about-abdm)

### Inferences
- Practo Ray (per clinic, about ₹1,000–2,000 a month) is cheaper than one Deskzo seat at ₹1,499 once a clinic has more than one staff member. Clinics also need ABDM certification work and health-data safeguards, so they are a poor first pilot.

### Gaps
- No current official Practo Ray ₹ page was found. No primary NHA/MoHFW or DPDP Rules text was fetched; the regulatory points come from secondary or vendor sources.

## All-in-one suites in India: where does ₹1,499 per person per month sit?

### Takeaway
₹1,499 is almost exactly Zoho One's widely cited All-Employee price (₹1,500, or ₹1,800 per press reports) but less than half its Flexible User price (about ₹3,500–4,000). It is above Odoo Custom India (about ₹1,080 yearly / ₹1,690 monthly, single source) and below Salesforce Starter (about ₹2,000–2,100), Business Central Essentials (about ₹5,500–6,500) and LeadSquared ($50–60). Since Deskzo charges per actual person, its true comparison is Zoho One Flexible, against which it is cheap.

## Evidence on per-user vs per-business pricing among Indian SMBs

### Takeaway
No survey or study was found showing that small Indian shops reject per-user pricing. The indirect evidence points the same way, though: Indian vertical tools are priced per business or location (Dingg, Salonist, ePackBook, Practo Ray, garage tools, Tekmetric). Commentators say traditional Indian SMBs are highly price-sensitive, compare against the cheapest option, and prefer flat yearly pricing.

### Cited Findings
- Traditional Indian SMBs are far more price-sensitive than VC-funded startups, often buy through local agencies, and prefer yearly flat pricing; seat- and usage-based pricing is less common. — [EmbedWorkflow, Selling SaaS in India](https://embedworkflow.com/blog/selling-saas-in-india/)
- An Indian SaaS founder says small businesses first compare a product to the cheapest available solution. — [Xconomy, India vs Silicon Valley](https://legacy.xconomy.com/?p=202370)
- Vertical vendors market flat or unlimited-staff pricing as a selling point: Dingg's flat fee with no commission, and Salonist's "independent of your team size" (vendor claims). — [Dingg](https://dingg.app/blogs/dingg-vs-fresha-for-indian-salons-honest-comparison-2026); [Salonist](https://salonist.io/compare/salonist-vs-zenoti)

### Inferences
- For salon, garage and movers pilots, a per-business floor or a cheap "staff/field" seat tier would probably be needed for ₹1,499 per person to compete. Per-person pricing fits better with office-heavy, CRM- or accounts-led SMBs, where the alternative is Zoho One.

### Gaps
- No quantitative Indian survey data (e.g., from Economic Times, YourStory, Inc42 or Entrackr) on per-seat resistance was found within the search budget.

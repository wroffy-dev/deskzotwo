# Deskzo matches suites on breadth, trails on proof

Deskzo's real rival is **Zoho One**. It is the only competitor that, like Deskzo One, sells CRM, GST accounting, HR and payroll, helpdesk, inventory and campaigns as one India-hosted suite, and at almost the same price: about **₹1,250–1,800 per employee a month against Deskzo One's ₹1,499**. Every other famous name competes with one Deskzo module at a time. In CRM that means Bigin, Zoho CRM, Freshsales, LeadSquared, Kylas and HubSpot. In accounting it is TallyPrime, Zoho Books and BUSY. In payroll it is greytHR, Keka and Zoho Payroll. In digital cards it is Blinq, HiHello and Wave. For WhatsApp and support it is WATI, Interakt, Gallabox, Freshdesk and Zendesk. On paper Deskzo's feature list already reaches mid-tier or top-tier at most of them. It is ahead where its single database pays off: CRM incentives paid on the payslip, cards issued from the HR record, order to vendor PO to invoice to OEM rebate, and an exit handover that moves leads and tickets. It is also ahead on India tax *computation* (direct NIC e-invoice, a vendor-TDS Form 140 file, salary TDS under the Income-tax Act 2025, professional tax for about 17 states), and on bundled automation and India-region AI. It falls behind where an Indian SMB's money and penalties sit. It cannot file GST returns. It has no GSTR-2B or IMS matching. It has no PF ECR, ESI file, Form 138 or Form 130, no telephony or call recording, and no released mobile app. Worst, nearly every outside integration has run only against fakes: Razorpay, Stripe, Cashfree, NIC e-invoice and e-way, Bedrock, the calendars, signature sync and WhatsApp. So the most important gap is not a feature. It is proof that what is built works against the real services. The codebase is about seven weeks old and the sources show no production customers, so every "ahead" claim below means "built", not "proven in the field".

## Zoho One is the rival that matters; everyone else flanks a single module

Deskzo is packaged the way Zoho is: **14 products (Deskzo One plus 13 standalone apps) and 5 add-ons, built on about 60 modules**. Every product shares one workspace, Companies & Contacts, tasks, notes, chat and Automate ([products.ts](../src/lib/products.ts)). That shape decides who the competitors are. The table maps each Deskzo product to the rivals an Indian buyer will actually shortlist.

|---|---|---|

| CRM / Pipeline | Zoho Bigin, Zoho CRM, Freshsales, LeadSquared, Kylas, Bitrix24; HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive globally | Automation tier, calling, WhatsApp, field sales, flat pricing |

Zoho is the default Indian SMB stack, and Zoho CRM Standard at ₹800 is the usual paid entry point ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/zoho-explained-products-features-and-pricing-for-indian-businesses-495208-2025-09-23)). Zoho's founder says Indian customer data is hosted in India, in Mumbai, Delhi and Chennai, and not on AWS, Azure or GCP ([storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/zoho-founder-clarifies-data-hosting-and-product-development-amid-misconceptions-says-made-in-india-made-for-the-world-81848.htm)). That removes a "made in India, data in India" argument Deskzo might otherwise use against a foreign rival. Odoo is the low-licence alternative, but in India the implementation dwarfs the licence. Partners put year-one cost at **2–4x the licence** and SMB rollouts at **Rs 5–25 lakh** ([braincuber](https://www.braincuber.com/blog/odoo-implementation-cost-india-2026-what-you-actually-pay)). Bitrix24 is the flat-price wildcard. It costs ₹4,790 a month on annual billing for 50 users ([Bitrix24 India](https://www.bitrix24.in/prices/)), but Indian reviewers complain about lag, a cluttered interface and features "not fully localized for India" ([capterra.in](https://www.capterra.in/reviews/113540/bitrix24)). QuickBooks is no longer a competitor: it **left India in 2023** ([The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/quickbooks-will-exit-the-indian-market-in-2023)).

There are two specialists to watch outside the suites. **LeadSquared** is the benchmark for field sales and high-volume calling. It bundles mobile cloud calling with speech-to-text, check-in and check-out tracking, and, on Super, geo-fencing and offline mode ([LeadSquared pricing](https://www.leadsquared.com/sales-crm-pricing/)). **Kylas** sells a flat monthly price with **unlimited users on every plan** ([Kylas pricing](https://kylas.io/pricing)). Deskzo's per-person model cannot match that structure at larger team sizes.

## At ₹1,499 a person, Deskzo prices like Zoho One and loses to flat-rate rivals

Deskzo charges **per person per month before 18% GST**. A year costs ten months, there is **no free plan**, and the trial covers 25 people ([price-book.ts](../src/lib/platform/price-book.ts); `memory/pricing-india-decisions.md`). Most Indian rivals in accounting and HR charge per organisation with users included, and several offer permanent free tiers. Whether Deskzo looks cheap or dear depends on the team size and the module.

The arithmetic shows three patterns. First, **Deskzo One sits squarely inside Zoho One's All-Employee band**. A 10-person company pays ₹14,990 a month for Deskzo One against ₹12,500–18,000 for Zoho One. On price alone, a buyer has no reason to switch either way. Second, **Deskzo's small apps beat everyone on price**. Pipeline at ₹299 undercuts Bigin Express at ₹400. Cards at ₹99 and Signatures at ₹59 cost a fraction of Blinq's $4.99 and match the cheapest signature tools. Deskzo also charges no per-lead fee, where Blinq charges **$9.99 per lead captured and enriched** and Wave about **$2.50 a credit** ([Blinq](https://blinq.me/pricing); [Wave](https://wavecnct.com/pages/pricing)). Automation is generous too. Automate Plus gives 50,000 steps a month for ₹999 a workspace ([limits.ts](../src/lib/automate/limits.ts)), against Zapier's roughly $29.99 for 750 tasks ([codewords](https://codewords.ai/blog/zapier-vs-make-pricing)).

Third, **per-person pricing loses on Books and against flat-rate CRMs**. Deskzo Books costs ₹1,047 for 3 users against Zoho Books Standard at ₹749, and ₹3,490 for 10 against Zoho Premium at ₹2,999. Zoho also has a free plan for businesses under ₹25 lakh revenue ([Zoho Books](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/pricing/)). In CRM, Deskzo's ₹799 seat costs more than Kylas Elevate's flat ₹12,999 from about **17 users** upward, and more than Bitrix24 Standard's ₹4,790 for 50 users from about **6 users**. Deskzo's counter-arguments are real but need explaining. Zoho Books caps invoices per plan (5,000 a year on Standard) and charges **₹600 a month for each extra GSTIN** ([Zoho Books](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/pricing/)). Deskzo includes several GST registrations per workspace (commit b5c740c2). And WhatsApp tools cap users at 3–10 and charge ₹1,200–2,199 for each extra one ([WATI](https://www.wati.io/pricing/); [Gallabox](https://gallabox.com/pricing)).

HR pricing needs an honest reading. At ₹99 a person, a 50-person company pays ₹4,950 a month. That is about greytHR Growth's ₹4,495 and double greytHR Essential's ₹2,495 ([greytHR](https://www.greythr.com/pricing/)). Zoho Payroll is free for up to 10 employees and already lists Form 138 and Form 130 on its Standard plan ([Zoho Payroll](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/pricing/)). Deskzo is therefore priced like a mid-tier HRMS while missing the statutory filing outputs that entry-tier rivals advertise. There is also an internal leak. Users aren't tied to a product, so a workspace that buys People for 100 people and CRM for 10 can let all 110 use CRM (`memory/pricing-india-decisions.md`).

AI pricing is where the market moved in 2026. Deskzo sells credits at ₹1 each, with 100 a person a month included on One (`memory/ai-gateway-and-credits-decisions.md`). Rivals meter AI per outcome: Intercom Fin at $0.99 a resolution, Zendesk at $1.30–2.00 an automated resolution, Freshworks Freddy at $49 per 100 sessions ([helpdesk.com](https://www.helpdesk.com/blog/intercom-pricing/); [Zendesk](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/); [Freshdesk](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/)). greytHR went the other way. Its **NAVOS agentic assistant is included in all paid plans with no per-seat AI fee** ([greytHR](https://www.greythr.com/greythr-pressrelease/greytHR-launches-navos/)), which sets the SMB expectation in HR.

## Competitors excel at filing, phones and integrations that have met real services

### Accounting: Deskzo computes the return but cannot file it

Deskzo Books has a full ledger with banking, fixed assets, period lock and FX. It produces GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B summaries per registration ([tax-reports.ts](../src/actions/tax-reports.ts)). It does e-invoices and e-way bills straight to NIC with the required encryption, live GSTIN checks, vendor TDS with challans, and a quarterly **Form 140** file (commits f74f8572, f484d84e, db7c23f0). The filing side is missing. A code search found **no GSTN JSON export, no GSTR-2A/2B reconciliation and no GSTR-9**, and no IMS accept or reject actions surfaced either. Rivals already do this. Zoho is a registered GST Suvidha Provider and files from inside Books, with GSTR-2B reconciliation ([Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/zoho-books-guide-indian-businesses-2026)). BUSY 21 uploads GSTR-1 directly and reconciles 2B ([BUSY](https://busy.in/busy-21-software/)). Odoo files through the GSP BVM IT Consulting ([Odoo docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/20.0/applications/finance/fiscal_localizations/india.html)). TallyPrime 7.0 shipped IMS ITC reduction and Connected Banking 2.0 ([Tally release notes](https://help.tallysolutions.com/release-notes-tallyprime-7-0/)). Since the July 2025 period, GSTR-3B outward liability is **hard-locked**, with corrections going through GSTR-1A ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/hard-locking-in-gstr-3b)). Return preparation that stops at a summary screen therefore leaves the SMB's CA doing the real work in another tool.

The e-invoice path carries extra risk. Deskzo offers only Mock, NIC sandbox and NIC production, with **no GSP option**. Before 25 Oct 2026 the NIC path "could never have worked", it is still untried on the real sandbox, and it signs in again for every action, which may hit NIC's limits (`memory/nic-einvoice-encryption.md`; [options.ts](../src/lib/einvoice/options.ts)). Tally's other advantages are structural: offline operation, a deeper inventory engine, and CA familiarity ([AI Accountant](https://www.aiaccountant.com/blog/zoho-books-vs-tally)). Deskzo's answer, a one-way Tally export, is **a plan with nine open decisions and nothing built** (`memory/tally-export-plan.md`). Busy and Tally imports are parked. The mid-year Zoho import uses **guessed column names** until a real Zoho export is tried (`memory/mid-year-switch-plan.md`). One baseline needs checking. Since 1 Apr 2023, the Companies (Accounts) Rules require company books to keep an audit trail with an edit log that cannot be disabled ([SetIndiaBiz](https://www.setindiabiz.com/blog/audit-trail-compliance-accounting-software-mca-guidelines)). Tally sells an Edit Log variant and BUSY advertises a compulsory audit trail. The Deskzo inventory does not say whether Books meets the rule, and it should be confirmed before Deskzo is sold to any company customer.

### HRMS: attendance is strong, the statutory files and talent modules are not there

Deskzo People covers employee records, leave with accrual and encashment, short leave by the hour, and GPS clock-in restricted to the office. It accepts biometric push from eSSL/ZKTeco terminals with no connector PC. Payroll covers structures, revisions, pro-rating, full and final settlement, PF/ESI/PT, salary TDS under s.392, letters, visitors, expense claims and an exit handover ([hrms-roadmap.md](../docs/hrms-roadmap.md)). On those basics it reaches the core tier of Zimyo, HROne and factoHR. But the roadmap marks the **PF ECR file, ESI contribution file, LWF, bank salary-transfer files, loans and advances, comp-off and overtime rules, performance reviews and OKRs, a careers page, multi-level approvals, a compliance calendar, Form 130 and Form 138 as not built**. The TDS card itself says Form 138 "isn't made here yet" ([tds-statement-card.tsx](../src/components/accounting/tds-statement-card.tsx)). Employees also have no self-declaration screen; payroll enters declarations for them.

Competitors fill exactly these holes. Zoho Payroll's free tier already handles EPF, ESI, state PT and LWF and pays salaries through HSBC. Its Standard plan adds Form 138 and Form 130 with a digital signature ([Zoho Payroll](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/pricing/)). RazorpayX Payroll calculates, pays and files PF, ESI, PT and TDS, and pays salaries directly ([RazorpayX](https://razorpay.com/payroll/pricing/)). Keka's Growth tier sells OKRs, reviews, 1:1s, calibration and PIPs, and Keka Hiring adds a career portal and AI CV matching ([Keka](https://www.keka.com/pricing)). Zimyo's top tier adds 9-box and bell-curve normalisation ([Zimyo](https://www.zimyo.com/pricing)). factoHR includes AI face recognition in its first paid tier ([factoHR](https://www.factohr.com/pricing/)). The 2025–26 rule changes raise the stakes. The Labour Codes took effect on 21 Nov 2025 with the 50% wage rule ([greytHR Help](https://www.greythr.com/greythr-help/admin/answers/Aa_TXZJ0TgaxIptDNayk-g/)). The Income-tax Act 2025 renamed Form 16 to 130 and 24Q to 138 from 1 Apr 2026 ([greytHR guide](https://www.greythr.com/blog/income-tax-act-2025-payroll-compliance-guide-2026/)). Buyers now see these form names on competitor pricing pages.

### CRM: the feature list is wide, but calling and mobile are not in customers' hands

Deskzo CRM offers several pipelines, including connected and non-sales "process" pipelines. It has stage rules, lead assignment, booking pages, industry templates and imports from Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce and Pipedrive. Its forecasting weights each stage by its actual win rate. It also has field visits with GPS, targets, incentives, wins on TV screens, and a public API (`memory/bigin-like-product-decisions.md`; [modules.ts](../src/lib/modules.ts)). The gap is the phone. **Calls are logged, not placed**: there is no telephony provider and no recording ([ROADMAP.md](../docs/ROADMAP.md)). LeadSquared bundles mobile cloud calling with speech-to-text ([LeadSquared](https://www.leadsquared.com/sales-crm-pricing/)). Bitrix24 includes telephony from its Basic plan ([Bitrix24](https://www.bitrix24.in/prices/)). HubSpot includes 500–12,000 calling minutes by tier ([HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales)). Kylas includes calling, SMS and WhatsApp from Elevate, and its Android and iOS app is on every plan ([Kylas](https://kylas.io/pricing)). Deskzo's Kylas-style Android app syncs call logs, shows caller ID and an after-call card, and has native sales and field screens. But it is **hidden until a download URL is set and is not on Google Play**. Push notifications wait on a Firebase project, and the iPhone web app has **never been tried on a real iPhone** (`memory/deskzo-phone-app.md`; `memory/iphone-pwa-decisions.md`). For field-sales and attendance buyers, the app is the product.

Two smaller CRM gaps matter. In packaging, proposals and proformas belong to Books, so **a CRM-only buyer cannot send a quote** (`memory/no-external-accounting-sync.md`). Zoho CRM puts quotes and CPQ in Professional ([Cloudfy](https://cloudfysystems.com/blog/zoho-crm-pricing-india-2026)). Second, the inventory does not mention lead scoring, sales sequences or territory management, which Zoho, Freshsales Pro and HubSpot Professional all sell ([Freshworks](https://www.freshworks.com/crm/pricing/)). These should be confirmed rather than assumed absent. Trust credentials are a quieter gap. LeadSquared advertises **ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type 2** ([LeadSquared](https://www.leadsquared.com/sales-crm-pricing/)), and Blinq, Popl and Wave all sell SOC 2 ([Popl](https://popl.co/pages/pricing); [Wave](https://wavecnct.com/pages/pricing)). Deskzo has CERT-In log retention and DPDP request handling but no certification on record.

### Inbox, helpdesk and cards: matched on features, behind on channels and proof

Deskzo's Customer inbox has a website chat bot with optional AI answers, WhatsApp Cloud on shared and personal numbers, round-robin assignment, CSAT and saved replies. Desk adds SLA targets and tickets by email (`memory/customer-inbox-decisions.md`). That matches the core of WATI and Interakt. But Instagram and Facebook are parked and staff can't send files yet, while WATI's omnichannel inbox covers WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, webchat and RCS ([WATI](https://www.wati.io/pricing/)). Help desks reserve skills routing, multiple SLA policies and multilingual help centres for upper tiers ([Zendesk](https://www.zendesk.com/pricing/)). The inventory shows no customer-facing knowledge base in Desk, which Freshdesk includes on every plan ([Freshdesk](https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/pricing/)). Support replies can't yet go out as the company's own domain with DKIM. In cards, Deskzo matches the 2026 baseline: templates, QR, "Save contact", lead capture into the CRM and event booths. But wallet passes are parked, and signature sync to Gmail, Zoho Mail and Outlook has **never been tried on the real services** (`memory/cards-and-signatures-decisions.md`). Blinq and HiHello include wallet passes on every tier ([Blinq](https://blinq.me/pricing); [HiHello](https://www.hihello.com/pricing)). CodeTwo injects signatures server-side on every plan ([WiseStamp guide](https://www.wisestamp.com/guides/best-exclaimer-alternatives/)).

## Where Deskzo is genuinely ahead, and the selling points that survive scrutiny

Deskzo's strongest argument answers the most repeated complaint about suites, which is that they are separate apps loosely joined. A Gartner Peer Insights reviewer says Zoho One is "sold as a suite but does not appear to be configured as a fully integrated suite". The reviewer reports quotes behaving differently in CRM and Books, and a tax option missing when a quote starts in CRM ([Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/cloud-based-erp-for-us-local-government/vendor/zoho/product/zoho-one)). Odoo needs partners and extra modules for GST, TDS and e-way bills ([Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/disadvantages-of-odoo-accounting)). Zoho itself splits HR across Zoho People and Zoho Payroll ([Asanify](https://asanify.com/blog/competition/asanify-vs-zoho-people/)). Deskzo runs every module on **one database per workspace with one tax engine**. That produces workflows rivals can't easily copy. CRM incentive slabs are paid on the payslip. A digital card is granted and withdrawn with the HR record, so no directory sync or SCIM is needed. An exit hands over the leaver's accounts, leads, tickets and quotes. An order runs from sales punch to accounts approval, vendor PO, tax invoice by PO and backend OEM rebate (commits ee17af25, 6cfaa42f, 1e9ef794). That IT-reseller flow, with deal registrations, negative calls and vendor credits, has no counterpart in any competitor researched.

India tax computation is the second differentiator, with caveats attached. Deskzo writes a quarterly **Form 140 file from the books, with challans linked to the deductions they paid** (db7c23f0). This research found no competitor that publicly claims the same; Zoho Books has only confirmed renumbering its sections for the Income-tax Act 2025 ([Zoho Books Help](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/settings/tds-and-tcs-updates.md)). The file has **never been run through the real FVU**, and its deductee numbering is an assumption, so the claim can't be sold until it passes. Deskzo also has dated professional-tax rules for about 17 states, including West Bengal's October 2026 slabs. It has MSME interest under s.16 charged by debit note, and payment recovery with pay links, credit hold and DSO (`memory/professional-tax-rules.md`; `memory/payment-recovery-decisions.md`). The Revenue & Close add-on (Ind AS 115 schedules and a self-checking month-end close) is something no SMB competitor's material mentions. Zoho's nearest features are budgets and period locking ([Zoho Books](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/pricing/)).

Deskzo's third strength is bundling where rivals meter. Automation steps come in the plan instead of per task. Inbox seats come with the workspace instead of 3–10 users per plan. Cards carry no per-lead fee. Several GSTINs come with no add-on. Deskzo AI runs on Claude through AWS Bedrock in the India region with zero data retention (`memory/ai-gateway-and-credits-decisions.md`), which answers the data-residency question Zoho uses in its own pitch. Attendance has two concrete wins. **Biometric push over /iclock with no connector PC** removes the always-on PC that cheap HR tools still require. Visitor management with a reception kiosk is built into the HRMS. For Indian buyers of digital cards, the comparison is with vendors charging ₹1,999 plus ₹399 a year for a card with WhatsApp buttons ([Scribd vendor sheet](https://www.scribd.com/document/777094855/Krishiv-Groups)). This research found **no Indian player offering HR-granted cards with CRM lead capture and admin signatures**.

The selling points that survive scrutiny are therefore narrow and specific. One customer, one tax engine and one record across sales, books, HR and support. The IT-reseller order-to-rebate flow. Cards and signatures run from the HR record at ₹99 and ₹59. Bundled automation and India-region AI. Biometric attendance with no connector PC. A broad, generous price at Zoho One's level. These claims will not survive scrutiny yet: "files your GST", "ready for Form 138", "works on your phone today", or anything implying the Razorpay, Stripe, Cashfree, NIC, WhatsApp, calendar or signature integrations are proven. Every one of those has met only fakes or stand-ins (`memory/billing-verified-against-fakes-only.md`; `memory/nic-einvoice-encryption.md`; `memory/calendar-integrations-decisions.md`).

## Ten gaps to close, ranked by what an Indian SMB loses without them

The ranking weighs three things: statutory exposure (penalties and CA dependence), daily-use reach (how many staff touch it), and how often the gap shows up as a checkbox on competitor pricing pages.

| Rank | Gap | Why it ranks here | Competitor benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|

The order reflects one judgement: an Indian SMB forgives a missing OKR module but not a missed GSTR-1 or a wrong ECR. Gaps 2 and 3 also share a design problem. Deskzo already computes the numbers, so the remaining work is output formats, portal or GSP connections, and validation against the government tools. The marginal cost is lower than the business impact suggests. Gap 1 overlaps gaps 2 and 3, because both depend on validating against government tools: the NIC sandbox, the FVU and the GSTN offline tool. Gap 4 matters more than its rank because two moats depend on the phone: the Kylas-style call sync and the visit GPS. Neither exists for customers until the app is public. Agentic AI is deliberately left low, in line with the owner's decision to put Automate first (`memory/agentic-deskzo-plan.md`). greytHR's free NAVOS means the HR assistant will soon be expected rather than optional.

## Conclusion

The research changes the question Deskzo should ask itself. The question is no longer "which features are missing?" On breadth, Deskzo already reads like a Zoho One with sharper India-reseller workflows and a more joined-up data model. The real question is "which built features can be proven?" The competitive moat Deskzo has, one record and one tax engine across every module, is precisely what rivals' reviewers say they lack. It only becomes sellable once the e-invoice, payment, WhatsApp and calendar paths have met the real services. Until then, the safest positioning is narrow and verifiable: IT resellers and system integrators who need order-to-rebate flows, cards and signatures from the HR record, and attendance with biometric push. All three work without a government portal.

The pricing analysis carries a second implication. Matching Zoho One's price per seat removes price as a reason to switch. Losing to Zoho Books per organisation and to Kylas and Bitrix24 flat rates means price can become a reason *not* to switch. Deskzo's levers are the things rivals meter: GSTIN add-ons, invoice caps, per-lead fees, inbox user caps, automation tasks and AI sessions. A pricing page that names those meters, paired with a free micro tier and validated GST and payroll filing, would turn the suite's breadth into a buying reason rather than a feature count.

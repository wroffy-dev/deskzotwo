# Digital Cards — Deskzo

Digital Cards in Deskzo are digital business cards for the people your company chooses. Each card shows the person's details from their record in the company's card design, with a QR code and a link that save the contact in one tap. People who open a card can share their details back, and card events help you count the people you meet at trade shows. Digital Cards is part of Deskzo Cards and Deskzo One.

## Q: What is a Deskzo digital card?
A: A digital card is a web page with a person's name, photo, job title, department, company, work phone, work email and office address, laid out by the company's card template. Its QR code and link open it on any phone, and **Save contact** puts the person straight into the phone's contacts. Details come from the person's record, so a new job title or phone number shows on the card as soon as the record changes. Whoever opens a card can share their own details back if the template asks for them.

## Q: How do I get a digital card?
A: Having a digital card is not a permission. HR or an admin issues cards to the people the company chooses. Once you have a live card, **Tools → My card** appears in your menu. Ask HR or your admin if you need a card.

## Q: How do I issue digital cards to staff?
A: To issue cards:
1. Go to **Tools → Digital cards**, on the **People** tab.
2. Under **Template**, leave **The default, or theirs**, or choose a template.
3. Under **Issue to**, choose **A department**, **A branch**, **A role** or **Everybody active**, then which one.
4. Select **Issue cards**.
To issue to particular people, tick them in the table and select **Issue or move**. A person who already has a card keeps it, unless you chose a template; then their card moves to that template. A card that was switched off is switched back on with its old address. You need the **Manage digital cards** permission.

## Q: How do I switch off a person's digital card?
A: On **Tools → Digital cards**, **People** tab, tick the people and select **Switch off**. Their card address then shows the company's details instead of theirs. Issuing the card again switches it back on.

## Q: How do I design a card template?
A: To design a digital card template:
1. Open the **Templates** tab on **Tools → Digital cards** and select **New template**, or **Edit** beside one.
2. Set its **Name**, **Colour** and **Layout** (**Classic — photo at the side** or **Centred — photo above the name**), and whether to **Show the company logo**.
3. Under **From each person's record**, choose which fields to show and in what order. **Locked** fields cannot be left off by the person.
4. Under **On every card**, add what is the same for everybody, such as the website.
5. Tick **People may add their own links and numbers** to let holders add LinkedIn, WhatsApp, a booking link and so on.
6. Tick **Ask whoever opens the card to share their details back** for the share-back form. You can add your own questions and mark them **Required**.
7. Select **Save**.
Every card on the template changes when you save, and holders are told. **Make default** sets the template new cards get.

## Q: How do I share my digital card?
A: Go to **Tools → My card**. Select **Copy link** to send it, **Download QR** to print or show the QR code, or **Open card** to see it as others do. On your phone, open your card page and add it to your home screen so the QR code is one tap away. You can also write the link to an NFC card or sticker with any NFC app.

## Q: How do I change what my card shows?
A: On **Tools → My card**, under **From your record**, untick any field you want to leave off. Fields the template has locked stay on. Under **Your own links and numbers**, add your own links if your company's template allows it. Select **Save**. Your card updates at once.

## Q: Can I change my job title on my card?
A: Not on the card itself. Your name, job title, phone and photo come from your Deskzo record. Change the record, or ask whoever keeps the records, and your digital card follows.

## Q: How do I see who shared their details from my card?
A: Open the **Contacts** tab on **Tools → My card**. People who manage cards can see everybody's on **Tools → Digital cards**. Each person shows what they wrote and, if one was made, a link to their lead. Select **Add a note** to keep your own note. You are also notified when somebody shares their details from your card.

## Q: Somebody shared their details, but no lead was made.
A: Details shared back from a card are always kept in Contacts. A lead is made only where your workspace has Leads; otherwise the contact says **No lead**. You are still notified.

## Q: How do I set up a card event for a trade show?
A: To create a card event:
1. Go to **Tools → Card events** and select **New event**.
2. Fill in its **Name**, **Venue**, **First day** and **Last day**, and if you like **Goal — people to meet** and **What it cost**.
3. Under **The team**, pick the people working the stand. Only people with a live card get a booth form.
4. Add any **Booth form questions**, then select **Create event**.
While the event runs, each holder's booth form link and QR code are on the event page. The **Results** tab shows people met against the goal, leads made, cost per person, and numbers by person and by day. You need the **Manage digital cards** permission.

## Q: How do I add somebody I met at an event?
A: To add a contact at a card event:
1. Open the event and select **Add someone you met**.
2. Select **Scan their card's QR**, or paste a card's link or contact text and select **Read**, or type their details.
3. Check the details, add **Your note**, and select **Save**.
They need an email address or a phone number. People can be added from the first day of the event until a week after the last day. The same person twice at one event is refused.

## Q: I can't add people at an event.
A: Either the card event has not started yet, or it ended more than a week ago. If the same email or phone is already in for that event, Deskzo says who has them, for example "<name> is already in for this event, with <colleague>."

## Q: It says this browser can't read QR codes.
A: Some browsers cannot read QR codes. Scan the code with your phone's camera instead, copy the link it finds, and paste it into the box on the page and select **Read**. Or type the details in.

## Q: How do I download the people met at an event?
A: Open the card event and select **Export CSV**. This downloads everybody met at the event. You need the **Manage digital cards** permission.

## Q: Can I change a card's web address?
A: A person with the **Manage digital cards** permission can change a card's address on **Tools → Digital cards**, **People** tab. It must be 3 to 40 lower-case letters and digits, with single hyphens between. The old address stops working, and printed QR codes and NFC tags stop working with it.

## Q: My card says it's switched off.
A: Somebody who manages cards switched it off, or a last working day recorded for you has passed. Until it is live again, your card link shows the company's details instead of yours. Ask HR or your admin.

## Q: Which permissions control Digital Cards?
A: The **Manage digital cards** permission lets a person issue and switch off cards, change card addresses, design templates, run card events, export people met, and see every card. Management, HR and HR head have it by default. The **Tools › Digital Cards** section tick shows Digital Cards in the menu. Both are in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.

## Q: I can't see My card, Digital cards or Card events in my menu.
A: Each link shows to different people:
- **My card** shows only to a person with a live card.
- **Digital cards** needs the **Manage digital cards** permission.
- **Card events** shows to people with **Manage digital cards**, and to people on an event's team until a week after the event ends.
If nobody sees anything, check that your company's plan includes Digital Cards (Deskzo Cards or Deskzo One), that the module is switched on in **Settings → Modules**, and that the **Tools › Digital Cards** section is ticked in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**. Ask your admin.

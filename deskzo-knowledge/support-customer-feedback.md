# Customer Feedback — Deskzo

Customer Feedback in Deskzo sends a customer a one-time link that asks how it went: overall, about the person who dealt with them, and about the work. Every answer is kept whatever the score, and a happy customer can be offered your public review page. Customer Feedback is part of Deskzo Desk and Deskzo One.

## Q: What is Customer Feedback in Deskzo?
A: Customer Feedback asks a customer for a score. You make a link from the customer's page and send it to them. They answer a short form without signing in. The link works once and then closes, and it expires after a set number of days. The form asks how it went overall, out of five stars. If you named the colleague who dealt with them, it asks about that person too. If you said what the work was, it asks about the work. The menu links are **Support → Customer feedback** and **Support → About me**.

## Q: I can't see Customer Feedback in my menu. Why?
A: Customer Feedback shows in the menu only when:
1. Your company's plan includes it (Deskzo Desk or Deskzo One).
2. The Customer Feedback module is switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. The **Support › Customer Feedback** section is not hidden for your role in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
Ask your admin to check these settings.

## Q: Which permissions does Customer Feedback use?
A: Customer Feedback uses these permissions in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**:
- **Ask customers for feedback**: make a feedback link and withdraw one. Sales, Support and Management have it by default.
- **See all customer feedback**: see every response, including scores about other people. Management has it by default.
Without **See all customer feedback**, you see feedback about you, feedback you asked for, and feedback on accounts you run (as account manager or caller). Anybody can always see what was said about their own work.

## Q: How do I ask a customer for feedback?
A: To send a feedback link:
1. Open the customer's page, go to its **Feedback** tab and select **Ask for feedback**.
2. Under **Who are we asking?**, choose a contact. You can leave it empty and send the link yourself.
3. Under **What about?**, keep **How we're doing generally**, or pick a resolved or closed ticket, a completed visit or a fulfilled order. For a general request you can describe it under **Or describe it**.
4. Under **Who dealt with them?**, choose the colleague the form should ask about, or **Nobody in particular**.
5. Add **A line from you** if you like. It shows above the form.
6. Select **Make the link**.
7. Copy the link, or select **Send on WhatsApp** or **Open in email**. Then select **Done**.
You need the **Ask customers for feedback** permission.

## Q: How do I send a customer satisfaction survey?
A: Deskzo's Customer Feedback is a short survey link. Open the customer's page, go to the **Feedback** tab, select **Ask for feedback**, choose the contact, what it is about and who dealt with them, and select **Make the link**. Send the link with **Send on WhatsApp**, **Open in email**, or by copying it. The customer answers once, without signing in.

## Q: Does Deskzo email the feedback link for me?
A: No. Deskzo does not send the feedback link itself. You send it from your own WhatsApp or email, using **Send on WhatsApp** or **Open in email**, or by copying the link. Anybody holding the link can answer it, so send it only to the right person. Each request gets a reference like FB/2026-27/0001.

## Q: What does the customer see on the feedback form?
A: The customer sees a short form with your words at the top and your own line if you added one. They rate how their experience was overall, from one star (Very poor) to five stars (Excellent). When chosen, they also rate the named colleague and the work. There is a box for comments, and **Send my feedback**. After they answer they are thanked. If they are offered your review page, they see **Leave a review**.

## Q: How do I follow up a low feedback score?
A: To record what was done about a low score:
1. Open **Support → Customer feedback** and set **Show** to **Low scores nobody has answered**.
2. On the response, select **Record what was done**.
3. Write it under **What happened next** and select **Save**.
The note is kept with your name and the date, and the colleague named in the feedback is told. A score of 3 or below waits for this follow-up. While it has no answer, marketing campaigns leave that customer out.

## Q: How do I find feedback?
A: Open **Support → Customer feedback**. Search for a company, a reference or what the customer wrote. Use **Show** to pick **Answered**, **Waiting for a reply**, **3 stars or below** or **Low scores nobody has answered**. The strip at the top shows the **Average**, how many **Replied** and the **Spread** of scores. A customer's own **Feedback** tab shows the same for that customer.

## Q: How do I send a feedback link again or withdraw it?
A: Find the response while it is **Waiting for a reply**. Select **Show the link again** to copy it again, or **Withdraw** to close it at once. Once a customer has answered, the request cannot be withdrawn. Deskzo shows "They have already answered it. Withdrawing it now would delete what they said."

## Q: What is About me?
A: **Support → About me** shows what customers said about you. It lists answered feedback where you were named as the person the customer dealt with. The overall score is about the company. The score next to your name is about you. If nobody has asked about you, it says "No customer has been asked about you yet."

## Q: How do I see feedback about my own work?
A: Open **Support → About me**. It lists every answered feedback where you were named as the colleague who dealt with the customer. You do not need any special permission to see feedback about yourself.

## Q: How do I set up Customer Feedback settings?
A: An admin opens **Settings → Customer feedback** and sets:
- **Public review page**: your Google Business Profile review link. It must start with https://. Blank means nobody is sent anywhere.
- **Offer it from**: which customers are offered the review page, by overall score. **Everybody, whatever they rate**, or a number of stars and above. The default is 4 stars and above.
- **A link lasts**: how many days a new link stays open, from 1 to 365. The default is 30.
- **What the form says at the top**: words for the customer. Blank uses the default.
Select **Save**. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission.

## Q: Is it okay to offer the review page only to happy customers?
A: Google's review policy prohibits offering the review page only to customers you know are happy ("review gating"). **Settings → Customer feedback** warns you when **Offer it from** filters by score. Choose **Everybody, whatever they rate** to offer the review page to everyone. Every answer is stored either way.

## Q: Why wasn't a happy customer offered our review page?
A: Either no **Public review page** is set in **Settings → Customer feedback**, or the customer's overall score was below **Offer it from**.

## Q: Why can't I see the Ask for feedback button?
A: You need the **Ask customers for feedback** permission, and the customer must be one you can see. A vendor's page has no Feedback tab. A reseller's end customer cannot be asked; Deskzo says "This company is managed by a reseller, so we don't contact them directly. Ask the reseller instead."

## Q: The customer says the feedback link is closed. What happened?
A: The feedback link has expired, been withdrawn or already been answered. A link works once. Make a new link from the customer's **Feedback** tab with **Ask for feedback**.

## Q: Can I ask for feedback about a ticket that is still open?
A: No. Only finished work is listed under **What about?**: resolved or closed tickets, completed visits and fulfilled orders.

## Q: Is a bad feedback score hidden or deleted?
A: No. Customer Feedback keeps every answer, whatever the score. Only the offer of your public review page depends on the score.

## Q: What do the feedback score colours mean?
A: In Customer Feedback, a score of 4 or 5 is green, 3 is amber, and 1 or 2 is red. In the summary, 4 and 5 count as happy, and 3 or below as not. A 3 is followed up like a 1 or 2.

## Q: Who is told when feedback arrives?
A: The colleague named in the feedback, the person who asked for it and the customer's account manager get an in-app notification. Each person can switch it off in **Settings → Notification preferences**.

## Q: Is Customer Feedback the same as Anonymous feedback?
A: No. Customer Feedback (in the **Support** section) collects scores from your customers. Anonymous feedback is a separate HR feature in Deskzo People for staff to give feedback inside the company. See the People guide for Anonymous feedback.

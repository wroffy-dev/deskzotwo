# Notifications and Approvals — Deskzo

Notifications is where Deskzo tells you something needs you, through the bell at the top of every page and the full list in My work → Notifications. Approvals (My work → Approvals) lists things waiting for you to approve or reject, such as a workflow approval step. Both are part of every Deskzo product.

## Q: What are notifications in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo notifications tell you when something needs you. Examples: a task assigned to you or overdue, a callback due, a meeting about to start, a lead or ticket handed to you, an expense decided, or a mention in chat. The bell at the top of every page holds your latest twenty. **My work → Notifications** holds all of them, with filters, an archive and your preferences.

## Q: How do I check my new notifications?
A: In Deskzo:
1. Click the bell at the top of the page. Unread notifications have a dot.
2. Click a notification to go to what it is about. It is then marked read.
3. Click **Mark all read** to clear the count, or **See all notifications** to open the full list.

## Q: How do I find an older notification?
A: Open **My work → Notifications**. On the **Inbox** tab, narrow the list by kind (**Any kind**), by date (**Any date**), or tick **Unread only**.

## Q: How do I archive or delete notifications?
A: In **My work → Notifications**:
1. Tick the notifications you want, or click **Select all on this page**.
2. Click **Archive** to move them to the **Archived** tab, or **Delete** to remove them for good.
Archiving also marks them read. On the **Archived** tab, **Put back** returns them to the inbox. Deleted notifications cannot be brought back.

## Q: Can I get back a notification I deleted?
A: No. **Delete** removes a Deskzo notification for good. Use **Archive** instead if you might want it again. Archived notifications can be returned with **Put back**.

## Q: How do I turn off a type of notification?
A: To choose what Deskzo tells you about:
1. Open **My work → Notifications** and click **Preferences**.
2. Under each group (My work, Sales, Support, Money, People, Customers, Security), untick **In app** for any kind you do not want in the bell.
Each switch saves straight away. To undo, click **Turn all … back on**. Your preferences apply only to you. You can also reach this page from **Settings → Notification preferences**.

## Q: Why can't I switch off a security notification?
A: A few Deskzo security notices are always on: a security alert, somebody opening a password you own, and somebody opening a project credential. They cannot be switched off, because access nobody is told about is access nobody reviews.

## Q: Which notifications are sent by email?
A: In Deskzo, the company chooses which kinds of notification are also emailed, the same for everyone. At first, two kinds are emailed: a proforma issued on your deal, and AI credits running low. On your **Preferences** tab, kinds that are emailed carry an **Emailed** mark. Everything else reaches only the bell and the Notifications page.

## Q: How does an admin choose which notifications are emailed?
A: To choose emailed notifications in Deskzo:
1. Open **Settings → Email notifications**. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission.
2. Under each group, tick **Email it** for each kind that should go to people's inboxes as well as the bell. Untick the ones that should not.
Each switch saves straight away and applies to everyone. **Back to the defaults** returns to the two kinds emailed at first. Each email is sent as it happens, one email per notification, with a link to open it.

## Q: Can I stop a notification email I don't want?
A: Not by yourself. In Deskzo, which kinds are emailed is the company's choice. Switching off **In app** in your Preferences stops the bell, not the email. Ask whoever manages your workspace settings to change **Settings → Email notifications**.

## Q: Why are birthday and holiday notifications not emailed?
A: A colleague's birthday or work anniversary and a coming holiday go to everyone in the company. Deskzo shows these in the app only, because emailing them would email every employee.

## Q: How do I turn reminder sounds on or off?
A: On **My work → Notifications → Preferences**, use the **Sounds** card. Untick **Play a sound when a reminder or a chat message arrives** to silence all sounds. Or untick one kind: a meeting about to start, a task due or overdue, a note reminder, a callback due, or a new chat message. **Play the sound** lets you hear it. Your choice follows you to other browsers.

## Q: I set a reminder but didn't hear anything. Why?
A: Deskzo sounds play only while the app is open in a browser tab, and only after you have clicked on the page once (browsers do not allow sound before that). Also check the **Sounds** card on **My work → Notifications → Preferences**, because that kind may be switched off.

## Q: A reminder came late. Why?
A: Deskzo checks reminders, due tasks and meetings about to start while you have the app open. If Deskzo was closed, the notification is there next time you open it. If your workspace emails that kind of reminder, it is checked every five minutes and emailed whether the app is open or not.

## Q: Who can see my notifications?
A: Only you. Each person in Deskzo has their own notifications. Nobody else can read them, and your preferences apply only to you.

## Q: What is the Approvals page?
A: **My work → Approvals** in Deskzo lists everything waiting for you to approve or reject. Items come from a workflow's **Ask for approval** step that names you or your role, or from a draft the AI copilot made for you. The page also shows what you decided lately. Anybody signed in can have items here.

## Q: How do I approve or reject a request?
A: To decide a request in Deskzo:
1. Open **My work → Approvals**.
2. Under **Waiting for you**, read the request and its summary.
3. For a workflow request, you can type a comment in **A word for the record (optional)**.
4. Click **Approve** or **Reject**.
For a draft from the AI copilot, the buttons are **Do it** and **Don't**. The workflow then carries on the way you chose. You also get a notification "Approval needed" when something is waiting.

## Q: Several people were asked to approve. Who decides?
A: In Deskzo, the first person asked who decides is the one who counts. After that, others see "Somebody has already decided this." Only the people or role named in the workflow's approval step may decide; anyone else sees "You aren't one of the people asked to decide this."

## Q: What happens if nobody approves in time?
A: A Deskzo approval step has a time limit (at most 30 days). If nobody decides by then, the workflow takes it as approved or rejected, as the workflow's owner set it. If you try to decide after that, Deskzo shows "The time to decide has run out; the workflow went the way it was set to go." In **Decided lately** it shows as "Nobody decided in time".

## Q: What do the statuses in Decided lately mean?
A: On **My work → Approvals**, **Decided lately** shows: **Approved**, **Rejected**, **Nobody decided in time** (the time limit passed), or **The run was stopped** (the workflow run was cancelled, so there was nothing to decide).

## Q: Is Approvals the same as document approvals or expense approvals?
A: No. **My work → Approvals** is for workflow approval steps and AI copilot drafts. Sign-off rules for sales and purchase documents are set in **Settings → Document approvals**. Expense claim approvals are set in **Settings → Expense approvals**.

## Q: I can't see Notifications or Approvals in my menu. What should I do?
A: In Deskzo, Notifications and Approvals share one switch. Check with your admin:
1. The Notifications module must be in your company's Deskzo plan.
2. It must be switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. For Notifications, your role must have the **My work › Notifications** section permission ticked in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.

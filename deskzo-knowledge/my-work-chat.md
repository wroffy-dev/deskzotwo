# Chat — Deskzo

Chat is private team messaging inside Deskzo, one to one and in groups, with files, replies, reactions and read ticks. Messages stay in your workspace, and only the people in a chat can read them. Chat is part of every Deskzo product and sits in the sidebar under My work → Chat.

## Q: What is Deskzo Chat?
A: Deskzo Chat is messaging for your team, built into Deskzo. You can message any colleague one to one, or make a group for a team, department or project. Messages can carry files, reply to an earlier message, mention somebody with @, and get a reaction. Ticks show when a message is delivered and read. Messages and files stay in your Deskzo workspace.

## Q: How do I send a message to a colleague?
A: To message a colleague in Deskzo:
1. Open **My work → Chat**, or click the chat icon at the top of the page.
2. Click **New chat** and search for the person by name or department.
3. Type your message and send it.
There is one chat for each pair of people, whoever starts it.

## Q: How do I create a group chat?
A: To make a group in Deskzo Chat:
1. In the chat list, click **New group**.
2. Type a **Group name**.
3. Pick people, or use **Add my team** or **Add a department…** to add many at once.
4. Click **Create group**.
The person who creates the group is its first admin.

## Q: How do I add or remove people in a group chat?
A: In Deskzo Chat, open the group and choose **Group info** from the chat's menu. There, a group admin can rename the group, use **Add people**, **Make admin** or **Remove**. Only group admins can do this; others see "Only the group's admins can do that." A group must always have at least one admin.

## Q: How do I leave a group chat?
A: In Deskzo Chat, open the group, choose **Group info** from the chat's menu and click **Leave group**. Anyone can leave a group. After you leave, you keep the messages from before you left. If you are the only admin, make somebody else an admin first.

## Q: Can my manager or an admin read my chats?
A: No. Only the people in a Deskzo chat can read it. Managers and admins cannot browse other people's chats. The workspace's super admin can export a single chat for an investigation or a legal request, from **Settings → Chat**. The super admin sees who is in a chat, not what was said, until they export it. Every export is recorded in the audit trail and activity log.

## Q: How do I reply to, react to or pin a message?
A: In Deskzo Chat, point at a message to see its buttons:
- **React**: pick one of six emoji. One reaction per person.
- **Reply**: answers that message and quotes it.
- **Pin to the top**: up to three pinned messages per chat. Pinning a fourth unpins the oldest.

## Q: How do I edit or delete a chat message?
A: In Deskzo Chat, point at your own message:
- **Edit** works for 15 minutes after sending.
- **Delete for everyone** works for one hour after sending.
After that the message stays as it is. You can only edit or delete your own messages.

## Q: I can't edit or delete my message. Why?
A: Deskzo Chat lets you edit your own message for 15 minutes after sending ("Messages can be edited for 15 minutes after sending.") and delete it for everyone for one hour ("Messages can be deleted for everyone for an hour after sending."). After that, the message cannot be changed. You can never edit or delete someone else's message.

## Q: How do I send a file in chat?
A: In Deskzo Chat, use the attach button in the message box. You can attach up to five files to one message, up to 5 MB in total. Allowed files: pictures, PDFs, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files, text, CSV and zip files. Web pages, SVG images and programs cannot be sent. If the files are too big, Deskzo shows "Files can come to 5 MB at most on one message."

## Q: Why can't I attach files in chat?
A: Your admin has switched off **Files and pictures** in **Settings → Chat**. Then Deskzo Chat is text only: there is no attach button and pasted pictures are refused. Deskzo shows "Files can't be sent in chat in this workspace — text only." Files already sent stay where they are.

## Q: I can't download a file somebody sent me in chat.
A: Your company has made Deskzo Chat files view-only (**Files are view-only** in **Settings → Chat**). Pictures, PDFs and text files open in the browser with no download button. Other files cannot be opened at all.

## Q: How do I mention someone in a group chat?
A: In a Deskzo group chat, type @ and pick a name. The person gets a notification saying who mentioned them and in which group. The notification never shows the message itself. A mention reaches them even if they muted the group.

## Q: What is an important message in chat?
A: In Deskzo Chat, start a message with ! (for example "!call me before 5") to mark it **Important**. It opens by itself for the others in the chat, on whatever page they are, and is heard even if they muted the chat. This works only if your admin has left **Important messages** switched on in **Settings → Chat**.

## Q: How do I mute a chat?
A: In Deskzo Chat, open the chat's menu and choose **Mute for 8 hours**, **Mute for 1 week** or **Mute for good**. **Unmute** undoes it. A muted group still tells you when you are @mentioned.

## Q: How do I get desktop alerts for new chat messages?
A: In Deskzo Chat, click the bell button at the top of the chat list. This turns on desktop alerts in this browser, so you see new messages while Deskzo is in the background. Alerts say who and where, never the message text. Chat sounds are controlled in **My work → Notifications → Preferences**, in the Sounds card.

## Q: How do I search old chat messages?
A: Type in **Search chats and messages** at the top of the Deskzo chat list. It finds chats by name and messages by their words, in chats you are a member of.

## Q: What happens when I paste a link to a customer or lead in chat?
A: When you paste a link to a customer, lead, order or ticket in Deskzo Chat, it shows as a card. Each reader sees the record's name only if they are allowed to open that record. Sharing a link never gives anyone access.

## Q: My message was not sent and Deskzo named some words. Why?
A: Your company keeps certain words out of Deskzo Chat using the word filter. Change the named words and send the message again. Nobody else is told, and nothing is recorded.

## Q: Old chat messages have disappeared. Why?
A: Your company may delete chat messages after a set time. An admin chooses this in **Settings → Chat** under **Keep messages**: keep for good, or delete messages older than 90 days, 1 year or 3 years. Old messages are deleted each night, with their files, for everybody. Deleted messages cannot be brought back.

## Q: What chat settings can an admin change?
A: An admin with the **Change organisation settings** permission can open **Settings → Chat** and set:
- **Files and pictures**: on or off.
- **Emoji button**: show or hide the emoji button.
- **Reactions**: on or off. Off hides reactions; they come back if turned on again.
- **Important messages**: whether ! marks a message as important.
- **Files are view-only**: no downloads.
- **Keep messages**: how long messages are kept.
Click **Save** after changing.

## Q: How does the chat word filter work?
A: An admin opens **Settings → Word filter** and, in the Chat section, ticks **Keep these words out of chat**. The filter is off until switched on. It has a built-in list of abusive words in English, Hindi and Hinglish. The admin can add **Your own words** (one per line; end a word with * to catch its endings), and list preset words in **Let these preset words through**. **Try a message** shows what the filter would do. A message or group name with a filtered word is not sent.

## Q: I can't start a chat with somebody. Why?
A: Their Deskzo account is switched off. Deskzo shows "Their account is switched off, so a new chat can't be started with them." You can still read an existing chat with them, but not start a new one.

## Q: I can't see Chat in my menu. What should I do?
A: If **My work → Chat** is missing in Deskzo, check with your admin:
1. Chat must be in your company's Deskzo plan.
2. The Chat module must be switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. Your role must have the **My work › Chat** section permission ticked in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
Chat is also hidden while an admin is viewing the app as somebody else, because chat is private.

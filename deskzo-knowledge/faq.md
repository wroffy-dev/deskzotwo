# Frequently Asked Questions — Deskzo

General questions about Deskzo that are not tied to one module. For step-by-step help with a feature, see that feature's guide (for example the Leads guide or the Tax Invoices guide).

## Q: What is Deskzo, in one sentence?
A: Deskzo is online business software that puts sales (CRM), accounts and GST invoicing, HR and payroll, helpdesk support, inventory, projects and marketing in one company workspace, on one set of records.

## Q: Which Deskzo product do I need?
A: Choose by what you want to run. Sales pipeline and calls: Deskzo CRM, or Deskzo Pipeline for a small team. GST invoicing and accounts: Deskzo Books (India). HR, attendance and payroll: Deskzo People (India). Helpdesk tickets: Deskzo Desk. Stock and orders: Deskzo Inventory. Recurring orders and renewals: Deskzo Subscriptions. Everything together: Deskzo One. For prices, see [PRICING: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: Can I use Deskzo outside India?
A: Yes, most Deskzo products are sold worldwide. Deskzo Books and Deskzo People follow Indian rules (GST, TDS, PF, ESI, professional tax), so those two products are sold only in India. Some features, such as E-Way Bills, only appear for workspaces in India.

## Q: Is there a free trial of Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo workspace can start on a free trial. The plan status in Settings → Plan & billing shows when the trial ends. For trial length and what it includes, see [PRICING: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: How do I sign in to Deskzo?
A: Open your company's Deskzo workspace address in a web browser and sign in with your email and password. If your company uses two-factor sign-in, also type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app. If your company turned on Microsoft, Google or Zoho sign-in, you can use the **Sign in with …** button instead. See the Sign-in and Account Security guide.

## Q: I forgot my Deskzo workspace address. How do I find it?
A: Your Deskzo workspace address is usually made from your company's registered name. Ask your admin or a colleague for the address you sign in at. In the Deskzo phone app, the sign-in screen also has **Find my workspace**.

## Q: Can I belong to more than one Deskzo workspace?
A: Yes. If you have accounts in two Deskzo workspaces, you can link them. On **My profile**, use the **Linked workspaces** card and choose **Add a workspace**. Then switch between workspaces from the switcher in the header. Each workspace keeps its own password and two-factor sign-in.

## Q: Can I rename words like "Lead" or "Customer" in Deskzo?
A: Yes. An admin can rename terms such as Lead, Company, Customer or Ticket in **Settings → Wording**. The new words then show in the menu and on screens for everybody in the workspace. That is why menu names can differ between companies.

## Q: How do I switch a Deskzo module on or off?
A: An admin opens **Settings → Modules** and switches the module on or off. Only modules in your company's plan can be switched on. Core modules, such as Companies & Contacts, are always on.

## Q: Why can't I see a feature that my colleague can see in Deskzo?
A: In Deskzo, each person sees only what their role allows. A menu link appears when the module is in your company's plan, the module is switched on in Settings → Modules, and your role has the right permission in Settings → Roles & Permissions. Ask your admin to check your role.

## Q: Can I import my existing data into Deskzo?
A: Yes. Go to **Administration → Import & export**, choose **Import** beside an area (for example Companies, Contacts, Leads or Items), download the template, fill it in, and upload it. Deskzo shows a preview of new, changed and unusable rows before importing. You need an import permission. Files can be .csv or .xlsx, up to 25 MB.

## Q: Can I move from Zoho Books to Deskzo in the middle of the financial year?
A: Yes. In **Administration → Import & export**, choose "Switching to Deskzo partway through the year?". Pick **Moving from** (Zoho Books, or another system using Deskzo's templates), the year start date and your first day in Deskzo, then bring across invoices, credit notes, receipts, bills, payments, expenses, journals and open orders step by step. Each step is checked and a migration can be undone.

## Q: Can I export my data from Deskzo?
A: Yes. People with an export permission can download data from **Administration → Import & export**, and many lists and reports also have an export or download button. Downloading financial data needs the **Export financial data** permission, and customer data needs **Export customer data**.

## Q: Does Deskzo take backups of my data?
A: An admin with the **Take database backups** permission can see backups in **Settings → Backups**, take a backup now, and switch on daily automatic backups. For how Deskzo's hosted service protects your data, see [POLICY: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: Is my data in Deskzo separate from other companies?
A: Yes. Each Deskzo workspace keeps its business data separate from every other workspace, with its own encryption keys. For details on privacy, data location and compliance, see [POLICY: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: Does Deskzo have a mobile app?
A: There is a Deskzo app for Android phones. The Deskzo app logs calls with customers automatically, and shows your day, leads, companies, contacts, chat, visits and expense claims. If **Settings → Deskzo app** does not appear on a computer, the Deskzo app is not yet available for your workspace. Download link: [LINK: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: Does Deskzo work offline?
A: No. The Deskzo website and the Deskzo phone app both need an internet connection.

## Q: Does Deskzo have a dark mode?
A: Yes. Use the theme buttons in the Deskzo header: the sun button for **Light** and the moon button for **Dark**. On a phone, the theme buttons are in the menu drawer. Your choice is remembered in that browser.

## Q: Does Deskzo have keyboard shortcuts?
A: Yes. Press **?** (outside a text box) to see every Deskzo keyboard shortcut. Deskzo Accounting also supports BUSY-style keys, such as Alt + F5 for a payment voucher and Alt + T for the trial balance.

## Q: Can a company with several branches or GST registrations use Deskzo?
A: Yes. In **Settings → Branches**, add each office, shop or warehouse with the GSTIN it bills under, its address and bank, and mark the head office. Every Deskzo document is raised from a branch and prints that branch's details.

## Q: Which emails does Deskzo send to my customers, and can I stop them?
A: An admin can see every email your customers get from Deskzo in **Settings → Customer emails**, with where each email is sent from and what stops it. The automatic Deskzo emails have an on/off switch on that screen. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission. Emails for invoices and other documents are set up in **Settings → Document emails**.

## Q: Does Deskzo use AI?
A: Yes. Deskzo AI (the AI copilot) answers questions about your company's data in plain words and can draft tasks, notes, new leads and a change to one lead for you to confirm. Deskzo AI only sees what you are allowed to see. An admin switches Deskzo AI on in Settings → Deskzo AI. For AI credit amounts and prices, see [PRICING: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: How do I add more users to Deskzo?
A: An admin goes to **Settings → Users** and adds the person with their email and role. The person gets a setup email to choose a password. Each active user uses one seat on your plan. If every seat is taken, switch off a leaver or add seats in Settings → Plan & billing. For seat prices, see [PRICING: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: How do I cancel my Deskzo subscription?
A: The workspace owner or billing manager opens **Settings → Plan & billing** and chooses the cancel option beside the plan. The plan runs to the end of the period already paid for and then does not renew. For the cancellation and refund policy, see [POLICY: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: What happens to my data if my Deskzo plan is not paid?
A: If a trial ends or a payment fails, the Deskzo workspace is held. Nothing is deleted. Paying for a plan in Settings → Plan & billing opens the workspace again at once. For how long held data is kept, see [POLICY: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: Where can I find help inside Deskzo?
A: Use the **Help** and **Video walkthroughs** tools on the tool rail on the right of the screen. **What's new** shows recent Deskzo changes. Your company may also add its own guides there. To contact Deskzo, use **Contact support** if it is shown, or see [CONTACT: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: How do I contact Deskzo support?
A: Inside your Deskzo workspace, use **Contact support** on the tool rail if your workspace shows it. Each request gets a number like SR-1042. For Deskzo's support email, phone and hours, see [CONTACT: to be filled by Deskzo team].

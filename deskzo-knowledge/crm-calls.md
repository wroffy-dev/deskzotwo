# Calls — Deskzo

Calls is the Deskzo call log for calling and sales teams. You log each call from the phone icon on a company, lead, ticket, order or renewal, record how it went, and keep a list of the callbacks you promised. Calls is part of Deskzo CRM, Deskzo Pipeline and Deskzo One, and appears in the sidebar as Prospecting → Calls.

## Q: What is the Calls feature for?
A: Calls in Deskzo keeps a record of every customer call. You make the call on your phone as usual. Deskzo times it and records who you rang, the number, how it went and what was said. The company's history then shows every conversation. A callback you promise becomes a task for you, and you are reminded when it is due.

## Q: How do I log a call?
A: To log a call in Deskzo:
1. Open the company, lead, ticket, order or renewal you are calling about, and click the phone icon (**Call**).
2. Under **Who are you calling?**, pick the contact. Their number fills in under **Number**. Or choose **Someone else at this company** and type the number.
3. Click **Start timer** when the call begins, and stop it when you hang up. **Reset** starts over.
4. Choose **How did it go?** and the **Direction**: **We called them** or **They called us**.
5. Add **Notes** if there is anything worth keeping.
6. Click **Log call**.
Only the outcome and the number are required. A call logged without the timer is saved with no duration.

## Q: How do I record a phone call with a customer?
A: Open the customer's company page (or the lead, ticket, order or renewal) and click the phone icon (**Call**). Pick the contact under **Who are you calling?**, use **Start timer** during the call, choose **How did it go?** and the **Direction**, add **Notes**, and click **Log call**.

## Q: Does the phone icon dial the number for me?
A: No. In Deskzo you make the call on your own phone as you normally do. The call dialog shows the number, times the call while you talk, and records how it went.

## Q: Who can use Calls? Which permission do I need?
A: You need the **View calls** permission. It opens the Calls screen, a company's Calls tab and the phone icon. By default the Profiling, Calling, Sales, Support, Management, Accounts and Purchase roles have it. An admin sets this in Settings → Roles & Permissions.

## Q: I can't see Calls in my menu. Why?
A: Check these with your admin:
1. Calls must be in your company's plan (Deskzo CRM, Deskzo Pipeline or Deskzo One).
2. The Calls module must be switched on in Settings → Modules.
3. Your role needs the **View calls** permission, and the **Prospecting › Calls** section must be ticked for your role in Settings → Roles & Permissions.

## Q: What do the call outcomes mean? What counts as connected?
A: The outcomes in Deskzo are Connected, No answer, Busy, Switched off, Wrong number, Left voicemail, Callback requested and Not interested. A call counts as **connected** when the outcome is Connected, Callback requested or Not interested, because somebody actually spoke to a person. The other outcomes are dials that did not connect.

## Q: How do I promise a callback?
A: When you log the call in Deskzo:
1. Set **Call back on** to the date and time you agreed.
2. Click **Log call**.
If the outcome is **Callback requested**, you must set **Call back on** before you can save. The callback becomes a task for you, titled "Call back" with the person's name, due at that time. It appears under **Callbacks owed**, and you get a "Callback due" notification when it is due. The callback is yours, not the account manager's.

## Q: How do I see and work through the callbacks I owe?
A: In Deskzo:
1. Go to Prospecting → Calls and click **Callbacks owed**. They are listed soonest first. Overdue ones show **Callback overdue**.
2. Make the call and log it from the record as usual.
3. On the earlier call, click **Done**. It then shows **Called back**.
Marking a callback done also completes its task, and completing the task in Tasks marks the callback done.

## Q: What do the four cards at the top of the Calls screen show?
A: On Prospecting → Calls, the cards show: **Calls** (how many calls, and companies reached), **Connected** (with the share of dials that reached a person), **Talk time**, and **Callbacks due** (callbacks promised whose time has passed). With no dates chosen, the cards are for today. When you choose dates, they cover that period. Callbacks due always counts every overdue callback you can see.

## Q: How do I search or filter calls?
A: On Prospecting → Calls, use the search box to search by company, contact, number or a word in the notes. Narrow the list by **Outcome**, by **Caller**, or by dates in **Called on**.

## Q: How do I see all calls with one company?
A: Open the company and click its **Calls** tab. The **Call history** lists every call on that account that you can see. You can log a new call from the same card.

## Q: Which calls can I see?
A: Two rules apply in Deskzo:
- The account: you only see calls on companies you can see.
- The caller: you see your own calls and the calls of the people who report to you.
With the **See everyone's calls, notes & meetings** permission you see every caller's calls. The headline figures are worked out from the calls you can see.

## Q: I can't see a colleague's calls. Why?
A: You see your own calls and your team's (people who report to you), and only on accounts you can see. To see everybody's calls, your role needs the **See everyone's calls, notes & meetings** permission. For a manager to see the team's calls, each team member must have that manager as **Reporting manager** in Settings → Users.

## Q: How do I see how my team is calling?
A: Go to Prospecting → Calls and click **Team report**. Choose **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week**, **Last 7 days**, **This month** or your own dates (up to three months). Each person's row shows calls, how many connected, talk time, first and last call of the day, overdue callbacks, and how fast new leads were first called. You see only the people whose calls you can see.

## Q: The call dialog says no contact has a phone number.
A: The message is "No contact here has a phone number on file. Type one below and it will be logged against the company." Nobody at that company has a usable number. Type the number in **Number** and log the call. Then add the number to the right contact on the company's Contacts tab so it is there next time.

## Q: Why can't I save the call? The Log call button is greyed out.
A: Every call needs the number that was dialled and an outcome. If you chose **Callback requested**, you must also set **Call back on**. Possible messages: "Pick how the call went." or "A call needs the number that was dialled."

## Q: Why is there no phone icon on this company?
A: The company is managed by a reseller. A reseller's customers are theirs to contact, so Deskzo does not show their numbers or the call button on the company's page.

## Q: Can I delete a call?
A: Only the workspace's super admin can delete calls. Other users see "Only the workspace's super admin can delete calls."

## Q: Does a call on a lead show on the lead's timeline?
A: Yes. A call logged from a lead in Deskzo also goes on that lead's activity timeline. A call logged from a lead, ticket, order or renewal is linked to it and shows the link in the call history.

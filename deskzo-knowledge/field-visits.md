# Field Visits — Deskzo

Field Visits is the log of client meetings and visits your people make. You plan a visit with a purpose and agenda, check in when you arrive, write up the outcome, and claim the travel against it. Field Visits is part of Deskzo CRM and Deskzo One.

## Q: What is Field Visits in Deskzo?
A: Field Visits records where your people went and what came of it. A visit is planned for a customer, with who you are meeting, the office, a purpose and an agenda. On the day you check in. Afterwards you complete it with a written outcome. Travel expenses can be claimed against the visit. A visit can also be linked to an open lead.

## Q: I can't see Field Visits in my menu. Why?
A: Ask your admin to check:
1. Your company's plan includes Field Visits (Deskzo CRM or Deskzo One).
2. The Field Visits module is switched on in Settings → Modules.
3. Your role has the **Field Visits** section ticked, and the **View visits** permission, in Settings → Roles & Permissions.
Note: your admin may have renamed "visit" in Settings → Wording, so the menu may use another word.

## Q: Which permissions does Field Visits use?
A: 
- **View visits**: see visits at all. Profiling, Calling, Sales, Support, Management, Accounts and Purchase have it by default.
- **View all field visits**: see every visit in the company, not just your own and your team's. Management has it by default. It does not let you change other people's visits.

## Q: Whose visits can I see?
A: You see your own visits and those of people who report to you. With the **View all field visits** permission you see every visit in the company. An admin can set other levels on the **Record Access** tab of a role in Settings → Roles & Permissions.

## Q: How do I plan a visit?
A: 
1. Go to Field Visits and click **Plan visit**. Or click **Plan visit** on a customer's or lead's page.
2. Choose the **Company**, then who you are **Meeting with** and the **Office**. The **Address** follows the office.
3. Pick the **Purpose** and the **Date & time**. A manager can choose another **Rep** from their team.
4. If it is for a deal, choose it under **Against lead**.
5. Add the planned **Distance (km)** and an **Agenda**.
6. Click **Plan visit**.
If you plan a visit for someone else, they get a notification.

## Q: How do I schedule a client meeting for my team member?
A: You can plan a visit for someone who reports to you:
1. Go to Field Visits and click **Plan visit**.
2. Fill in the company, contact, office, purpose and date.
3. Under **Rep**, choose your team member.
4. Click **Plan visit**. They get a notification.
If you pick someone not on your team, you see "You can only plan visits for yourself or someone on your team."

## Q: What visit purposes can I choose?
A: Intro meeting, Requirement gathering, Product demo, Proposal discussion, Negotiation, Order collection, Payment follow-up, Support escalation, Relationship building, Delivery / installation, or Other.

## Q: How do I check in to a visit?
A: Open the visit and click **Check in** when you arrive. Deskzo stamps the arrival time, so the visit can show how long you were on site.

## Q: How do I complete a visit and write the outcome?
A: 
1. Open the visit and click **Complete visit**.
2. Write the **Outcome**: what was discussed, agreed and what happens next.
3. Correct the **Distance travelled (km)** if needed.
4. Click **Complete**. The time you left is stamped.

## Q: I forgot to check in. Can I still complete the visit?
A: Yes. **Complete visit** also works on a planned visit and stamps the time you left. But without a check-in, the visit cannot show how long you were on site.

## Q: How do I claim travel expenses for a visit?
A: Open the visit and click **Add expense**. The claim is linked to this visit and listed under **Expenses claimed**, with its status. For more on claims, see the Expenses guide.

## Q: How do I cancel a visit or mark a no-show?
A: 
1. Open the visit and click **Cancel / no show**.
2. Choose **Cancelled** or **They didn't show**.
3. Write **Why**. A reason is required.
4. Click **Save**.

## Q: Why can't I delete a visit?
A: Only a planned visit can be deleted. A completed visit is a record, so Deskzo shows "A completed visit is a record — cancel it instead of deleting it." If expenses are claimed against the visit, you see "Expenses are claimed against this visit. Remove them first."

## Q: I can see a colleague's visit but can't change it. Why?
A: Seeing all visits does not let you change them. Only the person visiting and the people they report to can plan, check in, complete or cancel a visit. You may see "That isn't your visit." or "You can only edit your own visits, or your team's."

## Q: Why isn't a lead listed under Against lead?
A: Only open leads at the chosen company that you can see are offered. Won, lost and disqualified leads are not shown. Choose the company first; the lead list fills in from it.

## Q: How do I add a visit to my calendar?
A: If the Calendar module is on, open the planned visit. In the **Meetings** card, click **Add to my calendar**. If the visit moves or is cancelled later, the calendar entry changes too.

## Q: How do I find visits?
A: Go to Field Visits. Search by company, agenda or outcome. Filter by **Status**, **Purpose**, **Rep** and **Scheduled** dates. The count at the top shows how many visits are still open.

## Q: Why can't my manager see my visits?
A: Managers see their team's visits only if each person has a reporting manager. An admin sets this in Settings → Users.

## Q: Why is the Office list empty when I plan a visit?
A: The **Office** list shows the addresses saved on the customer. Add the customer's locations on the company record first. You can also type the **Address** yourself.

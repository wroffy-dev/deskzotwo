# Visitor Management (Front desk) — Deskzo

Visitor Management gives you a sign-in tablet for reception, invitations with a code for expected visitors, an instant notification to the host, and a visitor book of who is in the building. It is part of Deskzo People (India only) and Deskzo One. In the sidebar it is the **Front desk** section, with **Visitors** and **Expected visitors**.

## Q: What is Visitor Management in Deskzo?
A: Visitor Management handles visitors at your office. Visitors sign themselves in on a reception tablet: purpose, team and person they are seeing, name, phone, company, email, a photo, and anyone with them. The host is notified straight away. You can also invite expected visitors in advance with a code. **Front desk → Visitors** is the visitor book.

## Q: I can't see Visitors in my menu.
A: Check with your admin:
1. Your plan must include Deskzo People or Deskzo One.
2. Visitor Management must be switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. Your role needs the section **Front desk › Visitor Management** in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
The **Visitors** page (the visitor book) also needs **See the visitor book**. Without it you can still invite visitors from **Front desk → Expected visitors**.

## Q: Who can do what in Visitor Management?
A: - **Everyone** can invite a visitor from **Front desk → Expected visitors** and sees their own invitations.
- People with **See the visitor book** (reception, security, HR) see **Front desk → Visitors**: who signed in, who is in the building, signing out, and every invitation.
- People with **Set up reception tablets** create tablet links and keep the visitor company list.

## Q: How do I set up a reception tablet?
A: Someone with **Set up reception tablets** does this for each desk:
1. Go to **Settings → Reception tablets** (or **Reception tablets** on the Visitors page).
2. Under **Add a desk**, type **Where is it?** (for example, Main reception) and select **Create**.
3. Copy the **Tablet link** and open it in Chrome on the tablet.
4. Add it to the home screen. **Put it on the tablet as an app → Show me how** has the steps. Open it from the icon so it runs full screen.
5. In the tablet's own settings, switch off the screen lock and keep the display on.

## Q: Is the reception tablet link safe to share?
A: Treat the tablet link like a password. Anyone with it can open the sign-in screen without a login and see every employee's name and department. If it has been shared beyond the tablet, go to **Settings → Reception tablets** and select **Rotate** to give it a new link.

## Q: How do I invite a visitor?
A: To invite an expected visitor:
1. Go to **Front desk → Expected visitors** and select **Invite a visitor**.
2. Fill in **Who's coming** and **When**, plus phone, email and company if you have them.
3. Choose **What for** and how many **Others with them**.
4. **Here to see** is you, unless you pick a colleague (they are told).
5. Select **Create the invitation**.
6. Select **Copy the message** and send it yourself by WhatsApp or email.
Deskzo does not email the visitor automatically.

## Q: How do I pre-register a guest for a meeting?
A: Pre-registering is an invitation. Go to **Front desk → Expected visitors**, select **Invite a visitor**, fill in **Who's coming**, **When** and **What for**, select **Create the invitation**, then **Copy the message** and send it to the guest. They type the code at the reception tablet.

## Q: How does a visitor sign in at reception?
A: On the reception tablet:
1. With a code: select **I have an invite code**, type it, check the name and select **That's me — continue**.
2. Without a code: choose **A meeting**, **An interview**, **A delivery** or **Vendor or service**, then the team and, if known, the person.
3. Enter name, phone, company and email (email is optional for a delivery).
4. Take a photo and add anyone with them.
5. Select **Sign me in**.
The tablet shows a visitor number and resets for the next person.

## Q: Who is notified when a visitor arrives?
A: The person the visitor chose to see. If the visitor chose only a team, everyone in that team is notified. People need a department (set in **Settings → Users**) so visitors can pick their team.

## Q: A visitor's invite code doesn't work.
A: An invite code works on the day of the visit and the following morning, and only on a reception tablet. A cancelled invitation's code stops working. The tablet says "We can't find that code. Please sign in below, or ask at the desk." After many wrong tries it says "Too many wrong codes. Please ask at the desk." Check the invitation on **Front desk → Expected visitors**, or invite them again.

## Q: Can a visitor sign in without a photo?
A: A photo is needed. The message is "A photo is needed to sign in. Please ask at the desk if the camera isn't working." If the tablet camera is not working, it offers to continue without one, and the entry says the camera was unavailable.

## Q: The tablet says "This tablet isn't set up. Please ask at the desk."
A: The tablet was taken out of service, deleted, or its link was rotated. Go to **Settings → Reception tablets**, check **In service** is ticked, and open the current link on the tablet.

## Q: How do I see who is in the building and sign visitors out?
A: Go to **Front desk → Visitors**. The top shows how many visitors are in the building. When someone leaves, select **Sign out** on their entry. You need **See the visitor book**.

## Q: Why does a visitor from yesterday still show as in the building?
A: They were not signed out. On **Front desk → Visitors**, select **Close them off** for earlier days. They are marked **Not signed out**, because nobody saw them leave. Or select **Sign out** on the entry.

## Q: How do I cancel a visitor invitation?
A: Go to **Front desk → Expected visitors**, select the cross on the invitation and confirm. The code stops working. You can cancel invitations you made or that are for you; reception can cancel any. **Tidy up old ones** closes off invitations for past days.

## Q: How do I take a reception tablet out of service?
A: Go to **Settings → Reception tablets**. Untick **In service** to stop it, or select **Rotate** for a new link (then open the new link on the tablet). Delete a tablet only if nobody has signed in on it yet; otherwise take it out of service.

## Q: How do I manage the company names the tablet suggests?
A: Go to **Front desk → Visitors** and open **Companies**. The list grows as visitors type new names. **Bring our vendors across** adds your vendors. **Add one by hand** adds one more. Untick **Offered** to stop suggesting a name. Use **Merge** to fold a misspelling into the right entry. Your customers are never on this list.

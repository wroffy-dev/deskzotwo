# People (HR): employee records, My HR and setup — Deskzo

People (HR) is where a company keeps its employee records, and where staff see their own leave, attendance and payslips on the My HR page. It is part of Deskzo People (India only) and Deskzo One. In the sidebar it is in the **People** section.

## Q: What is People (HR) in Deskzo?
A: People (HR) is the Deskzo module for employees. It holds each person's employee record: employment details, personal and emergency contacts, statutory and bank details, documents, previous employment and letters. It also covers hiring, leave, attendance, holidays, celebrations and biometric terminals. Everyone gets a **My HR** page for their own leave, attendance, corrections and payslips. Salary and payslips are in a separate module, Payroll.

## Q: What links does People (HR) add to the menu?
A: In the **People** section of the sidebar, People (HR) can show: **My HR**, **People**, **Hiring**, **Leave**, **Attendance**, **Attendance exceptions**, **Letters**, **File manager**, **Celebrations** and **Biometric**. Everyone sees My HR, People, Leave and Attendance. The other links need extra permissions.

## Q: I can't see People (HR) or one of its pages in my menu. Why?
A: Check three things with your admin:
1. Your company's plan must include Deskzo People or Deskzo One.
2. People (HR) must be switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. Your role must have the permission. The section itself is **People › People (HR)** in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**. Some pages need more: **Hiring** needs **Run hiring**. **Letters**, **Celebrations** and **Biometric** need **Manage people records**. **Attendance exceptions** needs **Approve attendance location exceptions**. **File manager** needs **Use the file manager**.

## Q: Who can see what in People (HR)?
A: In People (HR):
- **Everyone** sees the People directory (name, designation, team, who they report to) and their own full record.
- **Managers** (anyone with people reporting to them, directly or lower down) open those people's records and decide their leave and attendance corrections.
- **HR** (people with **Manage people records**) see and edit everyone's record, record exits, keep the holiday calendar and leave types, issue letters, post celebrations and look after biometric terminals.
Admins have every permission. A record you are not allowed to open shows as a page that does not exist.

## Q: Which permissions control People (HR)?
A: In **Settings → Roles & Permissions**, the People (HR) permissions are:
- **Manage people records** — the HR administrator permission. Edit anyone's record, holidays, leave types, office hours, letters, celebrations, biometric. It does not show anyone's pay.
- **Run hiring** — candidates, intake links and offer letters.
- **See everyone's attendance & leave** — see everyone's attendance and leave, and read every employee record.
- **Approve leave for anyone** — decide anyone's leave or correction, not only your team's.
- **Approve attendance location exceptions** — decide requests to clock in away from the office.
- **Hand over someone's work** — move a leaver's work to colleagues.
- **Use the file manager** — only an admin can give this.
- **Import employee data** and **Export employee data** — for Settings → Import & export.

## Q: How do I set up People (HR) for my company?
A: An HR person, with an admin for step 1, does this once:
1. In **Settings → Users**, give everyone a **Department** and a **Reporting manager**. The reporting manager decides that person's leave.
2. In **Settings → Holidays & leave**, add the year's holidays and your leave types. Nobody can apply for leave until there is at least one leave type.
3. Go to **People → People**, open each person and select **Edit record** to fill in the employee record.
4. If you are moving from another system mid-year, enter opening balances on **People → Leave → Opening balances →**.
5. To mark late, left early and overtime, set office hours on **People → Attendance**.
6. If you use biometric terminals, register them on **People → Biometric**.
7. If staff must be at the office to clock in, set each office in **Settings → Attendance locations**.

## Q: How do I add many employees at once?
A: Use **Settings → Import & export** to bring employee records in from a file. You need the **Import employee data** permission. Exporting employee records, attendance, leave and candidates needs the **Export employee data** permission.

## Q: How do I edit an employee record?
A: To edit an employee record in People (HR):
1. Go to **People → People**.
2. Search by name, email, employee code or designation, and open the person.
3. Select **Edit record**.
4. Change what you need and select **Save record**.
You need **Manage people records** to edit someone else's employee record. Without it you see the message "You can't edit someone else's employee record."

## Q: Can I edit my own employee record?
A: Yes, partly. In People (HR) you can edit your own personal, address, emergency, statutory and bank details. Your employment terms (employee code, designation, joining and probation dates) are set by HR.

## Q: What does "No HR record" mean on the People page?
A: On **People → People**, the **No HR record** count shows logins that do not have an employee record yet. HR should open each one and select **Edit record** to fill it in.

## Q: How do I upload a document to an employee's file?
A: To file a document on an employee record:
1. Open the person from **People → People**.
2. On the **Documents** card, select **Upload**.
3. Choose the file and give it a name.
4. HR can untick **The employee can see this** for a document filed about the person rather than for them.
5. Select **Add to file**.

## Q: Who can see my personal and bank details?
A: In People (HR), your personal and bank details are seen by you, the people above you in the reporting line, and HR. Other colleagues only see the directory details: name, designation, team, employment type and who you report to. They cannot open your record.

## Q: What is the My HR page?
A: **People → My HR** is your own HR page in Deskzo. It shows your attendance with **Clock in** and **Clock out**, your leave, your **Attendance corrections**, your short leave count for the month, your **My in & out** card and your payslips (when Payroll is used). Managers also see **Attendance corrections waiting on you** there.

## Q: Where do I see my payslips?
A: Open **People → My HR** or **People → Payroll**. Your payslips appear under **Your payslips** once payroll has locked the month. Select **Download** on the month you want.

## Q: How do I record that an employee is leaving?
A: To record an exit in People (HR):
1. Open the person's record and select **Record exit**.
2. Fill in the **Last working day**, **How** they left and any **Notes**.
3. Leave **Deactivate their login now** ticked, unless they are serving notice and still need access.
4. Select **Record exit**.
This raises offboarding tasks (revoke access, collect the laptop, settlement, relieving letters). The **Offboarding** card on the record shows what is still open. You cannot record your own exit; you see "You can't record your own exit — somebody else has to."

## Q: How do I hand over a leaving employee's work?
A: After recording the exit:
1. Open the person's record and select **Hand over work**.
2. Choose who takes over each area, or use **Send everything to** to fill every row with one person, then change any row.
3. Add **Why (optional)**.
4. Select the **Hand over** button (it shows how many items will move).
This moves their accounts, leads, tickets, open quotes, equipment and reports. It never changes their attendance, leave or pay. Everyone who receives work is notified. You need the **Hand over someone's work** permission.

## Q: Who sets the reporting manager and department?
A: An admin sets **Department**, **Reporting manager** and **Works at** (branch) on each login in **Settings → Users**. The reporting manager decides that person's leave and attendance corrections. The department is the team used in filters, celebrations and forms.

## Q: How do I post a celebration or announcement?
A: To post on **People → Celebrations**:
1. Select **Post something**.
2. Choose **What kind** and **Who sees it**: **Everyone**, **One team** or **One person**.
3. Write a **Title** and **Message**. Pick **Who it's about** if it is about someone.
4. Set **Shows from** and **Until**.
5. Pick a colour or add an image if you like, then select **Post it**.
Birthdays, work anniversaries and holidays appear automatically from employee records and the holiday calendar. You need **Manage people records**.

## Q: Do I need to enter birthdays and work anniversaries in Celebrations?
A: No. In Deskzo Celebrations, birthdays and work anniversaries come from the employee records, and holidays come from the holiday calendar. Just keep the employee records up to date.

## Q: How do goals and performance reviews work?
A: HR starts a review cycle in **People › Goals & reviews** for a period, for everybody working or one department. Each person is reviewed by whoever they report to. Set your goals with your manager (each can have a weight and progress). At the end, rate each goal and the period overall from 1 (below expectations) to 5 (outstanding) and select **Send to my reviewer**. Your reviewer, anybody above you or HR then rates you and selects **Complete the review**. You're told, and you select **I've read my review**. HR follows every review on the cycle's page and closes the cycle when it's done.

## Q: My manager can't see my self-ratings yet.
A: They appear once you select **Send to my reviewer**. Until then only you see them. After you send it, ask your manager to change a goal; you can't.

## Q: How do I set up shifts and a roster?
A: In **People › Shifts & roster**, HR adds each shift (name, code, start and end, grace, days off; an end before the start is a night shift). In the **Roster**, tick people, choose a shift and a start day, and select **Assign**. To change one day, pick another shift or **Off** in that day's cell. Attendance then judges late, early, overtime and week offs by each person's shift for the day. People without a shift keep the office hours, Monday to Friday.

## Q: Someone on the night shift clocked out after midnight. Which day does it count for?
A: The day the shift started, as long as they clocked in that day and haven't clocked in again since.

## Q: Where do I see my insurance and add my family to it?
A: Open **People › Insurance**. It shows each company policy you are on, with your member number, the cover, the dates and the claims helpline. Select **Change family** to add or update the family members on it while the policy runs. HR adds the policies and the people on them.

## Q: How do courses work?
A: **People › Learning** lists the courses you have been given, with any finish-by date. Open the material, then select **I've finished it**. HR adds courses and gives them to people, a department or everybody. A manager can give a course to their own team, and both can see who has finished.

## Q: How do I refer someone for a job?
A: Open **People › Referrals**, fill in **Refer somebody** and select **Send the referral**. They go to HR in **Hiring** as a referral with your name on it. You are told when they are offered a role, join, or are closed.

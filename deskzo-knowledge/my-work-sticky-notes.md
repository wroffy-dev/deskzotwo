# Sticky Notes — Deskzo

Sticky Notes is a board of short notes in Deskzo for things worth remembering that are not anybody's job. A note can be private, shared with your department, shared with the whole company, or stuck to a customer. Sticky Notes is part of every Deskzo product and sits in the sidebar under My work → Sticky Notes.

## Q: What are Sticky Notes in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo Sticky Notes is a board of short notes, for example "They always order in March" or "Ask for Priya, not the front desk." A sticky note can be yours alone, shared with your department, or put on everybody's board. A note can also be stuck to a customer so it shows on that customer's page. A sticky note has no due date and nobody assigned to it.

## Q: How do I write a sticky note?
A: To write a sticky note in Deskzo:
1. Open **My work → Sticky Notes** and click **New note**.
2. Type the note. A **Title** is optional.
3. Pick a **Colour** if you like. You can filter the board by colour later.
4. Under **Who can see it**, choose **Just me**, **My team** or **Everyone**.
5. Click **Add note**.

## Q: How do I add a quick note?
A: In Deskzo, go to **My work → Sticky Notes** and click **New note**. Type your note, choose **Who can see it** (**Just me**, **My team** or **Everyone**), and click **Add note**. The note appears on your sticky notes board.

## Q: Who can see my sticky note?
A: Each Deskzo sticky note says who can see it:
- **Just me**: nobody else can read it. Not your manager, not an admin, not the super admin.
- **My team**: everybody in your department can read it. If you are not in a department, the note stays yours alone.
- **Everyone**: it goes on the board of every signed-in person in the company. Only people with the **Post a note to everyone** permission can choose this.
A note stuck to a customer is also hidden from anyone who cannot see that customer.

## Q: Can an admin read my private sticky notes?
A: No. A Deskzo sticky note set to **Just me** can be read by you alone. No permission, role or admin setting opens it.

## Q: Why is Everyone greyed out on my sticky note?
A: Choosing **Everyone** on a Deskzo sticky note needs the **Post a note to everyone** permission. The Management role has it by default. Ask your admin to tick it for your role in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**, or share the note with **My team** instead. Without the permission, Deskzo shows "You can't put a note on everybody's board."

## Q: I shared a sticky note with My team, but a colleague can't see it. Why?
A: In Deskzo, **My team** means your department. You and your colleague must both be in the same department. If either of you has no department, a team note stays with its writer. If the note is stuck to a customer, your colleague must also be able to see that customer. An admin sets each person's department in **Settings → Users**.

## Q: How do I stick a note to a customer?
A: To put a sticky note on a customer in Deskzo:
1. Open the customer's page.
2. In the **Notes** card, click **Add note**.
3. Write the note and choose who can see it.
The note shows on that customer's page and on your sticky notes board, with the customer's name on it. You can only stick a note to a record you have access to; otherwise Deskzo shows "You can't stick a note to a record you don't have access to."

## Q: How do I set a reminder on a sticky note?
A: To get reminded about a Deskzo sticky note:
1. Open the note, or write a new one.
2. Under **Remind me**, pick a date and time, or choose **This evening**, **Tomorrow 9am** or **Next Monday**.
3. Click **Save**.
At that time you get a notification with the note's title or first words. The time is in your workspace's time zone. To stop the reminder, clear it. An archived note never reminds you.

## Q: Will a shared sticky note remind my colleagues too?
A: No. A Deskzo sticky note reminder is only for the person who set it, even on a shared note. To give someone else a deadline, create a task for them in **My work → Tasks**.

## Q: How do I pin a sticky note to the top?
A: Open the note in **My work → Sticky Notes** and choose **Pin to the top of the board**. The note then stays above the others. Only the person who wrote the note can pin it.

## Q: How do I archive or delete a sticky note?
A: In Deskzo Sticky Notes, archive a note you are done with. Click **Archived** at the top of the board to see archived notes, and you can bring one back. Delete a note only when you are sure, because deleting cannot be undone. Only the person who wrote a note can archive or delete it.

## Q: Can I rearrange my sticky notes?
A: Yes. On **My work → Sticky Notes**, drag notes to put them in your own order. Use **Pin to the top of the board** to keep important notes at the top.

## Q: How do I find a sticky note?
A: On **My work → Sticky Notes**, type in **Search notes…**. You can also choose **All notes**, **Mine** or **Shared with me**, or click a colour to see only notes of that colour.

## Q: Why can't I change somebody else's sticky note?
A: In Deskzo, only the person who wrote a sticky note can edit, pin, archive or delete it. You can still read it while it is shared with you.

## Q: Should I use a sticky note or a task?
A: Use a Deskzo sticky note for something worth remembering that is nobody's job. Use a task (**My work → Tasks**) when somebody has to do something by a date. A task has an assignee, a due date and a status; a sticky note does not.

## Q: Is there anything to set up for Sticky Notes?
A: No. The Deskzo sticky notes board is ready for everybody once the module is on. For **My team** to work, people need a department, set by an admin in **Settings → Users** (departments are created in **Settings → Departments**). To let more people post to everyone, an admin ticks **Post a note to everyone** for their role in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.

## Q: I can't see Sticky Notes in my menu. What should I do?
A: If **My work → Sticky Notes** is missing in Deskzo, check with your admin:
1. Sticky Notes must be in your company's Deskzo plan.
2. The Sticky Notes module must be switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. Your role must have the **My work › Sticky Notes** section permission ticked in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.

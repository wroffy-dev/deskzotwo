# Glossary: People, My Work, Automate and Tools Terms — Deskzo

Plain definitions of Deskzo words used in HR, Payroll, Visitors, IT Assets, Tasks, Chat, Calendar, Approvals, Automate, the Credential Vault, Digital Cards and Email Signatures.

## Q: What is My HR in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo My HR is your own HR page: clock in and out, leave, attendance corrections, short leave and payslips. Find it at People → My HR.

## Q: What is short leave in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo short leave is a few hours off on one working day. It uses no leave balance, up to a monthly count.

## Q: What is an attendance correction in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo attendance correction is a request to fix one day's attendance. Your manager decides it.

## Q: What is an attendance exception in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo attendance exception is permission to clock in or out away from your office's GPS area.

## Q: What is an intake link in Deskzo Hiring?
A: A Deskzo intake link is a 14-day link a candidate uses to fill in their own details before joining. Convert to employee then turns an accepted candidate into an employee with a login.

## Q: What is "Hand over work" in Deskzo?
A: "Hand over work" moves a leaving employee's accounts, leads, tickets, quotes, equipment and reports to colleagues in Deskzo.

## Q: What is the File manager in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo File manager holds folders where an employee sees their own payslips, letters and documents, given by an admin.

## Q: What is full and final settlement in Deskzo Payroll?
A: Deskzo full and final settlement is a leaver's last pay, with leave encashment and gratuity, less any recoveries.

## Q: What is loss of pay in Deskzo Payroll?
A: Deskzo loss of pay is pay deducted for days recorded Absent or as unpaid leave. Days with nothing recorded never count as loss of pay.

## Q: What is Speak up in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo Speak up is an anonymous feedback channel that stores no sender name. Forms can also be Anonymous, or Attributed (linked to the person's name).

## Q: What is a reception tablet in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo reception tablet is a tablet at the front desk running a no-login sign-in link for visitors. Expected visitors type an invite code on it.

## Q: What does "Whose is it?" mean on a Deskzo IT asset?
A: "Whose is it?" is a Deskzo asset's ownership: Ours — used internally, Ours — at a client, or The client's — we manage it.

## Q: What is a consignment in Deskzo IT Assets?
A: A Deskzo consignment is a shipment of assets, with a delivery challan and, in India, an e-way bill if needed. "Cover running out" lists assets whose warranty and AMC end within 30 days or have lapsed.

## Q: What does Upcoming mean on a Deskzo task?
A: An Upcoming Deskzo task is one whose start date is still in the future.

## Q: What does Need help mean on a Deskzo task?
A: Need help is a Deskzo task status for an open task where the assignee asked for help. It needs a note saying what help is needed.

## Q: What is XP in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo XP is points earned by finishing a task on or before its due date. XP shows on the Performance board. A challenge groups tasks under one shared goal.

## Q: What is a sticky note in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo sticky note is a short note with no due date or assignee. It is visible to Just me, My team or Everyone.

## Q: What is an important message in Deskzo Chat?
A: A Deskzo important message is a chat message starting with "!". It pops up for others and makes a sound even when the chat is muted.

## Q: What is a calendar subscribe link in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo calendar subscribe link is a private web address that shows your Deskzo calendar in your phone's calendar app.

## Q: What is Approvals in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo Approvals, under My work, is the inbox of workflow approval steps and AI copilot drafts waiting for you.

## Q: What is a workflow in Deskzo Automate?
A: A Deskzo workflow is a trigger plus ordered steps that Deskzo Automate runs on its own. Each time it runs is a run, with every step recorded.

## Q: What do draft, publish and owner mean in Deskzo Automate?
A: In Deskzo Automate, edits go into a draft, and publishing makes the draft the running version. The owner is the person a workflow runs as; only the owner changes, tests or publishes it. "Take over" makes another person's workflow yours.

## Q: What is a connection in Deskzo Automate?
A: A Deskzo connection is a saved Slack, Microsoft Teams or outside-app destination for workflow steps. A webhook address is a private web address that lets an outside app start a workflow.

## Q: What does "Needs a decision" mean in Deskzo Automate?
A: "Needs a decision" is a Deskzo workflow run that stopped at a step whose outcome is unknown. A person must retry or cancel it.

## Q: What is the Credential Vault in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo Credential Vault is the encrypted store of the company's own logins. An admin override opens a record not shared with you; it is recorded and the owner is told.

## Q: What is a digital card in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo digital card is a person's business card web page with a QR code and link. A card template sets the design every card on it follows.

## Q: What is a share-back on a Deskzo digital card?
A: A Deskzo share-back is the details a person enters on someone's digital card. They are saved to Contacts and can become a lead. A card event counts people met at a trade show against a goal.

## Q: What is a signature template in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo signature template is the company email signature design, filled in with each person's details. "Who gets which" rules decide which template a person gets.

## Q: What is the Outlook add-in in Deskzo Signatures?
A: The Deskzo Outlook add-in is a Microsoft 365 add-in that puts your Deskzo email signature into each new Outlook message.

# Glossary: Workspace, Account and Admin Terms — Deskzo

Plain definitions of Deskzo words about your workspace, signing in, users, roles, settings and help.

## Q: What is a workspace in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo workspace is your company's own Deskzo, at its own web address, with its own data. Everybody in the company signs in to the same workspace.

## Q: What is the workspace address in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo workspace address is the web address you sign in at. It is made from the company's registered name, or it can be the company's own custom domain.

## Q: What is the workspace owner in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo workspace owner is the person who signed the company up. The owner holds every permission.

## Q: What is the super admin in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo super admin is the workspace owner's account. The super admin holds every permission, cannot be locked out, and alone can give certain critical permissions, change roles, and delete some records.

## Q: What is a seat in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo seat is one active user account counted against your plan. A switched-off user does not use a seat.

## Q: What is a module in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo module is one part of the app, such as Leads, Tax Invoices or Payroll. Admins switch modules on or off in Settings → Modules. Core modules are always on.

## Q: What is Wording in Deskzo?
A: Wording is the Deskzo Settings screen where an admin renames terms such as Lead or Customer to suit the business, for example "Enquiries" or "Students".

## Q: What is a role in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo role is a set of permissions given to users, set in Settings → Roles & Permissions. Each user has one role.

## Q: What is a section permission in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo section permission shows or hides a whole menu section for a role. Section permissions are named like **Sales › Leads** in Settings → Roles & Permissions.

## Q: What is Record Access in Deskzo?
A: Record Access is a tab on a Deskzo role that sets which records the role reaches for each action: None, Own, Team, Branch, All, or As the account.

## Q: What is an exception to a role in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo exception is a permission or access change made for one person only, on top of their role. It can be temporary.

## Q: What is View as user in Deskzo?
A: View as user lets a Deskzo admin see the app exactly as another user sees it. Anything done is logged under the admin's name.

## Q: What is an account lock in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo account lock is a notice that blocks one person, or the whole company, from using the app while still letting them sign in to see the notice.

## Q: What is DLP in Deskzo?
A: DLP (data loss prevention) in Deskzo is a set of security settings that limit copying, printing, screenshots and exports.

## Q: What is maintenance mode in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo maintenance mode takes the app down for everyone except admins, with a message, while changes are made.

## Q: What does "workspace held" mean in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo workspace is held when no plan is paid for, for example after a trial ends. Data is kept, and paying opens it again at once.

## Q: What is two-factor sign-in in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo two-factor sign-in asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone as well as your password.

## Q: What is a temporary password in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo temporary password is one an admin sets for you. You must choose your own password at your next sign-in.

## Q: What is a setup email in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo setup email is the email a new user gets to choose their password. The link works once, for three days.

## Q: What are linked workspaces in Deskzo?
A: Linked workspaces are your accounts in several Deskzo workspaces joined together, so you can switch between them from the header without signing in again.

## Q: What is Getting Started in Deskzo?
A: Getting Started is the Deskzo dashboard tab and pop-up wizard that list setup steps, such as the company profile and two-factor sign-in, until you finish onboarding.

## Q: What is the tool rail in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo tool rail is the column of icons on the right edge of the screen: My tasks, Help, What's new, calculators, the AI copilot and Contact support.

## Q: What is the create menu in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo create menu is the button in the header to start a new lead, company, order, ticket and more from any page.

## Q: What is Deskzo AI or the AI copilot?
A: Deskzo AI (the AI copilot) is a chat assistant that answers questions about your data and drafts tasks, notes, new leads and a change to one lead for you to confirm. It uses Deskzo AI credits.

## Q: What are Deskzo AI credits?
A: Deskzo AI credits are the allowance the AI copilot uses. Credits renew, can be topped up with credit packs, and may have a daily limit per person.

## Q: What are "your company's guides" in Deskzo?
A: "Your company's guides" are help articles and videos your company adds for its own people. Deskzo shows them apart from Deskzo's own help.

## Q: What is a support request (SR number) in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo support request is a request sent with **Contact support**. Each one gets a number like SR-1042.

## Q: What is the Deskzo app?
A: The Deskzo app is the Android phone app. You pair it with your account, and it logs calls with customers and shows your day, sales, chat, visits and expense claims.

## Q: What is a pairing code in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo pairing code is a ten-character code and QR code from Settings → Deskzo app that links a phone to your account. It works once, for ten minutes.

## Q: What is call sync in Deskzo?
A: Call sync is the Deskzo app feature that logs your calls with known customers and contacts automatically.

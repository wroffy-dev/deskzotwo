# Targets and Performance — Deskzo

Targets in Deskzo give a person, a team or the whole company a number to hit over a month, a quarter or a financial year, such as invoiced value, collections, calls connected, visits or tickets resolved. Deskzo works out the achievement from your records every time you look. The Performance page shows each user's activity. Targets is part of Deskzo CRM and Deskzo One.

## Q: What are Targets in Deskzo?
A: Targets give each person, team or the whole company a number to hit over a period, and show how they are doing. Deskzo stores only the target itself. The achievement is worked out from the records every time the page loads, so a cancelled invoice or a reassigned lead changes the figure straight away. Each target card says whether you are ahead of pace, on track, behind or at risk, and what each remaining day needs to look like.

## Q: How do I check my targets?
A: To see your own targets:
1. Go to **Targets & incentives → My targets**.
2. Each card shows what you achieved against the target, a bar with a mark for how far through the period you are, and what is left to do per day.
3. If a figure looks wrong, open **What counts** on the card to see exactly what is counted.
Targets whose period has ended move to **Finished**, kept as they were measured.

## Q: How do I set a target for a salesperson?
A: To set a target for one person, a team or the company:
1. Go to **Targets & incentives → All targets** and select **Set a target**.
2. Leave **One person** selected.
3. Under **What's being measured**, pick the measure. Under **Over what period**, pick this month, next month, this quarter, next quarter or the financial year.
4. Under **Who carries it**, choose **One person**, **A team** or **The whole company**, then the person or team.
5. Type **The number to hit**. For one person, Deskzo shows what they did in the period before; **Use +10%** fills in that figure plus ten per cent.
6. Add a **Note** if you like, and select **Set it**.
You need the **Set targets** permission. The person gets a notification.

## Q: How do I give targets to a whole team at once?
A: To set targets for many people together:
1. In **Targets & incentives → All targets**, select **Set a target**, then **A whole team**.
2. Pick the measure and the period.
3. Under **Team**, choose a department, or leave **Everybody**.
4. Select **Show what they did last period** to see each person's previous figure.
5. Type a number for each person who should have one, and select **Set them**.
Anybody left blank is skipped. Nobody is given a target of nothing.

## Q: What can a target measure?
A: Measures available in Deskzo targets: Invoiced value, Collected, Order value, Order margin, Purchase savings, Leads won, Value of leads won, Leads created, Calls connected, Time on the phone, Companies added, Contacts added, New customers won, Added seats, Visits completed and Tickets resolved. Leads won, Value of leads won and Leads created are offered only where your plan includes Leads. Open **What counts** on any target card to see the exact rule for that measure.

## Q: What counts towards invoiced value and collected targets?
A: Invoiced value counts tax invoices issued, less credit notes in the same period. Collected counts money received in the period, whenever it was invoiced. Order value and Order margin count orders you punched once they are past approval. Open **What counts** on the target card for the full rule.

## Q: What periods can a target cover?
A: A Deskzo target can cover this month, next month, this quarter, next quarter or the financial year. Quarters and years follow the Indian April-to-March financial year, so Q1 is April to June.

## Q: How is "on track" decided?
A: Deskzo judges by pace, not just percentage. The target card compares what has been achieved with what an even run rate would have reached by today.
- More than ten per cent ahead: **Ahead of pace**.
- Within ten per cent: **On track**.
- More than a quarter behind, with the period past half way: **At risk**.
- Anything else short of pace: **Behind pace**.

## Q: Why did my target figure go down?
A: Target achievement is worked out fresh from the records every time. A cancelled invoice, a credit note, or a lead moved to somebody else shows straight away. Open **What counts** on the target card to see the rule for that measure.

## Q: It says there is already a target for that. What now?
A: Each person, team or the company can have only one target for the same measure and period. Deskzo says: "There's already a … target for … Edit that one rather than adding a second." Edit the existing target, or pick a different period or measure.

## Q: Who can see whose targets?
A: Everybody sees their own targets. Without extra permissions, you also see the targets of anybody who reports to you (directly or further down), and every team and company target. With **See everyone's targets**, you see the whole company's targets and **All targets** appears in your menu. With **Set targets**, you can set targets and also see everybody's.

## Q: Can I see my team's targets?
A: Yes. You see the targets of everybody who reports to you, directly or further down, if their reporting manager is set in **Settings → Users**. Select a person's name on a target card to open all of their targets.

## Q: How do I find targets that need attention?
A: Go to **Targets & incentives → All targets**. The counts at the top show how many targets are **Met**, **On track**, **Behind** and **At risk**. Narrow the list with **Measure**, **Whose** and **Period** (current and upcoming by default, or **Finished** or **Everything**). Select a person's name to see all their targets.

## Q: Which permissions control Targets?
A: These permissions are in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**:
- **Set targets**: give people, teams and the company targets; also shows everybody's targets. Management has it by default.
- **See everyone's targets**: shows **All targets** in the menu. Management has it by default.
- **View team performance**: opens the Performance page. Management has it by default.
- The **Targets & incentives › Targets** section tick shows Targets in the menu. Every role has it until an admin unticks it.

## Q: What do I need to set up before using team targets?
A: People need a department and a reporting manager. An admin sets both for each person in **Settings → Users**. Departments are in **Settings → Departments**. A team target counts the work of everybody in that department.

## Q: Do team and company targets pay an incentive?
A: No. Team and company targets are measured and shown like any other target, but only a target for one person can turn into an incentive. To pay for a target, attach an incentive scheme to it (see the Incentives guide).

## Q: What is the Performance page?
A: The Performance page shows each user's activity for a period: active time, records created, records edited, leads won, tickets open, tickets resolved, average resolution time and SLA met. Where Tasks is on, it also shows **Task XP**, earned by finishing tasks on time. Open it from **Reports → Performance** and pick a **Period**. You need the **View team performance** permission.

## Q: How do I see my team's activity?
A: Go to **Reports → Performance** and pick a **Period**. The table lists each user with active time, records created and edited, leads won, ticket figures and SLA met. You need the **View team performance** permission. Management has it by default.

## Q: I can't see Performance in my menu.
A: The Performance page appears under **Reports** only for people with the **View team performance** permission. Ask your admin to add it to your role in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.

## Q: I can't see Targets, or All targets, in my menu.
A: Targets shows in the menu only when all of these are true:
1. Your company's plan includes Targets (Deskzo CRM or Deskzo One).
2. The Targets module is switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. The **Targets & incentives › Targets** section is ticked for your role in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
**All targets** appears only to people with the **See everyone's targets** permission. Ask your admin if you need it.

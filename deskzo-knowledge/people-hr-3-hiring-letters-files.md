# People (HR): hiring, letters and file manager — Deskzo

Hiring, Letters and File manager are part of People (HR), in Deskzo People (India only) and Deskzo One. Hiring tracks candidates up to their joining day, Letters issues HR letters with your own templates and signature, and File manager lets chosen staff see their own payslips, letters and documents.

## Q: How do I add a candidate in Hiring?
A: To add a candidate:
1. Go to **People → Hiring** and select **Add candidate**.
2. Fill in their name and email.
3. Fill in the offer: team, reporting manager, **Access role on joining**, **Offered CTC (annual)** and **Expected joining**.
4. Select **Add**.
You need the **Run hiring** permission.

## Q: How do I hire someone in Deskzo from start to joining?
A: The Hiring steps are:
1. **People → Hiring → Add candidate**, with the offer details.
2. Open the candidate and select **Issue intake link**. Send the link to them so they fill in their address, PAN and bank details.
3. Draft their offer letter from the **Letters** card and select **Mark offer sent**.
4. When they accept, select **Offer accepted**. If not, select **They declined** and record why.
5. On their joining day, select **Convert to employee**, check **Joining date**, **Employee code** and **Probation (months)**, and select **Create their record**.

## Q: What is the candidate intake link?
A: In Deskzo Hiring, the intake link is a link you send to a candidate so they fill in their own details (address, PAN, bank details) without a login. Open the candidate and select **Issue intake link**, then copy and send it yourself. The link lasts 14 days.

## Q: How do I convert a candidate into an employee?
A: Open the candidate in **People → Hiring** and select **Convert to employee**. Check the **Joining date**, **Employee code** and **Probation (months)**, then select **Create their record**. This creates their login, emails them a link to choose a password, moves their details, documents and letters to the new employee record, and raises onboarding tasks for IT, HR and their manager. Set their salary in Payroll straight after.

## Q: Why can't I convert a candidate?
A: Converting needs the **Manage people records** permission; **Run hiring** alone is not enough ("Only HR can convert a candidate."). The candidate must also be marked **Offer accepted** first: "Mark the offer accepted first — only somebody who has said yes can be converted." If the email already has a login, you see a message that someone already has an account with that email.

## Q: I can't see Hiring in my menu.
A: Hiring needs: your company's plan to include Deskzo People or Deskzo One, People (HR) switched on in **Settings → Modules**, and the **Run hiring** permission on your role in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**. Ask your admin.

## Q: Why can't I draft an offer letter for a candidate?
A: The offered CTC must be set first. Deskzo shows: "Set the offered CTC first — an offer letter that doesn't state the pay is not an offer." Edit the candidate, fill in **Offered CTC (annual)**, then draft the offer letter.

## Q: How do I issue a letter to an employee?
A: To issue an HR letter:
1. Open the person from **People → People**.
2. On the **Letters** card, pick a built-in letter or one under **Our templates**, and select **Draft**.
3. Edit the wording. Tick or untick **Add digital signature**. Select **Save changes**.
4. Select **Issue**, then **Print**.
A letter is dated the day it is issued.

## Q: How do I change a letter that is already issued?
A: An issued letter's wording is fixed, because its letter number has been given out. Select **Revoke** on it. It stays on record as withdrawn. Then draft a new letter.

## Q: How do I make my own letter template?
A: To create a letter template:
1. Go to **People → Letters**, choose **Our templates**, and select **New template**.
2. Give it a **Name** and a **Code** of 2 to 5 letters. The code goes in the letter number, like WRF/BON/2026/001.
3. Write the **Subject** and the **Letter**. Click a field on the right (employee name, CTC, joining date and so on) to insert it. It fills in from the person's record.
4. Select **Save template**.
A field that is empty on someone's record leaves a blank line to fill in on the draft. You need **Manage people records**.

## Q: How do I add a digital signature to letters?
A: Go to **People → Letters** and choose **Signature**. Select **Upload an image** (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 256KB) or **Sign here** to sign with a mouse or finger, then **Save signature**. Leave **Sign new letters** ticked so new letters start signed. Each draft still has its own **Add digital signature** switch. This is a picture of the signature, not an Aadhaar eSign or a DSC.

## Q: I can't see Letters in my menu.
A: **People → Letters** needs the **Manage people records** permission, People (HR) switched on in **Settings → Modules**, and a plan that includes Deskzo People or Deskzo One. Ask your admin.

## Q: What is the File manager?
A: **People → File manager** lets an employee see and download their own salary slips, salary certificates, offer and joining letters, other letters and documents, in folders. It needs the **Use the file manager** permission, which only an admin or the super admin can give. Each person sees only their own files, and never draft or withdrawn letters.

## Q: How do I give an employee access to the File manager?
A: As an admin or super admin:
1. Go to **People → File manager** and choose **Employees**.
2. Switch the file manager on beside each person, or select **Give everyone**.
You cannot take the file manager away from yourself.

## Q: How do I hide a file from an employee?
A: As an admin, go to **People → File manager → Employees** and choose the person. Open a folder (**Salary slips**, **Salary certificates**, **Offer & joining letters**, **Other letters** or **Documents**). Switch each file on or off, or use **Show all** or **Hide all**. A hidden file is kept from that person everywhere (My HR, Payroll and their Letters card) until switched back on. HR and payroll still see it.

## Q: I can't see File manager in my menu.
A: The File manager needs the **Use the file manager** permission. No role has it by default; only an admin or super admin can give it, from **People → File manager → Employees** or in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**. People (HR) must also be switched on in **Settings → Modules**.

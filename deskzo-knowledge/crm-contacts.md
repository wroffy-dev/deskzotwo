# Contacts Library — Deskzo

The Contacts Library is one searchable list of every contact at every company you can see: clients, resellers, vendors, OEMs and partners. It is part of Deskzo CRM, Deskzo Pipeline and Deskzo One, and appears in the sidebar as Sales → Contacts.

## Q: What is the Contacts Library?
A: In Deskzo every contact belongs to a company and is added on that company's page. The Contacts Library lists the people from every company together. You can find a person without knowing where they work, or pull out every purchase head in one industry. From a row you can open the person's company, see if their email was checked, and reach them by WhatsApp, LinkedIn, a meeting (with Calendar) or a logged call (with Calls).

## Q: How do I open the Contacts Library?
A: Go to Sales → Contacts in the sidebar. You need the **View contacts** permission. Your admin may have renamed "Contacts" in Settings → Wording, so the menu name may differ.

## Q: I can't see Contacts in my menu. Why?
A: Check these with your admin:
1. The Contacts Library must be in your company's plan (Deskzo CRM, Deskzo Pipeline or Deskzo One).
2. The Contacts Library module must be switched on in Settings → Modules.
3. Your role needs the **View contacts** permission, and the **Sales › Contacts Library** section must be ticked for your role in Settings → Roles & Permissions.

## Q: Which contacts can I see in the Contacts Library?
A: With the **See every account, not just your own** permission you see every company's contacts. Without it you see the contacts at the accounts you manage and at your team's accounts. Sales does not have "See every account" by default.

## Q: How do I find a contact?
A: To find a contact in Deskzo:
1. Go to Sales → Contacts.
2. Type in the search box. You can search by name, email, phone or company.
3. Narrow the list with **Relationship** (client, reseller, vendor and so on), **Designation**, **Industry** or **Primary only**.
4. Click the company name on a row to open that company's Contacts tab.

## Q: How do I search for a person's phone number or email?
A: Go to Sales → Contacts and type the name, email, phone number or company in the search box. The matching contacts appear. If a person's email and phone are hidden, they work for a reseller's end customer (see the question about hidden details).

## Q: How do I add a new contact?
A: You cannot add a contact from the Contacts Library. In Deskzo every contact belongs to a company. Open the company (Sales → Companies or Sales → Customer), go to its **Contacts** tab, click **+ Add contact**, fill in the details and click **Save contact**.

## Q: How do I edit a contact's details?
A: Edit one person on their company's page. Open the company, go to the **Contacts** tab and click the pencil (**Edit contact**) on their row. In the Contacts Library you can only change several people's designation at once.

## Q: How do I see contacts who have left their company?
A: By default the Contacts Library shows only people still at their company. Set the **Status** filter to **Left the company** to see only those who have left, or **Everybody** to see both. A person who has left shows a **Left** badge.

## Q: Why isn't somebody I know about in the Contacts Library?
A: Either the person has left their company (set **Status** to **Everybody**), or they work at a company you cannot see. Without the **See every account, not just your own** permission, you only see contacts at the accounts you and your team manage.

## Q: How do I verify email addresses before sending a mailing?
A: In Sales → Contacts:
1. Filter the list to the people you plan to write to.
2. Tick them, or tick the box at the top to select the whole page.
3. Click **Verify emails**.
Each address is checked against its domain's mail records and gets **Verified**, **Reaches someone** (a shared or personal inbox) or **Bad address**. The address is never changed. If someone edits the address later, the old result no longer counts and it shows as not checked.

## Q: What do Verified, Reaches someone and Bad address mean?
A: These are email check results in Deskzo. **Verified** means the address looks deliverable. **Reaches someone** means the domain accepts mail but it may be a shared or personal inbox. **Bad address** means mail to it will probably not arrive. Fix bad addresses on the contact's company page.

## Q: How do I give several contacts the same designation?
A: In Sales → Contacts, tick the contacts, type or pick the designation in **Designation to give them**, and click **Apply**. If you click Apply with nothing chosen you see "Pick a designation to apply."

## Q: How do I delete contacts in bulk?
A: In Sales → Contacts, tick the contacts, click **Delete** and confirm. Contacts linked to a lead are kept, and the message says how many (for example "2 kept — they're linked to a lead."). Deleting cannot be undone. For someone who has moved on, it is better to mark them as left on their company's page, so their history stays.

## Q: Some contacts weren't deleted. Why?
A: A contact linked to a lead cannot be deleted in Deskzo. Mark them as left on their company's Contacts tab instead.

## Q: Why are some emails and phone numbers hidden in the Contacts Library?
A: Those people work for a reseller's end customer. You contact them only through the reseller. Their details are shown only to people with the **View reseller-managed contact details** permission (Management and Support by default).

## Q: Can I filter contacts by my own custom fields?
A: Yes. Contact fields your admin added in Settings → Custom fields can be used as filters in the Contacts Library (the ones you are allowed to see). Fields set to show as a column appear as columns in the list.

## Q: How do I keep the Designation filter tidy?
A: An admin manages the list of contact designations in Settings → Lists. A designation typed on a contact that is not in the list is added to it automatically.

## Q: How do I export contacts to a spreadsheet?
A: Go to Settings → Import & export. You need the **Export customer data** permission. No built-in role has it by default; admins do. The export holds only the contacts you can already see.

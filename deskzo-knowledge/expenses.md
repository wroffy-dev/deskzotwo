# Expenses — Deskzo

Expenses is where people claim what they spent for the business, attach the receipt, and send it for approval. HR (or the reporting manager, as the company chooses) approves, and Accounts pays it back. Expenses is part of Deskzo Books and Deskzo One.

## Q: What is the Expenses module?
A: Expenses in Deskzo is where you claim business spending: a cab to a client, a hotel, fuel, a software subscription, office supplies. Each claim has a category, amount, date, how it was paid, what it was for and ideally a receipt. A claim moves through **Draft**, **Awaiting approval**, **Approved** or **Rejected**, and finally **Reimbursed**. Open it from **People → Expenses**.

## Q: How do I claim an expense?
A: To claim an expense in Deskzo:
1. Go to **People → Expenses** and select **New expense** (or use **Create → New expense** in the header).
2. Under **What was spent**, choose the **Category** and type the **Amount**. If there was GST you can claim, enter it in **Of which tax**.
3. Set **Spent on** (the date) and **Paid by**.
4. Leave **Reimburse me for this** ticked if you paid yourself. Untick it if the company paid (for example a company card).
5. Write **What it was for**.
6. Under **What it was against**, pick the **Company** (customer) if any, or leave it blank.
7. Attach the **Receipt**.
8. Select **Submit for approval**, or **Save as draft** to submit later.

## Q: How do I submit an expense report / reimbursement request?
A: In Deskzo each expense is its own claim. Go to **People → Expenses**, select **New expense**, fill in **Category**, **Amount**, **Spent on**, **Paid by** and **What it was for**, keep **Reimburse me for this** ticked, attach the receipt, and select **Submit for approval**.

## Q: Which expense categories are there?
A: Deskzo expense categories are: Travel, Fuel, Mileage, Toll & parking, Accommodation, Meals, Client entertainment, Courier, Phone & internet, Office supplies, Software subscription, Marketing, Training, Repairs & maintenance, Professional fees and Other.

## Q: What payment methods can I choose in Paid by?
A: In a Deskzo expense claim, **Paid by** can be Cash, Personal card, Company card, UPI or Bank transfer.

## Q: The receipt won't attach.
A: A Deskzo expense receipt must be a PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF of 512KB or less. If it is too big you see "That receipt is over 512KB — use a smaller file." Take a smaller photo or save the PDF at lower quality.

## Q: It says my claim needs a photo of its receipt.
A: Your company has set an amount above which a Deskzo claim cannot be submitted without a receipt. The message reads "A claim over ₹… needs a photo of its receipt. Add one, then submit it." Attach the receipt and submit again. You can save a draft without one.

## Q: How do I claim travel for a field visit?
A: In Deskzo, open the field visit and select **Add expense**. The form opens tied to the visit with Travel chosen, and the company comes from the visit. Fill in the rest and submit. The claim shows on the visit.

## Q: Who approves my expense claim?
A: It depends on the setting in **Settings → Expense approvals** in Deskzo:
- **HR** — anybody with **Approve expense claims (HR)** decides.
- **The reporting manager** — your manager decides. If you have no manager, someone with **Approve or reject expense claims** does.
- **The reporting manager, then HR** — your manager approves first, then HR.
Nobody can approve their own claim. Your claim shows "Waiting on …" with the approver's name.

## Q: How do I approve or reject expense claims?
A: To decide claims in Deskzo:
1. Go to **People → Expenses** and select the **To approve** tab (or **To approve (HR)**). It shows how many wait for you.
2. Open a claim and check it and its receipt.
3. Select **Approve** (or **Approve and send to HR** when HR approves after the manager).
4. To reject, select **Reject**, write the **Reason**, and select **Reject**. The claimant sees the reason.
To approve several at once, tick them in the list and select **Approve** with the number.

## Q: Why can't I approve this claim?
A: In Deskzo you cannot approve a claim if it is your own, if it is with HR and you do not have **Approve expense claims (HR)**, or if it was sent to another manager and you do not have **Approve or reject expense claims**.

## Q: My claim says there's nobody to route it to.
A: Deskzo shows this when you have no reporting manager and nobody else can approve claims. Ask an admin to set your manager in **Settings → Users**, or to give someone the **Approve or reject expense claims** permission. That person can then pick up your claim.

## Q: Why can't I edit my claim?
A: A submitted Deskzo claim cannot be edited while it is waiting ("A submitted claim can't be edited while it's being reviewed."). If it is rejected, select **Edit**, fix it, and select **Resubmit**. An approved or reimbursed claim is a record and cannot be changed.

## Q: How do I delete an expense claim?
A: In Deskzo you can delete only your own claim while it is a draft or rejected. Open it and select **Delete**. A submitted, approved or reimbursed claim cannot be deleted ("A claim that's been submitted or decided is a record — it can't be deleted.").

## Q: How does Accounts pay back approved claims?
A: In Deskzo, Accounts opens **Accounts → Expense claims**. **To pay** lists approved claims owed to somebody. Tick the claims paid, type the **Payment reference** (UTR or cheque number), pick **Paid on**, and select **Mark … paid**. For one claim, open it, select **Mark reimbursed**, type the **Payment reference** and select **Mark paid**. Each claimant is told. You need the **Mark expenses reimbursed** permission.

## Q: When will I get my money back?
A: In Deskzo, an approved claim with **Reimburse me for this** ticked appears for Accounts to pay. You are notified when it is approved and again when Accounts marks it reimbursed. The timing of the actual payment depends on your company's process. [POLICY: to be filled by Deskzo team]

## Q: What does "Not reimbursable" mean on a claim?
A: In Deskzo, "Not reimbursable" means **Reimburse me for this** was unticked: the company paid, for example with a company card. The claim still goes for approval, but nobody is paid back. It shows under **Company paid** on Accounts → Expense claims.

## Q: Can I see a colleague's claims?
A: In Deskzo you see your own claims and those of people who report to you. To see everyone's you need **View all company expenses**, or an admin can give your role a wider reach in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.

## Q: How do I find and total expenses?
A: On **People → Expenses** in Deskzo, search by description, company or claimant, and filter by **Status**, **Category**, **Claimant**, **Reimbursable** and **Spent on**. The totals at the top (**Total**, **Awaiting approval**, **Payable now**, **Reimbursed**) and **Where it goes** (spend by category) follow the filters.

## Q: How do I change who approves expense claims?
A: In Deskzo, go to **Settings → Expense approvals** and choose under **Expense claims are approved by**: **HR**, **The reporting manager**, or **The reporting manager, then HR**. Save. Claims already waiting keep their old route. The same page sets **A receipt is needed for claims over** an amount. You need the **Manage people records** permission.

## Q: It said the claim was approved but didn't reach the ledger.
A: With Accounting on, approving a Deskzo claim posts its cost to the ledger. If that fails, the approval still stands. The claim is listed on **Accounts → Overview** and **Accounts → Expense claims**, and someone with **Mark expenses reimbursed** can post it with **Post these now**.

## Q: I can't see Expenses in my menu.
A: Check with your admin:
1. Your company's plan must include Expenses (Deskzo Books or Deskzo One), and it must be switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
2. Your role must not have the People → Expenses section unticked in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
Everyone can claim their own expenses once the module is visible.

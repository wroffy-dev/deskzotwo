# Deskzo Overview — Deskzo

Deskzo is a business suite for growing companies. It brings sales, accounts, people and support into one workspace. A company signs up on the Deskzo website, gets its own workspace at its own web address, and switches on the Deskzo products it needs.

## Q: What is Deskzo?
A: Deskzo is online business software for growing companies. Deskzo covers sales (CRM), accounting and GST invoicing, HR and payroll, helpdesk support, inventory, projects, marketing, reports and more. Each company gets its own private Deskzo workspace. Everybody in the company signs in to that one workspace and works on the same set of records.

## Q: Who is Deskzo for?
A: Deskzo is built for small and growing businesses. Deskzo is built India-first: it supports GST invoicing, e-invoices, e-way bills, TDS, and payroll with PF, ESI and professional tax. Deskzo Books and Deskzo People follow Indian rules, so they are sold only in India. The other Deskzo products are sold worldwide.

## Q: What products does Deskzo have?
A: Deskzo has these products:
- **Deskzo One** — every Deskzo product in one workspace, on one set of records.
- **Deskzo CRM** — leads, pipeline, calls, field visits, targets and incentives.
- **Deskzo Pipeline** — a simple pipeline CRM for small teams: deals, contacts, follow-ups and bookings.
- **Deskzo Books** — GST invoicing, accounting, payables, receivables and expenses (India only).
- **Deskzo People** — HR, payroll, attendance, leave, recruitment and visitors (India only).
- **Deskzo Desk** — helpdesk tickets with SLAs, a customer portal and feedback.
- **Deskzo Inventory** — items and stock, orders, purchases and IT assets.
- **Deskzo Subscriptions** — recurring orders, renewals, add-ons and AMCs.
- **Deskzo Projects** — projects with milestones, billing stages, risks and tasks.
- **Deskzo Campaigns** — email journeys, forms and events, with consent built in.
- **Deskzo Analytics** — reports by any dimension, and the sales forecast.
- **Deskzo Vault** — shared passwords and credentials, with owners and rotation.
- **Deskzo Cards** — branded digital business cards, a QR code each, and leads from every meeting.
- **Deskzo Signatures** — company email signatures, kept in step and put into every mailbox.

## Q: What is the difference between Deskzo CRM and Deskzo Pipeline?
A: Deskzo Pipeline is a simple pipeline CRM for small teams. Deskzo Pipeline has deals on a board, companies, contacts, calls, web forms and booking pages, with a short, simple menu. Deskzo CRM has everything in Deskzo Pipeline, plus field visits, domain intel, targets, incentives, sales wins, resellers, commission parties, tasks and sticky notes.

## Q: What comes with every Deskzo workspace, whatever product I buy?
A: Every Deskzo workspace includes Companies & Contacts, the notification centre and Approvals, Tasks, Sticky Notes, Chat, Workspace saved lists, and Deskzo Automate (workflows). The Activity log, Work activity, your profile and Settings are also in every workspace.

## Q: What is a Deskzo workspace?
A: A Deskzo workspace is your company's own private space in Deskzo. Each workspace has its own web address (for example, yourcompany followed by the Deskzo domain). A workspace can also use the company's own custom domain once it is verified. Everything your company stores in Deskzo — customers, invoices, employees, tickets — lives in your workspace. Each workspace's data is kept separate from every other company's workspace.

## Q: What are Deskzo modules?
A: Each Deskzo product is made of modules. A module is one area of the app, such as Leads, Tax Invoices, Payroll or Tickets. An admin can switch modules on or off in Settings → Modules, but only for modules your plan includes. If a module is off, its links do not appear in the menu.

## Q: How is the Deskzo menu organised?
A: The Deskzo sidebar menu is grouped under headings:
- **(top)** — Dashboard and My work: Notifications, Approvals, Tasks, Calendar, Chat, Sticky Notes.
- **CRM** — Sales, Prospecting, Marketing, Support, Renewals, Partners.
- **Finance** — Accounts (items, orders, sales and purchase documents, payments, receivables, payables, accounting, GST and reports).
- **Operations** — Projects, Field Visits, IT Assets, Front desk.
- **HR** — People (My HR, people, attendance, leave, expenses, payroll, hiring and more).
- **Insights** — Reports, Performance, Activity log, Targets & incentives.
- **Workspace** — Tools (Vault, Cards, Signatures), Automate, and Administration (Settings, Users, Roles & Permissions, Security & DLP, Import & export).
You only see the sections your company's plan includes and your role allows.

## Q: Why does my Deskzo menu look different from my colleague's menu?
A: The Deskzo menu is built for each person. You see a section only when three things are true: the module is in your company's plan, an admin has switched the module on in Settings → Modules, and your role has permission to use it. Your colleague may have a different role. Also, an admin can rename words like "Leads" or "Companies" in Settings → Wording, so labels can differ between companies.

## Q: What are the main features of Deskzo CRM?

Deskzo Books is sold in India only.

## Q: What are the main features of Deskzo People?

Deskzo People is sold in India only.

## Q: What are the main features of Deskzo Desk?
A: Deskzo Desk covers: helpdesk Tickets with SLAs, a Support inbox, Support load, a Customer Portal where your customers raise and follow requests, Customer requests, and Customer feedback.

## Q: Does Deskzo have AI features?
A: Yes. Deskzo AI (the AI copilot) is a chat where you ask questions about your company's data in plain words, for example "Which of my leads are hot right now?" The AI copilot can look up records, run reports, and draft tasks, notes, new leads and a change to one lead for you to confirm. The AI copilot only sees and does what you are already allowed to. An admin switches it on in Settings → Deskzo AI, and your role needs the **Use the AI copilot** permission. Deskzo AI uses AI credits; the AI Plus add-on gives more credits each month.

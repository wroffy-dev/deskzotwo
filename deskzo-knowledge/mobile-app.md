# Deskzo app for phones — Deskzo

The Deskzo app is a phone app for Android. You pair it with your Deskzo account, and your calls with customers log themselves in Deskzo. The app also shows your day (clocking in, tasks, approvals, leave), sales (leads, companies, contacts), chat, visits and expense claims. It needs the Calls module, and the **Deskzo app** page in Settings appears only once the app is released for your workspace.

## Q: What can I do in the Deskzo app?
A: The Deskzo app has these tabs:
- **Today** — clock in and out, tasks due today, approvals waiting for you, and leave.
- **Sales** — leads, companies and contacts: search, move a lead's stage, log a call or note, call, WhatsApp or email a customer, and add a new lead, company or contact.
- **Chat** — chat with people in your company.
- **Calls** — calls to write up, callbacks, and calls you were asked to make.
- **More** — everything else you can open in Deskzo. Pages without a phone screen open as their web page.
It also has visits, expense claims with a receipt photo, and your calendar. The app works online only.

## Q: How do I sign in to the Deskzo app?
A: 1. Open the app. The screen says **Sign in to Deskzo**.
2. Type your company's Deskzo address, like "yourcompany".
3. Choose **Sign in**. You sign in on your company's own Deskzo page, as on a computer — with your password, two-factor or single sign-on.
4. Confirm the sign-in for the Deskzo app.
If you do not know your address, choose **Find my workspace**.

## Q: How do I pair my phone with a code from a computer?
A: 1. On a computer, open Deskzo and go to **Settings → Deskzo app**.
2. Choose **Show a pairing code**. A QR code and a ten-character code appear. The code works once, for ten minutes.
3. In the app, choose **Pair with a code from a computer instead**.
4. Choose **Scan the code** and point the camera at the QR, or type the code with your workspace address.
5. Choose **Pair**.
If the code ran out, choose **Show a pairing code** again.

## Q: The app says my phone is waiting for approval.
A: Your role allows only approved devices. The app says "This phone is waiting for an administrator to approve it in Deskzo (Security › Devices). Pull down to check again." Ask your admin to approve the phone, then pull down on the screen to refresh.

## Q: How do I turn on call sync?
A: Call sync logs your calls with customers in Deskzo by itself.
1. In the app, open **Calls** and choose **Set up call sync** (or go to **More → Settings**).
2. Choose **Turn on call sync** and allow the app to read the call log and phone state.
3. For calls to log while the app is closed, open battery settings, find Deskzo and choose "Don't optimise" (or "Unrestricted").
Call sync works on Android phones only.

## Q: Does the Deskzo app send all my personal calls to my company?
A: No. With call sync on, Deskzo is sent only the number, time and length of each call. Only calls with a number that belongs to a customer or contact you can see in Deskzo are kept. Your other calls go no further. Calls from before you switched call sync on are never sent.

## Q: How do I get Deskzo notifications on my phone?
A: In the app, go to **More → Settings** and under **Notifications** choose **Turn on notifications**. Then a lead given to you, something to approve, a task or a ticket's reply shows on your phone, even with the app closed.

## Q: How do I sign out of the Deskzo app?
A: In the app, go to **More → Settings** and choose **Sign out of Deskzo**. Calls stop logging from that phone until you pair it again. On a computer, you can also sign a phone out from **Settings → Deskzo app** under **Your phones**.

## Q: I can't see "Deskzo app" in Settings.
A: The **Deskzo app** page appears only when the Deskzo app is released for your workspace and the Calls module is in your company's plan and switched on in **Settings → Modules**. Ask your admin.

## Q: Is there an iPhone app?
A: The Deskzo app is for Android. On an iPhone you can use Deskzo in the browser and add it to the Home Screen (tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**) to open it like an app. Call sync works only on Android.

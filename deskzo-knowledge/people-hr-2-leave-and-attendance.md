# People (HR): leave, attendance and biometric — Deskzo

Leave and Attendance are part of People (HR), in Deskzo People (India only) and Deskzo One. Staff apply for leave and short leave, clock in and out, and ask for corrections; managers approve; HR keeps holidays, leave types, office hours, GPS office locations and biometric terminals.

## Q: How do I apply for leave?
A: To apply for leave in Deskzo:
1. Go to **People → Leave** and select **Apply for leave**.
2. Choose the **Type**. The form shows how many days you have left.
3. Pick the **From** and **To** dates. Tick **First half only** or **Second half only** for a half day at either end.
4. Check how many days will come off your balance. Weekends and company holidays inside the dates cost nothing.
5. Write a **Reason** and select **Submit**.
Your manager is notified.

## Q: How do I request time off / a day off?
A: Time off in Deskzo is a leave request. Go to **People → Leave**, select **Apply for leave**, choose the leave **Type**, pick **From** and **To** dates, write a **Reason** and select **Submit**. Your reporting manager decides it.

## Q: Why does the leave form say I don't have enough days?
A: In Deskzo, leave days still waiting for approval already count against your balance, so you cannot book the same days twice. The message reads like "That's 3 day(s) but you have 1 left of Casual Leave". Weekends and holidays do not count. If you think your balance is wrong, ask HR.

## Q: I can't apply for leave. There are no leave types.
A: Nobody can apply for leave until HR adds at least one leave type in **Settings → Holidays & leave**. Ask your HR team.

## Q: Who approves my leave?
A: Your reporting manager, anyone above them in the reporting line, or someone with the **Approve leave for anyone** permission. Nobody can approve their own leave. If you have no reporting manager, nobody is notified — ask an admin to set one in **Settings → Users**.

## Q: How do I approve or reject a leave request?
A: To decide leave in Deskzo:
1. Go to **People → Leave** and look under **Waiting on you**.
2. Add a note if you want; the person sees it.
3. Select **Approve** or **Reject**.
Approving takes the days off the balance and marks them On leave (or Half day) on the attendance grid. The person is notified either way.

## Q: Can I cancel leave that was already approved?
A: Yes, until the leave starts. Go to **People → Leave**, find the request under **Your requests** and select **Withdraw**. The days go back on your balance. Once the first day has arrived, Withdraw is not offered; you see "That leave has already started — ask HR to correct it."

## Q: What is a short leave and how do I ask for one?
A: A short leave is a couple of hours off on one working day: coming in late, leaving early or stepping out. It uses no leave balance, up to the number per month your company allows.
1. Go to **People → Leave** and select **Short leave**.
2. Select **Ask for a short leave**.
3. Pick the **Day**, the **From** and **To** times inside office hours, and write a **Reason**.
4. Send it. Your manager decides it like leave.
An approved short leave clears the Late or Left early mark it covers. HR sets the length and monthly count under **Rules** on the Short leave tab.

## Q: How do I clock in and clock out?
A: On **People → My HR** or **People → Attendance**, select **Clock in** when you start and **Clock out** when you finish. In the Deskzo mobile app, use the Today screen.

## Q: Why won't Deskzo let me clock in?
A: Common reasons:
- "You're already clocked in today."
- "You're on approved leave today. Cancel the leave first if you're working."
- "You've punched on the biometric terminal today, so clock out there: the day ends at your last punch."
- If your office checks location: "You are outside the permitted office area.", "Your location accuracy is insufficient. Please try again in an open area." or "Your location is needed to clock in or out at your office. Please allow location and try again."
If you are working somewhere else, ask for an attendance exception.

## Q: How does GPS clock-in work?
A: If HR has turned on **Require GPS to clock in and out here** for your office, Deskzo reads your phone or browser location once when you tap **Clock in** or **Clock out**. It never tracks you in the background. Allow location when asked. If you are inside the office area, you are clocked in. If not, nothing is recorded. Your office is your own branch, or the head office if you have none.

## Q: I'm working away from the office. How do I clock in?
A: Ask for an attendance exception:
1. Select **Clock in**. When it is refused, select **Ask for an exception** (in the mobile app: **Ask to clock in away from the office**).
2. Choose a reason, say what happened and send it.
3. Once it is approved, clock in again.
An approved exception counts once each way, for that day. If your office does not allow exceptions, you see "Your office doesn't allow attendance exceptions. Please contact HR."

## Q: How do I approve, grant or revoke an attendance exception?
A: Go to **People → Attendance exceptions** (needs **Approve attendance location exceptions**):
1. Waiting requests are at the top, with the reason and how far from the office the person was.
2. Select **Approve** or **Refuse**, with a note if you like.
3. To allow someone in advance, fill in **Grant an exception**: the person, clock in, out or both, the reason and the days (up to a week). Select **Grant exception**.
4. Under **History**, select **Revoke** to take back one not yet used.
Nobody can approve, grant or revoke their own exception.

## Q: How do I set up office locations for GPS attendance?
A: In **Settings → Attendance locations** (in the People group), for each office enter the **Latitude** and **Longitude** (or stand in the office and select **Use this device's location**), the **Allowed radius** (100 m to start) and the **Least accuracy accepted** (50 m). Turn on **Require GPS to clock in and out here**. Tick **Allow exceptions** so refused people can ask. You need **Manage people records**.

## Q: My attendance for a day is wrong. How do I fix it?
A: Ask for an attendance correction:
1. Go to **People → My HR**.
2. Under **Attendance corrections**, select **Ask for a correction**.
3. Fill in **Which day**, **Should have been**, the times if you know them, and **What happened**.
4. Select **Send request**.
Nothing changes until your manager approves. Leave days are not corrected here; withdraw the leave request instead.

## Q: How do I approve an attendance correction?
A: Open **People → My HR** and look under **Attendance corrections waiting on you**. Add a note if you like and select **Approve** or **Reject**. You cannot decide your own correction.

## Q: How do I check and correct the team attendance grid?
A: On **People → Attendance**:
1. Pick the month, or step with the arrows.
2. Use **Show** to narrow to **Had an absence**, **Days not recorded** or **Took leave**, and **Team** or **Person** to narrow who.
3. To correct a day, select it, choose the **Status**, add a **Note** and select **Save**.
A day covered by approved leave cannot be changed here. People with no team and no HR permission see only **My attendance**.

## Q: My attendance shows a dashed square. Was I marked absent?
A: No. In Deskzo attendance, a dashed square is a working day nobody recorded. It is not an absence and does not cause loss of pay. Ask for a correction from **People → My HR** if it should say something else.

## Q: Where can I see my own in and out times?
A: Open **People → My HR** or **People → Attendance** and find the **My in & out** card. It opens on this week. Select **Month** for the whole month and **Week** to go back. Each day shows your first punch (In), last punch (Out) and total hours. Only you see this card.

## Q: How do I set office hours for late and overtime marks?
A: On **People → Attendance**, select **Set office hours** (or **Change**). Enter when the day **Starts** and **Ends** and the **Grace (minutes)**, then **Save**. The grid then marks late, left early and overtime. **Clear office hours** turns this off. Only HR can do this.

## Q: How do I add holidays and leave types?
A: Go to **Settings → Holidays & leave** (needs **Manage people records**). For a holiday, type the name and date and select **Add**. Tick **Restricted (optional) holiday** for a day the office stays open. For a leave type, enter **Code**, **Name**, **Days a year**, when it is **Credited** (**All at once, in April** or **A twelfth each month**), whether it is **Paid**, and **Carries into next year** with a maximum. Select **Add leave type**. Check carefully: leave types cannot be edited after adding. Balances run April to March.

## Q: How do I enter opening leave balances from our old system?
A: Go to **People → Leave → Opening balances →**. For each person and leave type, type **Carried in** and **Taken before**. Or use **Paste from your old system** with lines of email, leave code, carried in, taken. Note **Where these came from** and save.

## Q: How do I connect a biometric attendance machine?
A: Deskzo supports eSSL and other ZKTeco-based terminals:
1. Go to **People → Biometric** (also in Settings as **Biometric terminals**).
2. Select **Register terminal** and enter its **Serial number** and a **Name**. The serial is on the sticker or in the terminal's Device Info menu.
3. On the terminal, set the Cloud Server / ADMS settings exactly as the Biometric page shows.
4. About a minute later, **Last seen** changes from never.
5. Under **Unmapped enrolment numbers**, choose who each number belongs to and select **Map**.

## Q: Punches from our biometric terminal are not showing.
A: Check on **People → Biometric** that the terminal is registered and enabled, and that the person's enrolment number is mapped. Unmapped punches are kept and recovered when mapped. If **Last seen** says never, the terminal is not reaching Deskzo: check its Cloud Server settings against the page and the office network. If times look hours out, fix the terminal's date and time zone, then select **Reprocess**. Days with leave or a manual correction are never overwritten.

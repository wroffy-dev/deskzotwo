# Troubleshooting: CRM, Marketing and Support — Deskzo

Common Deskzo messages and problems in Leads, Companies, Contacts, Booking pages, Forecast, Marketing, Forms & events, Workspace lists, Field Visits, Resellers, Tickets, the Customer Portal, Customer feedback and the Customer inbox.

## Q: Deskzo says "Before Proposal sent, fill in Value and Expected close date." when I move a lead.
A: Your Deskzo pipeline has a stage rule. A lead must have certain fields filled before it can enter that stage. Edit the lead, fill in the fields the message names, then move the lead again. If the message says to issue a quotation, raise the quotation first.

## Q: Deskzo says "You can't assign leads to other people."
A: Your Deskzo role does not have the **Assign leads to others** permission. Leave the Salesperson empty so the lead assignment rules choose someone, or ask your admin for the permission.

## Q: Deskzo says "Some of the selected leads are not yours to change."
A: You chose leads that belong to other people, and your Deskzo role does not have **Reassign accounts and leads**. Untick those leads and try again, or ask a manager to change them.

## Q: Deskzo says "A company with this name already exists."
A: Deskzo company names are unique across the whole workspace, including vendors and resellers. Do not create a duplicate. Search for the company. If you cannot see it, ask your admin for access to the existing company.

## Q: Deskzo says "This contact is linked to a lead and can't be deleted."
A: A Deskzo contact linked to a lead is kept. Instead, mark the contact as left on the company's Contacts tab.

## Q: My customer sees "That time has just been taken. Choose another." on a Deskzo booking page.
A: Someone else booked that slot, or the host became busy, while your customer was choosing. Your customer should pick another time on the Deskzo booking page.

## Q: My Deskzo booking page says "No times are free just now".
A: All the hours on the Deskzo booking page are taken, the notice period is too long, or every host is busy. Widen the hours, add more hosts, or shorten the minimum notice on the booking page.

## Q: Deskzo says "That month is over — its commit stays as it was made." in Forecast.
A: In Deskzo Forecast, a commit for a past month is locked on purpose. You can only change commits for the current and future months.

## Q: Deskzo says "Somebody else has to approve a campaign you built."
A: The Deskzo marketing campaign is big enough to need approval, and you cannot approve your own campaign. Another person with the **Approve a large send** permission must approve it. At least two people need that permission.

## Q: Deskzo says "That CSV is over 5 MB — split it into smaller lists."
A: A Deskzo mailing list upload can be up to 5 MB. Split your CSV file into smaller files and upload them as separate lists.

## Q: Why didn't some people get my Deskzo marketing email?
A: Deskzo holds back recipients when a check fails, for example no consent for the topic, they unsubscribed, they are on the suppression list, or they have an overdue invoice. Deskzo also sends no marketing email during quiet hours. Open the campaign report to see who was held back and why.

## Q: I can't remove someone from the Deskzo suppression list. It says "They asked us to stop."
A: People who unsubscribed or complained cannot be removed from the Deskzo suppression list by hand. If they want emails again, they choose topics again on their own preferences page.

## Q: Deskzo says a form "can't be deleted. Close it instead."
A: A Deskzo form that has answers, or for which invitations went out, cannot be deleted. Use **Close** instead. The answers are kept.

## Q: Deskzo says "The event is full. Raise the seat limit first if there is room."
A: The Deskzo event form has reached its seat limit. If you have space, raise **Seats** on the form.

## Q: Deskzo says "This list belongs to someone else. Duplicate it to make your own version."
A: You cannot change another person's Deskzo Workspace list. Choose **Duplicate** to make your own copy, or ask someone with the **Act on anyone's saved list** permission.

## Q: Deskzo won't let me delete a field visit.
A: A completed Deskzo field visit is a record: cancel it instead of deleting it. If the message says "Expenses are claimed against this visit", remove those expense claims first.

## Q: Deskzo says "This company has no website on file."
A: Deskzo Domain Intel needs a website to look up. Add the **Website** on the company, then choose **Look up** again.

## Q: Deskzo says "Change the credit limit from the Credit tab" on a reseller.
A: A Deskzo reseller's credit limit is set on the reseller's **Credit** tab, not in onboarding. You need the **Override credit terms & limits** permission and must give a reason.

## Q: Deskzo says "Tickets can only be assigned to someone in the Tech Support department."
A: The person is not in a Deskzo department that can hold tickets. An admin ticks **Ticket assignment** for that department in Settings → Departments, or moves the person into such a department.

## Q: Deskzo says "Only the workspace's super admin can delete tickets."
A: In Deskzo, only the super admin can delete a ticket. Ask the super admin, or close the ticket instead.

## Q: A customer email did not create a Deskzo ticket. Why?
A: Possible reasons: the sender is not a known contact (the email waits in **Support → Support inbox**), it was an automatic reply, bounce or newsletter, or more than 30 emails an hour came from one address. Check the Support inbox, and check Settings → Support email for when the last email arrived.

## Q: My customer sees "This link is no longer valid." on the Deskzo customer portal.
A: The Deskzo portal link may have expired or been revoked, the portal may be off, the customer may not have portal access, or the contact may have left. Open the customer's **Portal** tab, check access, and create a new link.

## Q: My customer sees "This feedback link is no longer open."
A: The Deskzo feedback link expired, was withdrawn, or was already answered. Make a new link from the customer's **Feedback** tab.

## Q: Deskzo says "It's over 24 hours since they last wrote, so WhatsApp only allows an approved template now."
A: WhatsApp only allows free replies within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that, send a Meta-approved WhatsApp template from the Deskzo Customer inbox.

## Q: Deskzo says "Link it to a customer first" when I make a ticket from an inbox conversation.
A: A Deskzo ticket needs a customer. Choose **Make lead** first, or the conversation links itself once the customer's email or number is on file.

## Q: Deskzo says "Files can be up to 5 MB." in the Customer inbox.
A: Files sent in the Deskzo Customer inbox can be up to 5 MB. Send a smaller file.

# Workspace Lists and Contact Checks — Deskzo

Workspace lets you build saved lists of companies from filters, share them, assign them, and turn them into calling activities. Contact Checks is where you review the contact corrections callers report. Workspace is included in every Deskzo plan; calling activities need the Calls module (Deskzo CRM, Deskzo Pipeline or Deskzo One).

## Q: What is Workspace in Deskzo?
A: Workspace is in the menu at Prospecting → Workspace. A Workspace list is a set of filters with a name, for example "a territory", "renewals next month" or "not called in 90 days". Each time you open the list, Deskzo runs the filters again. So the list always shows the companies that match today.

## Q: I can't see Workspace or Contact Checks in my menu. Why?
A: Ask your admin to check:
1. The Workspace module is switched on in Settings → Modules.
2. Your role has the **Prospecting › Workspace** section ticked in Settings → Roles & Permissions.
3. For Contact Checks, your role also needs the **View contacts** permission.

## Q: How do I create a saved list in Workspace?
A: 
1. Go to Prospecting → Workspace and click **New list**.
2. Choose filters in the groups: **Who they are**, **Who owns them**, **What they've bought**, **Products & brands**, **Renewals**, **Pipeline**, **Engagement** and **What their domain says**. The count at the top shows how many companies match.
3. Enter a **List name** and, if useful, **What it's for**.
4. Leave **Everyone can see this list** ticked to share it. Untick it to keep it private.
5. Click **Save list**. Nothing is saved until you do.

## Q: How do I build a cross-sell list of companies that never bought a product?
A: In the Workspace list editor, open **Products & brands** and choose the products or brands. Then tick **Flip this: companies who have NEVER bought the above**. The list now shows companies that never bought them. Click **Save list**.

## Q: Why does my colleague's list show more companies than mine?
A: A Workspace list only shows companies the person opening it can see. Without the **See every account, not just your own** permission, you see only the accounts you and your team manage. So the same filters match fewer companies for you.

## Q: How do I open and work a Workspace list?
A: 
1. Go to Prospecting → Workspace. Lists assigned to you are at the top, under **Assigned to you**.
2. Open a list. Its filters show as badges under the name.
3. Use **Search within this list…** to find a company, or switch to **Split** to open each company beside the list.

## Q: How do I assign a Workspace list to people?
A: 
1. Open the list and click **Assign** (or **Change**).
2. Pick the people who should work it.
3. Fill in **What should they do with it?** if you like, and click **Assign**.
Each new person gets a notification. Only the list's builder can assign it, unless you have the **Act on anyone's saved list** permission.

## Q: Why can't I edit a list someone else built?
A: A shared Workspace list belongs to the person who built it. You see "This list belongs to someone else. Duplicate it to make your own version." Click **Duplicate** to make your own copy and change that. Or ask someone with the **Act on anyone's saved list** permission.

## Q: How do I change, copy or delete a Workspace list?
A: Open the list:
- **Edit filters** opens the editor. Click **Save changes** when done.
- **Duplicate** makes your own copy.
- **Delete** (in the editor) removes the saved list. The companies are not affected.

## Q: What is a calling activity?
A: A calling activity turns a Workspace list into work for callers. The companies that match at that moment are frozen into a fixed set. They are shared out between the callers. Each caller gets their own queue and a task. Calling activities need the Calls module and the **View calls** permission.

## Q: How do I start a calling activity from a list?
A: You must be the list's builder, or have **Act on anyone's saved list**.
1. Open the list and click **Start calling activity**.
2. Under **Who is calling?**, pick the callers.
3. Choose **How should it be split?**: **Round robin** (one each in turn), **Equal blocks** (each caller works one stretch), or **To the account owner** (each company goes to its owner; unowned ones are shared round robin).
4. Set **Finish by** and write a **Brief**.
5. Click **Start with** and the number of callers.
After this, the list shows progress for each caller instead of companies.

## Q: I don't see Start calling activity. Why?
A: Only the list's builder, or someone with **Act on anyone's saved list**, can start one. Your company also needs the Calls module, and you need **View calls**. A list that is already a calling activity shows **Open my queue** instead.

## Q: How do I work my calling queue?
A: 
1. Open the calling activity and click **Open my queue**. You see only companies given to you.
2. Call the contact. Beside each phone number and email, click **Right** or **Wrong**.
3. For a wrong detail, type the correct value and click **Save correction**. Or click **Just wrong** if you don't know the right one.
4. Fill in **How did it go?** and click **Done — next**, or **Skip**.
Finishing your last company closes your task.

## Q: My calling queue is empty. Why?
A: Nothing in that calling activity was given to you. The person who started it chose the callers and how to split the companies.

## Q: Does marking a number wrong change the contact?
A: No. Marking a detail wrong sends it to Prospecting → Contact Checks. Nothing changes on the contact until someone applies the correction there.

## Q: What is Contact Checks?
A: Contact Checks is at Prospecting → Contact Checks. It shows contact details that callers reported as wrong, with any corrections. It also shows email health: addresses that won't deliver or only reach a shared inbox. You need the **View contacts** permission. You only see checks for accounts whose contacts you can see.

## Q: How do I review contact corrections?
A: 
1. Go to Prospecting → Contact Checks.
2. Under **Reported on calls** or **With a correction**, compare the old value with the new one.
3. Click **Apply** to put the correction on the contact, **Reject** to turn it down, or **Acknowledge** for a detail reported wrong with no new value.
An email correction you apply counts as verified straight away.

## Q: How do I check bad email addresses in Contact Checks?
A: Go to Prospecting → Contact Checks and open **Email health**. It lists addresses that won't deliver or only reach a shared inbox. Fix them on the contact. Click **Re-check this page** to check them again.

## Q: Can I export saved lists?
A: Yes, people with the **Export customer data** permission can download saved lists from Settings → Import & export. No role has this permission by default; admins do.

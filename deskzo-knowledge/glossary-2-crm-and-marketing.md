# Glossary: CRM and Marketing Terms — Deskzo

Plain definitions of Deskzo words used in Leads, Companies, Calls, Wins, Forecast, Booking pages, Marketing, Prospecting and Partners.

## Q: What is a lead in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo lead is a real requirement from a company: what they want, its rough value and when it may close. Leads move through a pipeline on a board.

## Q: What is a pipeline in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo pipeline is the set of lead stages a team works through. A workspace can have several team pipelines.

## Q: What does "Counts as" mean on a Deskzo pipeline stage?
A: "Counts as" is the fixed meaning behind each custom Deskzo stage name: New, Contacted, Qualifying, Qualified, Proposal sent, Negotiation, Won, Lost or Disqualified.

## Q: What does "Counts as sales" mean on a Deskzo pipeline?
A: "Counts as sales" is a Deskzo pipeline setting. Won leads in that pipeline count towards targets, wins and the forecast, and make the company a customer.

## Q: What is a lead score in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo lead score is a 0–100 score on open leads, graded Hot, Warm or Cold, with each line of the score explained.

## Q: What are lead assignment rules in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo lead assignment rules decide who gets a new lead that nobody assigned, for example round robin, fewest open leads, a named person, or the account manager.

## Q: What does Prospect or Awaiting Order mean for a Deskzo company?
A: These are Deskzo company stages. Prospect means the company never had a lead. Awaiting Order means it won a deal but has no order yet.

## Q: What is an account manager in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo account manager is the person who looks after a company. The account manager decides who sees the account.

## Q: What is a caller in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo caller is the person who works a company for lead generation by phone.

## Q: What is the Mail log in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo Mail log lists every email Deskzo sent to customers, with its delivery status.

## Q: What is the Contacts Library in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo Contacts Library is one list of every contact at every company you can see, under Sales → Contacts.

## Q: What is a callback owed in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo callback owed is a promised call-back time logged on a call. It becomes a task with a reminder.

## Q: What is a connected call in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo connected call is a call with the outcome Connected, Callback requested or Not interested.

## Q: What is the Wins wall in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo Wins wall is Sales → Wins & leaderboard: this month's bookings, wins and the sales leaderboard.

## Q: What is "Most active of the fortnight" in Deskzo?
A: "Most active of the fortnight" is a Deskzo award given on the 1st and 16th of each month to the most active people, based on work that can be checked.

## Q: What is the Hall of fame in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo Hall of fame lists past top sellers and most-active winners with their prizes.

## Q: What is a weighted forecast in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo weighted forecast is each open deal's value multiplied by the learned win rate of its stage, added up.

## Q: What is a commit in Deskzo Forecast?
A: A Deskzo commit is a salesperson's own figure for what they will close in a month. Past months' commits are locked.

## Q: What is a booking page in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo booking page is a public page where customers book a meeting in your team's free time. Each booking opens a lead. The team members customers can meet are called hosts.

## Q: What is a campaign in Deskzo Marketing?
A: A Deskzo campaign is one marketing email (a template) sent once to an audience, a mailing list, or both.

## Q: What is a journey in Deskzo Marketing?
A: A Deskzo journey is a series of automatic steps, such as emails or tasks, that starts when something happens to a customer.

## Q: What is an audience in Deskzo Marketing?
A: A Deskzo audience is a saved set of company filters, plus rules about which people at each company get marketing email.

## Q: What is a mailing list in Deskzo Marketing?
A: A Deskzo mailing list is a set of people uploaded from a CSV file, with a record of how they agreed to hear from you.

## Q: What is the suppression list in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo suppression list holds the people, addresses or domains that must not get marketing email, and why.

## Q: What is a topic in Deskzo Marketing?
A: A Deskzo topic is what a marketing email or form is about, for example Newsletter. A contact only gets marketing on topics they agreed to.

## Q: What do "held back" and "quiet hours" mean in Deskzo Marketing?
A: "Held back" means Deskzo did not send to a recipient because a check failed, such as no consent or an overdue invoice. Quiet hours are the times of day when Deskzo sends no marketing email (by default 8:00 pm to 9:00 am).

## Q: What is a Workspace list (saved list) in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo Workspace list is a named set of company filters that runs again each time it is opened. A calling activity freezes a list into a fixed set of companies shared out between callers.

## Q: What are Contact Checks in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo Contact Checks is where corrections reported by callers, and bad email addresses, are reviewed and applied.

## Q: What is Domain Intel in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo Domain Intel looks up a company's public domain records and shows its email provider, spoofing protection, website platform and host. "Spoofable" means the domain has no DMARC record.

## Q: What are a reseller, end customer and commission party in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo reseller is a channel partner that buys from you to sell to its own customers. An end customer is a reseller's customer, kept out of marketing. A commission party is an agent, broker or referral partner you pay commission to.

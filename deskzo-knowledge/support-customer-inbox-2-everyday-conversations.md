# Customer Inbox Conversations — Deskzo

The Customer inbox in Deskzo brings website chat and WhatsApp messages into one inbox for your team. This guide covers replying, assigning, closing, tagging, making leads and tickets from conversations, saved replies, office hours and Inbox reports. The Customer inbox is an add-on, already included in Deskzo One.

## Q: I can't see Customer inbox in my menu. Why?
A: The Customer inbox shows in the menu only when all of these are true:
1. Your company's plan includes the Customer inbox add-on (or Deskzo One).
2. The Customer inbox module is switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. Your role has the **Answer customer conversations** permission, and the **Support › Customer inbox** section is not hidden for your role.
Ask your admin to check **Settings → Modules** and **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.

## Q: How do I reply to a customer in the Customer inbox?
A: To reply:
1. Open **Support → Customer inbox** and choose a conversation.
2. Make sure the **Reply** tab is selected.
3. Type your message. Type **/** to pick a saved reply.
4. Select **Send** (or press Enter).
When you reply, the bot steps aside. If nobody had the conversation, it becomes yours. Messages can be up to 2,000 characters.

## Q: Why can't I send a normal WhatsApp reply?
A: WhatsApp allows free replies only for 24 hours after the customer's last message. After that, the Customer inbox says "It's over 24 hours since they last wrote, so WhatsApp only takes an approved template now." Type the **Template name** exactly as Meta has it, fill in **Its values, one a line**, and select **Send template**. A template must be approved by Meta first.

## Q: How do I write a private note to my team on a conversation?
A: Open the conversation and select the **Note** tab. Type your note ("A note only your team sees"). Type **@** and a name to tell a colleague. Select **Add note**. The customer never sees notes.

## Q: How do I take or pass on a conversation?
A: Open the conversation. Under **Who has it**, select **Take it** to make it yours, or choose a colleague to give it to them. Choose **In the queue** to put it back for anyone. The person you give it to gets a notification. If Deskzo says "They can't take customer conversations here.", that person lacks the inbox permission or cannot see that channel.

## Q: What are Mine, Unassigned, All open and Closed?
A: These tabs filter the Customer inbox list. **Mine** shows conversations you have. **Unassigned** shows conversations waiting in the queue. **All open** shows every open conversation you can see. **Closed** shows closed ones. You can also search by name, phone or email, and filter by channel or tag.

## Q: What do Available and Away mean in the Customer inbox?
A: The button at the top of the Customer inbox list shows **Available** or **Away**. When a channel gives conversations to each person in turn (round-robin), people set to **Away** are passed over. People not active in Deskzo in the last 10 minutes are also passed over.

## Q: How are new conversations given to people?
A: In **Settings → Customer inbox**, each shared channel has **Who gets a conversation that needs a person**:
- **The queue — whoever takes it first**.
- **Each person in turn (round-robin)**: you can tick who takes turns, or leave every box empty for everybody who answers customers.
A conversation on a rep's own WhatsApp number goes to that rep.

## Q: How do I close or reopen a conversation?
A: Open the conversation and select **Close**. For a closed conversation, select **Reopen**. If the customer writes again, the conversation opens again by itself. When **Ask "How did we do?"** is on for the channel, the customer is asked to rate the conversation when the team closes one it answered.

## Q: How do I make a lead from a conversation?
A: Open the conversation and select **Make lead** in the customer panel. You need access to Leads (the **View leads** permission). Deskzo can also make leads automatically: in **Settings → Customer inbox**, tick **Make a lead from a new customer automatically, once we have:** and choose the details needed. Customers already on file link to their record instead.

## Q: How do I make a ticket from a conversation?
A: Open the conversation and select **Make ticket**. Check the **Ticket title** and select **Make the ticket**. The ticket keeps what the customer said. You need the Helpdesk and the **Create tickets** permission. The conversation must be linked to a customer first; otherwise Deskzo says "Link it to a customer first — make a lead, or it links itself when their email or number is on file."

## Q: What are saved replies?
A: Saved replies are answers your team gives often. An admin adds them in **Settings → Customer inbox** under **Saved replies** with **Add a saved reply**: a **Shortcut**, **What it's for** and **The reply**. In the inbox, type **/** and the shortcut to insert one, then edit before sending. {name} becomes the customer's first name and {agent} becomes yours.

## Q: How do I tag a conversation?
A: Open the conversation and use **Tags** in the customer panel. Type a tag and press Enter. A conversation can have up to 10 tags. Filter the list by tag with the tag box above the list.

## Q: How do I send a file in the Customer inbox?
A: In the **Reply** tab, select the paperclip (**Send a photo or file**). Files can be photos, PDFs, and Word, Excel or text files, up to 5 MB each. This works only when **The team can send photos and documents** is on for the channel. A larger file shows "Files can be up to 5 MB."

## Q: How do I block a spam customer?
A: Open the conversation and select **Block this number** (WhatsApp) or **Block this visitor** (website). Add a reason under **Why (for the team)** and select **Block**. Their messages no longer come in and the conversation closes. To undo, select **Unblock them**, or use **Blocked** in **Settings → Customer inbox**.

## Q: Can I set office hours for the Customer inbox?
A: Yes. In **Settings → Customer inbox**, open a channel and use **Office hours**. Tick **Outside these hours, tell customers waiting for a person when you'll be back**, set each day's hours, and write the **Away message**. Hours use the workspace's time zone from **Settings → Company**.

## Q: What other website chat settings are there?
A: For a website chat channel in **Settings → Customer inbox**, you can also:
- **Show the team what a visitor is typing, before they send it**.
- **Show a short form before the chat starts** (name, company name, email, phone: Required, Optional or Don't ask).
- **Email a reply to a visitor who has left the website, when they gave an email**.
- **Say hello first**: a message beside the bubble after a visitor spends some seconds on a chosen page (up to 5 messages).

## Q: What does Inbox reports show?
A: **Support → Inbox reports** shows how quickly customers hear from a person, over 7, 30 or 90 days: **First response (median)**, **Answered within an hour**, **Every reply (median wait)**, **Waiting for a person now**, **Conversations**, **The bot handled alone**, **Leads made** and **Customers satisfied**. It also shows day-by-day and by-channel tables. Time with the bot is not counted against the team. The **By person** table shows only to people with **See every customer conversation**.

## Q: Will I be notified about new customer messages?
A: Yes, in the app. You are told when a customer starts a conversation on your own WhatsApp number, writes in a conversation you have, or somebody gives you a conversation. The menu also shows a count of waiting messages. You can switch on the sound "A new customer chat" in your notification preferences. Each person can switch notifications off in **Settings → Notification preferences**.

# Glossary: Finance, Orders and Inventory Terms — Deskzo

Plain definitions of Deskzo words used in Orders, Items & Inventory, Sales and Purchase Documents, Payments, Receivables, Payables, Accounting and Revenue & Close.

## Q: What does "punch an order" mean in Deskzo?
A: To punch an order in Deskzo means to enter a new customer order. A punched order then waits for accounts approval.

## Q: What is a held order (in hand) in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo held order is an order that was punched but not yet sent to purchase. The purchase hand-off can be now, held, or on a scheduled date.

## Q: What is the distributor price in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo distributor price is the price a distributor quoted to sales. Purchase savings are measured against it.

## Q: What are purchase savings in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo purchase savings are how much purchase bought below the distributor price, multiplied by the quantity.

## Q: What is a deal registration in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo deal registration is a special price from an OEM, registered for one specific deal.

## Q: What is a negative call in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo negative call is an order sold below cost. It needs the **Approve orders sold below cost** permission.

## Q: What is a backend rebate in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo backend rebate is money a distributor or OEM pays back after the sale, as a credit note or bank payment. A rebate programme is a standing rule for these rebates.

## Q: What is a vendor credit in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo vendor credit is a credit note or rebate payout from a distributor or OEM, posted to the books and set against rebates or bills.

## Q: What is a stock voucher in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo stock voucher is a document that moves stock: a goods receipt, transfer, adjustment, physical count or purchase return.

## Q: What is a GRN in Deskzo?
A: A GRN (goods receipt) in Deskzo is a stock voucher that records goods received from a vendor, usually against a purchase order.

## Q: What is a proforma invoice in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo proforma invoice is a request for payment before supply. It is not a tax invoice.

## Q: What is a delivery challan in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo delivery challan is paperwork for goods that move but are not being sold. It carries no tax.

## Q: What is an IRN in Deskzo?
A: An IRN (Invoice Reference Number) is the number India's government e-invoice portal gives a tax invoice, credit note or debit note. Deskzo gets it when you e-invoice a document.

## Q: What is an e-way bill in Deskzo?
A: An e-way bill is India's permit for moving goods. In Deskzo it is raised from a tax invoice, credit note or delivery challan.

## Q: What is place of supply in Deskzo?
A: Place of supply is the state where a supply happens. In Deskzo it decides whether a document charges CGST and SGST, or IGST.

## Q: What is a lump sum payment in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo lump sum payment is a customer payment recorded without an order, allocated to orders or invoices later. Un-apply removes an allocation so the money is unallocated again.

## Q: What is vendor statement reconciliation in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo reconciliation checks a distributor's statement against your orders for differences in seats, price and billing.

## Q: What is receivables ageing in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo receivables ageing groups what customers owe by how late it is: Not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days.

## Q: What is a customer credit rating in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo customer credit rating (Reliable, Fair, Risky or New) is based on how the customer actually paid. It suggests payment terms and a credit limit.

## Q: What is a credit hold in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo credit hold makes new orders from a customer with a long-overdue invoice wait for approval by someone who can override credit.

## Q: What is a promise to pay in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo promise to pay is a date and amount a customer said they would pay, logged with a follow-up. A passed, unpaid promise is called broken.

## Q: What is DSO in Deskzo?
A: DSO (days sales outstanding) in Deskzo compares what is owed with what was invoiced in the last three months, shown in days.

## Q: What is a voucher in Deskzo Accounting?
A: A Deskzo voucher (Payment, Receipt, Contra or Journal) is a hand-written accounting entry. A draft voucher changes nothing until it is posted.

## Q: What is a reversal in Deskzo Accounting?
A: A Deskzo reversal is an opposite, dated entry that cancels a posted entry. The original entry stays visible.

## Q: What is a period lock in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo period lock is a date on or before which nothing can be posted or changed.

## Q: What is the year-end close in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo year-end close moves income and expense balances to Retained Earnings and locks the books to 31 March.

## Q: What is a revenue schedule in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo revenue schedule is the plan for moving an invoice line's revenue from Deferred Revenue into Sales, month by month. It is part of the Revenue & Close add-on.

## Q: What is the Revenue Waterfall in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo Revenue Waterfall shows the revenue still to be recognised, by customer or item, over the next 12 or 24 months.

## Q: What is a prepaid or accrual schedule in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo prepaid or accrual schedule spreads a cost over the months it belongs to: paid ahead (prepaid), or incurred before the bill (accrual).

## Q: What is flux in Deskzo Month-end Close?
A: Flux is a large month-on-month movement in an account. In Deskzo Month-end Close, it needs a written explanation before the month can close.

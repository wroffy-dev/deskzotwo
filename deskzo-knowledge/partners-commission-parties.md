# Commission Parties — Deskzo

Commission Parties are the agents, brokers and referral partners you pay commission to for bringing you business. Deskzo keeps them in their own list, links them to the customers they bring, stores the accounts they are paid into, and shows what they have earned. Commission Parties is part of Deskzo CRM and Deskzo One.

## Q: What is a commission party in Deskzo?
A: A commission party is someone you pay for bringing you business, such as an agent, a broker or a referral partner. They are kept apart from vendors (you don't buy from them) and from customers (you don't sell to them). You find them at Partners → Commission Parties.

## Q: What can I do with a commission party?
A: For each commission party you can:
- move them through onboarding (Onboarding, Active, Inactive);
- link them to the customer companies they refer;
- keep the accounts they want to be paid into, with one default;
- see the commission paid to them, order by order.

## Q: I can't see Commission Parties in my menu. Why?
A: Ask your admin to check:
1. Your company's plan includes Commission Parties (Deskzo CRM or Deskzo One).
2. The Commission Parties module is switched on in Settings → Modules.
3. Your role has the **Partners › Commission Parties** section ticked in Settings → Roles & Permissions.

## Q: Which permissions does Commission Parties use?
A: 
- **See every account, not just your own**: see every commission party, not only those you or your team manage.
- **View orders**: see the orders behind commission. Without it, commission lists leave those orders out.
- **Reassign accounts and leads**: assign commission parties to anyone from the list.
- **Export customer data**: download commission parties from Settings → Import & export.

## Q: How do I add a commission party?
A: 
1. Go to Partners → Commission Parties and click **New commission party**.
2. Fill in their name, details, address and the person you deal with.
3. Click **Save company**.
They start as **Onboarding**.

## Q: How do I add a referral partner or agent?
A: Referral partners, agents and brokers are added as commission parties. Go to Partners → Commission Parties, click **New commission party**, fill in their details, and click **Save company**. Then add their payee accounts and link the customers they referred.

## Q: How do I record the bank account a commission party is paid into?
A: 
1. Open the commission party. On the **Details** tab, under **Related parties (payee accounts)**, click **+ Add related party**.
2. Fill in **Label**, **Account holder name**, **PAN**, **UPI ID**, **Bank account number**, **IFSC** and **Bank name**, as needed.
3. Tick **Default account for this commission party** for the main account.
4. Click **Save**.
Add one entry per account. An account can belong to the party, a firm or a family member.

## Q: How do I change the default payee account?
A: Open the commission party. Under **Related parties (payee accounts)**, edit the account you want and tick **Default account for this commission party**. Ticking it on one account removes it from the others.

## Q: Why can't I delete a payee account?
A: A commission payment has already been recorded against that account. Deleting it would erase how that commission was paid, so Deskzo does not allow it.

## Q: How do I link a commission party to a customer?
A: 
1. Open the commission party's **Details** tab.
2. Under **Linked companies**, type to search customer companies.
3. Pick the customer and click **Link**. Click **Unlink** to remove it.
You can also do this from the customer's **Commission** tab.

## Q: Why can't I link a commission party to this company?
A: Only customer companies can be linked. Vendors, resellers and other commission parties cannot. Deskzo shows "A commission party can only be linked to a customer company."

## Q: How do I see what a commission party has earned?
A: Open the commission party. On the **Details** tab, **Commission earned** lists every commission payment: the **Customer**, the **Order**, which account it went **Into**, the **Amount** and when it was **Recorded**.

## Q: How do I record commission paid to an agent?
A: Commission is recorded as an expense on an order, when the order is created. See the Orders guide. After that, it appears under **Commission earned** on the commission party's page and on the customer's **Commission** tab.

## Q: Commission earned is empty, but we have paid them. Why?
A: Commission shows only after it is recorded as an expense on an order. Also, orders you are not allowed to open are left out. A colleague with more access, or with the **View orders** permission, may see more than you.

## Q: How do I see the commission paid on a customer account?
A: Open the customer and go to its **Commission** tab. **Commission paid on this account** shows the total, a badge for each commission party, and each payment with the order, who was paid, the account and any notes.

## Q: How do I change a commission party's onboarding status?
A: For one party, use the status control at the top of its page: **Onboarding**, **Active** or **Inactive**. For several, tick them on the Partners → Commission Parties list, pick a status in **Onboarding — no change**, and click **Apply**. The same bar can assign them (**Assign to…**) and add tags.

## Q: Can I give a commission party a reference code?
A: Yes. On the commission party's page, click **Set vendor code** and type the code. It shows as the **Reference code** on the Details tab. It can be up to 30 characters and must be unique.

## Q: Can I turn a vendor or customer into a commission party?
A: No. A company cannot move between the customer, reseller, vendor and commission party modules. Create the commission party in Partners → Commission Parties instead.

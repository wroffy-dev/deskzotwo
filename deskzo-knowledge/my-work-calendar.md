# Calendar — Deskzo

Calendar is your diary inside Deskzo. It can show your own Outlook, Google or Zoho calendar, or Deskzo's own calendar, together with your due tasks, planned visits, approved leave, holidays and bookings. Calendar is part of every Deskzo product and sits in the sidebar under My work → Calendar.

## Q: What is the Calendar in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo Calendar is your diary inside the app. You can connect your own Outlook calendar, Google Calendar or Zoho Calendar, and its meetings show in Deskzo, kept in step. Meetings you schedule from Deskzo go into that calendar. Next to your meetings, the Deskzo Calendar also shows (read-only) your due tasks, planned visits, approved leave, company holidays and bookings made with you on a booking page. Each one shows only if that module is open to you.

## Q: Do I have to connect Outlook or Google to use the Calendar?
A: No. Without a connected calendar, Deskzo keeps your meetings in its own calendar. Deskzo emails the invitations for you with the meeting attached, so the meeting lands in each guest's own calendar. Replies come back to you.

## Q: How do I connect my Outlook, Google or Zoho calendar to Deskzo?
A: To connect your calendar:
1. Click your name and open **My profile**.
2. In **Your mailbox and calendar**, click **Connect Outlook** (or the Gmail or Zoho Mail option).
3. Sign in and allow the calendar when asked.
Your mail and calendar connect together. The Calendar page also has a **Connect** button. Before anyone can connect, an admin must set up Microsoft 365, Google Workspace or Zoho in the **Email & sign-in** card of **Settings → Security & data protection**.

## Q: How do I schedule a meeting?
A: To schedule a meeting in Deskzo:
1. Click **Schedule meeting** on **My work → Calendar**, or on a lead, customer or ticket page. You can also use **+ Meeting** in a record's **Meetings** card, or the calendar button next to a contact.
2. Type a **Title**. Pick when it **Starts** and its **Length**.
3. If your calendar is connected, leave the online meeting box ticked (for example "Add a Teams link") to add a Teams, Google Meet or Zoho Meeting link.
4. Under **Who's invited**, tick the customer's people, add colleagues with **Invite a colleague…**, or type an email address and press Enter.
5. Check **Busy that day** to see your busy times and each invited colleague's busy times.
6. Fill in **Where** and an **Agenda** if you like.
7. Click **Schedule and invite**.

## Q: How do I book a meeting with a customer from a lead?
A: Open the lead in Deskzo and click **Schedule meeting**, or **+ Meeting** in the lead's **Meetings** card. Fill in the **Title**, **Starts** and **Length**, tick the customer's contacts under **Who's invited**, and click **Schedule and invite**. The meeting stays listed on the lead. After the meeting has ended, Deskzo adds it to the lead's timeline as a meeting.

## Q: How many people can I invite to a meeting?
A: You can invite up to 50 people to one Deskzo meeting. For a customer that belongs to a reseller, only colleagues can be invited from Deskzo. When invitations go through Deskzo's own mail, the meeting dialog shows how many more people can be invited today.

## Q: How do I reschedule or cancel a meeting?
A: To move or cancel a meeting you organised in Deskzo:
1. Open the meeting on **My work → Calendar**, or find it in the record's **Meetings** card.
2. To move it, click **Reschedule**, change the time, and click **Save and tell everyone**.
3. To cancel it, click **Cancel meeting**, add **A line for them (optional)**, and confirm.
Everyone invited is told either way.

## Q: Why can't I schedule a meeting?
A: Scheduling or rescheduling a meeting in Deskzo needs the **Schedule meetings** permission. Most built-in roles (Profiling, Calling, Sales, Support, Management, Accounts and Purchase) have it by default. Without it, you can still see your own calendar and cancel meetings you already booked. Ask your admin to add the permission to your role in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.

## Q: Can my colleagues see what is in my calendar?
A: No. Each person in Deskzo sees only their own calendar. When somebody picks a time for a meeting, they see only that you are busy. They never see the meeting title or who it is with. For a colleague with no connected calendar, only their meetings in Deskzo's own calendar count as busy.

## Q: How do I see a colleague's free time when booking a meeting?
A: In the Deskzo **Schedule meeting** dialog, invite the colleague with **Invite a colleague…**. Then check **Busy that day**. It lays out your busy times and each invited colleague's busy times against the slot you picked. You see only busy times, never meeting details.

## Q: How do I move around the calendar?
A: Open **My work → Calendar**. Choose **Week** to see a week at a time (on a phone, one day at a time down the page) or **Agenda** to see the next two weeks as a list. Use the arrows and **Today** to move. Click anything to see its details, and **Join the meeting** if it has a link. With a calendar connected, **Sync** updates it straight away. **Print** opens a printable page of the days shown.

## Q: How do I see my Deskzo calendar on my phone?
A: To subscribe to your Deskzo calendar:
1. On **My work → Calendar**, click **Subscribe**, then **Make my link**.
2. Copy the address straight away. It is shown only once.
3. On an iPhone or Mac, use **open it to subscribe**. In Google Calendar, add it under Other calendars → From URL. In Outlook, use Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
4. Tick **Show each meeting's title** if you want titles. If it is off, your phone shows only when you are busy.
The link shows meetings in Deskzo's own calendar. Keep the link private: anyone with the address sees what it shows.

## Q: How do I stop or replace my calendar subscribe link?
A: In Deskzo, go to **My work → Calendar → Subscribe**. Click **Make a new link** to replace your link; the old link stops working. Click **Turn it off** to end the link completely.

## Q: How do I put a planned field visit in my calendar?
A: On the planned visit's page in Deskzo, the person making the visit clicks **Add to my calendar** in the visit's **Meetings** card. If the visit is moved or cancelled later, the calendar entry follows it.

## Q: My calendar says it needs connecting again. What do I do?
A: Your Microsoft, Google or Zoho account has stopped letting Deskzo in. This often happens after a password change or a withdrawn permission. Click **Reconnect** on **My work → Calendar** and allow the calendar. Until you reconnect, meetings you schedule go into Deskzo's own calendar.

## Q: Why is there no option to add a Teams, Meet or Zoho Meeting link?
A: The online meeting link option appears only when your own calendar is connected in Deskzo. Meetings kept in Deskzo's own calendar have no automatic meeting link. If you need one, type the link into **Where**.

## Q: Who can see meetings on a customer's Meetings card?
A: On a record's **Meetings** card in Deskzo, you see the meetings you organised and those organised by people who report to you. To see everybody's meetings, your role needs the **See everyone's calls, notes & meetings** permission. Management has it by default.

## Q: Where do the holidays on my calendar come from?
A: The holidays on Deskzo Calendar come from the company's working calendar in **Settings → Holidays & leave**. Your approved leave also shows, if the leave module is open to you.

## Q: What meeting lengths are allowed?
A: A Deskzo meeting must be between five minutes and one day long. If not, Deskzo shows "A meeting runs between five minutes and a day." The start time must be in the future.

## Q: I can't see Calendar in my menu. What should I do?
A: If **My work → Calendar** is missing in Deskzo, check with your admin:
1. Calendar must be in your company's Deskzo plan.
2. The Calendar module must be switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. Your role must have the **My work › Calendar** section permission ticked in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
Calendar is also hidden while an admin is viewing the app as somebody else.

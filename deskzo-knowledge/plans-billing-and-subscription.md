# Plan, Billing and Subscription — Deskzo

Settings → Plan & billing is where a Deskzo workspace owner sees and pays for the workspace's plan: the products and add-ons, how many people (seats) are in use, invoices, and billing details. It is the same for every Deskzo product. Prices, plan limits and policies are set by Deskzo: [PRICING: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: Where do I see my Deskzo plan?
A: Go to Settings → Plan & billing. The **Your plan** card shows your plan's status, the products and add-ons you pay for, how you pay, when it renews, and "People with an account: X of Y" (seats used out of seats in your plan).

## Q: Who can open Plan & billing?
A: The workspace owner (super admin) and anyone they give the **Manage the plan and billing** permission, for example an accounts person. Only the super admin can give this permission. Everyone else sees a "not found" page. If you are viewing the app as another user, you see "Switch back to your own account first."

## Q: What products and add-ons can I buy?
A: Deskzo products are Deskzo One (everything), Deskzo CRM, Deskzo Pipeline, Deskzo Books (India only), Deskzo People (India only), Deskzo Desk, Deskzo Inventory, Deskzo Subscriptions, Deskzo Projects, Deskzo Campaigns, Deskzo Analytics, Deskzo Vault, Deskzo Cards and Deskzo Signatures. Add-ons are Revenue & Close, AI Plus, More people, Automate Plus and Customer inbox. In Settings → Plan & billing, the **Choose a plan** card lists what is on sale for your workspace. ## Q: How do I choose and pay for a plan?
A: 1. Go to Settings → Plan & billing.
2. In **Choose a plan**, pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
3. Choose your product and the number of people.
4. Under "Add-ons — how many of each (none is fine)", add any add-ons.
5. Choose **Pay online** or **Pay by bank transfer** (bank transfer is offered in India).
6. Click **Continue to payment** (or **Email me a proforma** for bank transfer).
The plan switches on as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually within a minute. The page updates by itself.

## Q: How can I pay for Deskzo?
A: You can pay online by card, and in India also by UPI or netbanking mandate. In India you can also ask for a proforma and pay by bank transfer. Tax is added as your country requires. Accepted payment methods and currencies: [POLICY: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: How do I pay by bank transfer?
A: In Settings → Plan & billing, choose **Pay by bank transfer**, choose the plan, people and how many periods to **Pay for**, then click **Email me a proforma**. The proforma, with GST included, is emailed to the billing email. Pay it by bank transfer and quote the proforma number as the reference. The plan switches on, and the GST tax invoice is emailed, once the payment reaches Deskzo. Your proformas are listed in the **Proformas** card. An expired proforma says "expired — ask for a new one".

## Q: How do I add more users (seats) to my plan?
A: Each active user takes one seat. To add seats, the workspace owner changes the number of people on the plan in Settings → Plan & billing. When paying online through a mandate, the **Change plan or people** card applies the change from the next renewal date. If you need more people before then, contact Deskzo: [CONTACT: to be filled by Deskzo team]. Seat prices: [PRICING: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: I can't add a user because every seat is taken. What can I do?
A: Deskzo shows "This workspace's plan includes N users, and every seat is taken. Switch somebody off, or ask the workspace owner to add seats." You can switch off a user who has left (Administration → Users → Login & Access → **Switch off**) to free a seat. Or the workspace owner can add seats in Settings → Plan & billing.

## Q: Why can't I choose fewer people than I have users?
A: Deskzo checks that your plan covers everyone who has an active account. If you choose too few, you see a message like "12 people have an account in this workspace, and the plans chosen are for 10. Choose at least 12 people, or switch some accounts off first." Switch off unused accounts first, or choose more people.

## Q: How do I change my plan?
A: Go to Settings → Plan & billing. If you pay online through a mandate, use the **Change plan or people** card; the change starts when your current plan renews, and you authorise it on the payment page. Nothing is charged until then. If you pay by card, use **Manage billing**. If you pay by bank transfer, use **Pay the next period by bank transfer**. Upgrade and downgrade rules: [POLICY: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: How do I cancel my Deskzo subscription?
A: In Settings → Plan & billing, click the cancel option beside your plan and confirm. The plan runs to the end of the period already paid for and does not renew. The status then shows "Cancelled: it runs until … and does not renew." Cancellation and refund policy: [POLICY: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: Where do I download my Deskzo invoices?
A: Go to Settings → Plan & billing and scroll to the **Invoices** card. Each invoice shows its date, number, amount and status. Click **Tax invoice …** or **View** to open it. Credit notes appear beside their invoice.

## Q: How do I change the GSTIN or address on my Deskzo invoices?
A: Go to Settings → Plan & billing, **Billing details**. Set the **Billing email (reminders and receipts)**, the registered name for your tax invoices, the registered address, the State, and your GSTIN (or VAT/tax number outside India). Save. If the billing email is empty, the owner's email is used.

## Q: What does "free trial" mean on my plan?
A: The status shows "On a free trial until <date>." A trial has a set number of users. When the trial ends, you must choose a plan by a date, or the workspace is held. Trial length and trial limits: [PRICING: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: My workspace is held. What does that mean?
A: A held workspace means no plan is paid for, for example after a trial ends or a payment fails. The page says "The workspace is held until a plan is paid for. Nothing has been deleted — paying opens it again at once." The workspace owner can still open Settings → Plan & billing and pay. Grace periods: [POLICY: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: A payment failed. What happens?
A: The status shows "A payment has failed and is being retried. Unless it is paid by <date>, the workspace is held." Check your card or mandate and pay before that date. If you need help: [CONTACT: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: How do I buy more Deskzo AI credits?
A: Go to Settings → Plan & billing and find the **Deskzo AI credits** card. Choose a credit pack and pay online. Credits are added as soon as payment is confirmed. Packs are used after your plan's credits and last twelve months. The copilot and Automate's drafting use credits; a copilot question usually uses 2–8 credits. Pack prices: [PRICING: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: My plan says "looked after by Deskzo" or "Sold and supported by" a partner. What does it mean?
A: "Your plan is looked after by Deskzo — there is nothing to pay here" means Deskzo manages your plan directly. "Sold and supported by <partner>" names the partner who sold your workspace; contact them for billing questions. You cannot change the partner from this page.

## Q: A module says "Not in your plan". How do I get it?
A: A module marked **Not in your plan** in Settings → Modules is not included in your workspace's plan. Only the workspace owner can add it by changing the plan in Settings → Plan & billing. After that, switch it on in Settings → Modules.

## Q: I can't see Plan & billing in Settings.
A: Plan & billing is only for the workspace owner and people with the **Manage the plan and billing** permission. Ask your workspace owner to make the change or to give you that permission.

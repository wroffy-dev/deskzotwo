# Speak Up & Forms — Deskzo

Speak Up & Forms gives staff an anonymous feedback channel (**Speak up**) and lets HR send forms, polls and votes (**Forms & polls**) to everyone, some departments or chosen people. It is part of Deskzo People (India only) and Deskzo One. In the sidebar it is in the **People** section: **Speak up**, **Forms & polls** and **Anonymous feedback**.

## Q: What is Speak up in Deskzo?
A: Speak up lets anyone send praise, a concern, a suggestion, a grievance or something else, about the company or a named colleague, without their name on it. It goes to people with the **Read anonymous feedback** permission, usually HR, directors and admins. Open it from **People → Speak up**.

## Q: How do I send anonymous feedback?
A: To send anonymous feedback in Deskzo:
1. Go to **People → Speak up**.
2. Choose **What kind of thing**: **Praise**, **Concern**, **Suggestion**, **Grievance** or **Something else**.
3. Under **About someone (optional)**, pick a colleague, or leave **About the company generally**.
4. Add a **Rating (optional)** if you like.
5. Write **What you want to say** (at least ten characters).
6. Select **Send anonymously**.

## Q: How do I raise a complaint or grievance anonymously?
A: Use Speak up. Go to **People → Speak up**, choose **Grievance** under **What kind of thing**, write what you want to say and select **Send anonymously**. Your name is not stored with it.

## Q: Can HR or an admin find out who sent anonymous feedback?
A: No. Speak up stores no name, only the date (not the time), and writes nothing to the audit trail. The count for the daily limit is kept separately and never joined to what anyone wrote. Only the words themselves can give a sender away. If you describe something only you and one other person saw, that person may guess.

## Q: Will the person I write about see my feedback?
A: No. The person a piece of Speak up feedback is about never sees it directly. HR decides what to raise and how.

## Q: Is there a limit on how much feedback I can send?
A: Yes. Speak up allows ten items a day. When you reach it you see "That's 10 today, which is the daily limit. It resets tomorrow."

## Q: Why does Speak up say my message is too short?
A: Feedback needs at least ten characters. The message is "Say a little more — ten characters isn't enough for anybody to act on."

## Q: How do I read anonymous feedback as HR?
A: To read Speak up feedback:
1. Go to **People → Anonymous feedback**.
2. Read each item. Nothing on it can be traced to a sender.
3. Note **What was done about it (optional)** and select **Mark reviewed**.
You need the **Read anonymous feedback** permission. You are notified when something new arrives.

## Q: I can't see Speak up, Forms & polls or Anonymous feedback in my menu.
A: Check with your admin:
1. Your plan must include Deskzo People or Deskzo One.
2. Speak Up & Forms must be switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. Your role needs the section **People › Speak Up & Forms** in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
**Anonymous feedback** also needs **Read anonymous feedback**. The **Manage forms** button needs **Create forms, polls and votes**.

## Q: What is Forms & polls?
A: Forms & polls is where HR asks the company something in Deskzo: a form, a poll or a vote. It can go to **Everyone**, **Selected departments** or **Selected people**. Each form is either anonymous or attributed, and people are told which before they answer. Open it from **People → Forms & polls**.

## Q: How do I create a survey, poll or vote?
A: To create a form in Deskzo:
1. Go to **People → Forms & polls**, select **Manage forms**, then **New form**.
2. Choose the **Kind**: **Form**, **Poll** or **Vote**. Set **Closes on** if it should close.
3. Write a **Title** and **Introduction**.
4. Under **Answers are**, choose **Anonymous** or **Attributed**.
5. Under **Who it goes to**, choose **Everyone**, **Selected departments** or **Selected people**, and tick them.
6. Tick **Must be filled in** if everyone must answer.
7. Add questions. For each, pick a type and whether it is **Required**. For choice questions, type options separated by commas.
8. Under **Publish as**, choose **Open — notify them now** and select **Publish and notify**, or **Draft — nobody sees it** and **Save as draft**.
You need **Create forms, polls and votes**.

## Q: What question types can a form have?
A: Deskzo Forms & polls question types are: **Rating out of 5**, **Scale of 1 to 10**, **Choose one**, **Choose any**, **Yes or no** and **Written answer**.

## Q: What is the difference between anonymous and attributed forms?
A: In Forms & polls, **Anonymous** means you know who has answered but never what they said; answers have no link to anyone. **Attributed** means each answer is recorded against the person's name, and the form tells them so first. This cannot be changed once anyone has answered.

## Q: How do I answer a form?
A: Go to **People → Forms & polls**. Forms you still have to answer come first; **Must fill** marks ones you must answer. Open the form; it says at the top if it is anonymous or attributed. Answer and select **Submit**.

## Q: Why does a form cover my screen when I sign in?
A: It is a form marked **Must be filled in**. Select **Fill it in** to answer it, or **Not now** to put it away. It comes back each time you sign in until you answer.

## Q: Why can't I see a form's results?
A: Forms & polls results stay hidden until five people have answered, whatever your permissions. The results page says how many more answers are needed.

## Q: How do I see results, or close or delete a form?
A: Go to **People → Forms & polls → Manage forms**. Select **Results** to see who has answered, who has not, and the answers added up. Change the status to **Open**, **Closed** or **Draft**. You can delete a form only while nobody has answered. Otherwise Deskzo says to close it instead, because deleting destroys the answers.

## Q: I opened a draft form, but nobody was notified.
A: People are notified only when a form is published with **Open — notify them now** from **New form**. Changing a draft's status to Open later does not send notifications, so tell people yourself.

## Q: How do I send a form only to some departments?
A: When creating the form, under **Who it goes to** choose **Selected departments** and tick them. People need a department set by an admin in **Settings → Users**. Only the people a form goes to can see it.

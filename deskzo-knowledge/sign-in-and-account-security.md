# Signing in and account security — Deskzo

Every company on Deskzo has its own workspace at its own web address, and you sign in there with your work email and password. Deskzo supports two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app, and companies can also turn on sign-in with Microsoft, Google or Zoho. This applies to every Deskzo product.

## Q: How do I sign in to Deskzo?
A: 1. Open your company's workspace address in your browser.
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Choose **Sign in**.
4. If two-factor sign-in is on for you, type the 6-digit code from your authenticator app in **Authenticator code** and choose **Verify**.
If your company uses Microsoft, Google or Zoho sign-in, choose the **Sign in with ...** button instead.

## Q: What is my workspace address?
A: Your workspace address is your company's own web address on Deskzo. It starts with your company's workspace name (for example "yourcompany") followed by the Deskzo domain. Some companies use their own custom domain instead, set by the workspace owner. Ask your admin or the person who invited you for the address.

## Q: I forgot my workspace address. How do I find it?
A: 1. Go to the Deskzo website and open **Sign in**.
2. Under **Find my workspaces**, type your **Work email**.
3. Choose **Email me my workspaces**.
Deskzo emails you a link to every workspace you can sign in to. If nothing arrives after a few minutes, check your spam folder, or ask whoever invited you for your workspace address.

## Q: How do I sign in from the Deskzo website?
A: On the Deskzo website, open **Sign in**. Under **Go to your workspace**, type your **Workspace name** (the first part of your address) and choose **Continue to sign in**. Deskzo opens your workspace's own sign-in page. A workspace name is 3 to 40 letters, digits or hyphens.

## Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
A: 1. On your workspace's sign-in page, choose **Forgot your password?**
2. Type your **Email** and choose **Send me a link**.
3. Open the email "Set a new password". The link works once, for one hour.
4. Type a **New password** of at least 10 characters, and again in **The same again**.
5. Choose **Set my password**, then **Sign in**.
Setting a new password signs out everybody who was signed in with the old one. Two-factor sign-in stays on.

## Q: How do I change my password?
A: If you know your current password, change it on **My profile**:
1. Choose your name or photo at the top right to open **My profile**.
2. In the **Password** card, type your **Current password**, your **New password** and **Confirm new password**.
3. Choose **Update password**.
If you forgot your password, use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page instead.

## Q: The reset password link does not work. Why?
A: The message "This link has expired or been used. Ask for a new one from the sign-in page." means the link is older than one hour, was already used, or a newer link was sent. Asking again replaces earlier links. Go to the sign-in page, choose **Forgot your password?**, and use the newest email.

## Q: I did not get the password reset email.
A: For safety, Deskzo always shows the same message, whether or not the email has an account: "If ... has an account here, a link to set a new password is on its way." If no email arrives, check spam, check that you typed the email you use for Deskzo, and check you are on the right workspace's sign-in page. If it still does not come, ask your workspace admin to send a reset email or reset your password.

## Q: Sign-in says "Invalid email or password". What should I do?
A: The message "Invalid email or password." means the email or password is wrong, or the account is not active. Check for typing mistakes and Caps Lock. If you forgot the password, choose **Forgot your password?** If your account was deactivated, ask your workspace admin.

## Q: Sign-in says "Too many attempts". Is my account locked?
A: Yes, for a short time. After 8 failed sign-in attempts in 10 minutes, Deskzo blocks more tries for 15 minutes. The message says "Too many attempts. Try again in ... minutes." Wait, then try again. If you forgot the password, use **Forgot your password?** A new password clears the lock.

## Q: What is two-factor sign-in?
A: Two-factor sign-in (two-factor authentication) means you type a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone, as well as your password, when you sign in. It is the best protection for your Deskzo account. Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator or any similar app works.

## Q: How do I turn on two-factor sign-in?
A: 1. Open **My profile**.
2. In the **Two-factor authentication** card, choose **Set up two-factor**.
3. Type your **Current password** and choose **Continue**.
4. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, or type the key shown under "Can't scan?".
5. Type the **6-digit code** from the app and choose **Confirm**.
The card then shows **Enabled**.

## Q: Two-factor setup says "That code didn't match". What is wrong?
A: The message "That code didn't match. Check the time on your phone and try again." usually means the clock on your phone is wrong, or the code changed before you typed it. Set your phone's time to automatic, wait for a fresh code, and type it again.

## Q: I lost my phone and cannot get my two-factor code. How do I sign in?
A: Deskzo has no backup codes. Ask your workspace admin to reset your two-factor. An admin with the **Create and edit users** permission opens **Settings → Staff → Users**, finds you, and chooses **Reset** next to "2FA on". You can then sign in with your password and set up two-factor again on **My profile**. If your company requires two-factor, you must set it up again before you can use the rest of the app.

## Q: How do I change to a new phone for two-factor sign-in?
A: On **My profile**, in the **Two-factor authentication** card, choose **Replace authenticator**. Type your current password, scan the new QR code with the app on your new phone, and confirm with a 6-digit code. Your old authenticator stops working once the new one is confirmed.

## Q: How do I turn off two-factor sign-in?
A: On **My profile**, in the **Two-factor authentication** card, choose **Disable**. Type your current password and choose **Disable two-factor**. Deskzo sends you a security notice "Two-factor was switched off". If your company requires two-factor, you will be asked to set it up again.

## Q: Why does Deskzo force me to set up two-factor before I can work?
A: Your administrator turned on **Require two-factor authentication** in **Settings → Security**. Until you set it up, Deskzo keeps you on **My profile** with the message "Your administrator requires two-factor authentication for all accounts — set it up below." Set up two-factor, then the rest of the app opens.

## Q: Why does Deskzo say I must change my password?
A: Your admin reset your password to a temporary one. **My profile** shows: "You signed in with a temporary password from your administrator. Choose your own below before continuing — use the temporary one as your current password." In the **Password** card, type the temporary password as **Current password**, choose a new one, and choose **Update password**.

## Q: Can I sign in with Microsoft, Google or Zoho?
A: Yes, if your company turned it on. An admin switches on Microsoft, Google or Zoho sign-in in **Settings → Security**. Then the sign-in page shows buttons like **Sign in with Microsoft**. If the admin also chose **Require single sign-on**, everyone except admins must use that button. The sign-in page then shows "Admin sign-in only" above the password form.

## Q: Sign-in says "Your account signs in with ... — use that button above". What does it mean?
A: Your company or your role allows only single sign-on for your account, not a password. Use the **Sign in with Microsoft**, **Google** or **Zoho** button on the sign-in page. If the message says "the sign-in it uses is switched off", ask your administrator.

## Q: How do I sign out of Deskzo?
A: Choose **Sign out** at the top right of the screen. On a phone, it is the sign-out icon in the header.

## Q: How do I see where my account is signed in, and end a session?
A: Open **My profile** and scroll to **Your devices and sign-ins**. It lists your devices and recent sign-ins with network and approximate location. Your current one shows **This session**. To sign out a session you do not recognise, choose **End it** next to it, then change your password.

## Q: Deskzo says "Waiting for this device to be approved". What do I do?
A: Your company requires an administrator to approve each new device. The administrators have been told. Once they approve the device, choose **Check again**. Other messages on this page include "This network is blocked", "Not from this network", "Your access is locked" and "Share your location to continue". Each explains what your admin's rules need. Ask your admin if you think it is a mistake.

## Q: The sign-in page says the app is down for maintenance.
A: The message "The app is down for maintenance ... Only administrators can use it until then." means Deskzo is being updated. Only administrators can sign in until the time shown. Try again after that time.

## Q: Can I switch between two Deskzo workspaces without signing in again?
A: Yes, if linked sign-in is available. On **My profile**, the **Linked workspaces** card has **Add a workspace**. Type the other workspace's address and sign in there to confirm. After that, use the workspace switcher in the header to move between them. Each workspace keeps its own password and two-factor. Changing your password unlinks your workspaces.

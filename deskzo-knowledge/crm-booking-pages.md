# Booking Pages — Deskzo

Booking pages are public pages where your customers book a meeting with your team in the time you have free. Each booking goes into the host's calendar with an invitation and opens a lead in the pipeline you choose. Booking pages are part of Deskzo CRM, Deskzo Pipeline and Deskzo One, and appear in the sidebar as Sales → Booking pages.

## Q: What are booking pages?
A: A Deskzo booking page saves the back-and-forth of finding a meeting time. You share its web address on your website, in an email, an email signature or a message. The customer picks a day and a time from the times your team is actually free. Each page has its own address, weekly hours, meeting length and hosts.

## Q: Who can manage booking pages?
A: You need the **Manage booking pages** permission (Management by default). Without it, Booking pages is not in your menu. Hosts do not need this permission: anybody active in the workspace with an email address can be a host.

## Q: I can't see Booking pages in my menu. Why?
A: Check these with your admin:
1. Booking pages must be in your company's plan (Deskzo CRM, Deskzo Pipeline or Deskzo One).
2. The Booking pages module must be switched on in Settings → Modules.
3. Your role needs the **Manage booking pages** permission, and the **Sales › Booking pages** section must be ticked in Settings → Roles & Permissions.

## Q: How do I make a booking page?
A: To make a booking page in Deskzo:
1. Go to Sales → Booking pages and click **New booking page**.
2. Enter a **Title** (what the customer sees, for example "Product demo"). The **Address** fills in from the title; you can change it.
3. Under **What it is for**, describe the meeting.
4. Choose the **Length**, **Days ahead**, **Notice (hours)** and **Gap between (minutes)**.
5. Tick the people under **Who customers can meet**.
6. Set the **Hours** for each open day. Untick a day to close it.
7. Choose the pipeline under **Open a lead in**, and whether to **Add an online meeting link**.
8. Leave **Taking bookings** ticked and click **Make the page**.

## Q: How do I let customers schedule a meeting with us?
A: Make a booking page in Sales → Booking pages with **New booking page**. Set its title, length, hosts and hours, then click **Make the page**. Copy the page's address from its card and share it with customers. They pick a free time and book without signing in.

## Q: How do I share a booking page?
A: Copy the address from the page's card in Sales → Booking pages. Put it on your website, in an email, an email signature or a message. Anybody with the address can book, without signing in.

## Q: What does the customer see when booking?
A: The customer opens the address, picks a day under **Choose a day** and a time under **Choose a time**. Times are shown in your workspace's time zone. They enter **Your name** and **Email**, and optionally **Company**, **Phone** and **Anything we should know?**. They click **Book** and see "You're booked" with who they are meeting and when.

## Q: What happens after a customer books?
A: The meeting goes into the host's calendar and the customer gets an invitation. Deskzo also opens a lead, with its company and contact, owned by the host. Existing companies and contacts are matched where they exist. If your workspace does not have Leads, the booking still adds the company and contact, just no lead.

## Q: Which host gets the booking?
A: The customer does not choose the person. A booking goes to whichever host is free at that time, the one with the fewest upcoming bookings first. A time is offered while at least one host is free. A page can have up to ten hosts.

## Q: How do I see what has been booked?
A: The **Bookings** list on Sales → Booking pages shows upcoming bookings (and yesterday's): when, who booked (linked to their lead), who they are meeting and on which page. Each page's card also shows how many are coming up.

## Q: How do I change or switch off a booking page?
A: Click **Change** on the page's card, make your changes and click **Save**. To stop new bookings without losing the page, untick **Taking bookings**. Then the page's address says the booking page is not available.

## Q: How do I delete a booking page?
A: Click **Delete** on the page's card. A page that has been booked is switched off instead, so its bookings are kept. While bookings are coming up the button says **Switch off**.

## Q: What are the limits for a booking page?
A: In Deskzo: the Title is up to 80 characters. The Address is 3 to 41 lower-case letters, digits and hyphens, and must be unique. What it is for is up to 1,000 characters. Length is 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90 or 120 minutes. Days ahead is 1 to 90. Notice is 0 to 336 hours. Gap between is 0 to 120 minutes. Up to ten hosts. At least one day must be open. New pages start Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00. Changing the address breaks the old link.

## Q: It says "Nobody can host yet".
A: A host must be somebody active in the workspace with an email address on their user. Check in Settings → Users that the people who should take meetings are active and have an email address. If you pick an unsuitable host you see "Each host must be somebody here who is active and has an email address."

## Q: How do hosts' calendars affect a booking page?
A: Ask hosts to connect their Outlook, Google or Zoho calendar from My work → Calendar. Their busy times are then left out of the booking page, and a booking can include an online meeting link. Without a connected calendar, a booking goes into Deskzo's own calendar and the invitation is emailed.

## Q: Customers see "No times are free just now".
A: Every time in the page's hours is taken, too soon for the notice you set, beyond the days ahead, or the hosts' calendars show them busy. Widen the hours, add a host or shorten the notice.

## Q: A customer was told "That time has just been taken. Choose another."
A: Somebody else booked that time, or the host became busy, between the customer opening the page and booking. The same time can never be booked twice. The customer can choose another time.

## Q: The customer didn't get an invitation.
A: If the meeting could not be added to the host's calendar, the customer is told the host will send an invitation, and the host's notification says what went wrong. The host should send one themselves. Also check Settings → Customer emails: if the booking invitation email is switched off there, guests are not emailed for any booking page.

## Q: Who does the lead from a booking belong to?
A: The host the booking went to. The lead is titled with the page and the customer's company (or name), and it starts in the pipeline chosen under **Open a lead in**.

## Q: I get "The address ... is taken. Choose another."
A: Another booking page already uses that address. Each page's address must be unique. Type a different address and save again.

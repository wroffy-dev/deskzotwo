# My profile and preferences — Deskzo

**My profile** is your own account page in Deskzo. There you can add a photo, set your work phone, change your password, set up two-factor sign-in, connect your mailbox or calendar, link other workspaces, and see your devices and sign-ins. Notification and sound choices are on **Notifications → Preferences**, and light or dark theme is a button in the header. This is part of every Deskzo product.

## Q: How do I open My profile?
A: Choose your name or photo at the top right of the screen. You can also go to **Settings → My profile**. The page title is **My profile**.

## Q: What can I change on My profile?
A: On **My profile** you can:
- Add, change or remove your **Photo**.
- Set your **Work phone** in the **Contact** card.
- Change your **Password**.
- Set up, replace or turn off **Two-factor authentication**.
- Connect **Your mailbox** or calendar, if your company uses it.
- Manage **Linked workspaces**, if available.
- See and end sessions in **Your devices and sign-ins**.

## Q: How do I change my name, role or department?
A: You cannot change your name, role, department or reporting manager yourself. **My profile** shows them in the **Account** card, with the note: "Name, role, and department are managed by an admin under Settings → Staff → Users." Ask your admin to change them.

## Q: How do I change my email address in Deskzo?
A: You cannot change your email on **My profile**. Your email is how you sign in, so it is an account change. Ask your workspace admin.

## Q: How do I add or change my profile photo?
A: 1. Open **My profile**.
2. In the **Photo** card, choose **Add a photo** (or **Change photo**).
3. Pick an image file.
The photo must be PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, under 96 KB. Square images look best; others are cropped. Without a photo, Deskzo shows your initials.

## Q: My photo upload says the image is too big.
A: Deskzo profile photos must be under 96 KB. The message says, for example, "That image is 240KB — keep it under 96KB. Cropping it square usually does it." Crop the image to a square or make it smaller, then choose it again.

## Q: How do I remove my profile photo?
A: Open **My profile**. In the **Photo** card, choose **Remove**. Deskzo then shows your initials instead.

## Q: How do I add my phone number to my profile?
A: Open **My profile**. In the **Contact** card, type your number in **Work phone** and choose **Save**. Your work phone is shown on quotes and invoices you raise, so the customer can reach you. Leave it blank and only your email is given.

## Q: How do I change my password on My profile?
A: 1. Open **My profile**.
2. In the **Password** card, type your **Current password**.
3. Type your **New password** and the same again in **Confirm new password**.
4. Choose **Update password**. You see "Password updated."
If the current password is wrong, you see "Current password is incorrect." Changing your password also unlinks any linked workspaces.

## Q: How do I set up two-factor sign-in on My profile?
A: 1. Open **My profile**.
2. In **Two-factor authentication**, choose **Set up two-factor**.
3. Type your **Current password** and choose **Continue**.
4. Scan the QR code with Microsoft Authenticator, Google Authenticator or another authenticator app.
5. Type the **6-digit code** and choose **Confirm**.
The card then shows **Enabled**. To use a new phone later, choose **Replace authenticator**. To turn it off, choose **Disable**.

## Q: Why does Deskzo ask for my password to set up two-factor?
A: Deskzo asks you to confirm it is you before changing how your account signs in. A session on a borrowed or stolen computer should not be enough to swap your authenticator.

## Q: How do I connect my email so I can send invoices from my own address?
A: If your company has set up Microsoft 365, Google Workspace or Zoho, **My profile** shows a **Your mailbox** card. Choose to connect, and approve Deskzo at Microsoft, Google or Zoho. Invoices, proposals and credit notes you email then go from your own address, land in your Sent folder, and replies come back to you. If the card says your company has not set up mail yet, an admin does it under **Settings → Security**.

## Q: Connecting my mailbox says "That account isn't the one on your account here". Why?
A: You signed in at Microsoft, Google or Zoho with a different email from your Deskzo email. Connect the mailbox for the email address on your Deskzo profile.

## Q: How do I connect my calendar?
A: If the Calendar module is on, **My profile** shows a card for your calendar. Connect your Outlook calendar, Google Calendar or Zoho Calendar there to keep your calendar in step with Deskzo.

## Q: How do I switch between light and dark mode?
A: Use the theme buttons in the header at the top of the screen: the sun button for **Light** and the moon button for **Dark**. On a phone, the theme buttons are in the menu drawer. Your choice is remembered in that browser. Until you choose, Deskzo follows your company's default theme or your computer's setting.

## Q: How do I choose which notifications I get?
A: 1. Go to **My work → Notifications** (or **Settings → Notification preferences**).
2. Open the **Preferences** tab.
3. Under **Tell me when…**, switch each kind of notice on or off for **In app**.
Some notices are always on. Which notices are emailed is chosen by your workspace admins for everyone, under **Settings → Email notifications**.

## Q: How do I turn notification sounds on or off?
A: 1. Go to **My work → Notifications** and open the **Preferences** tab.
2. In the **Sounds** card, tick or untick **Play a sound when a reminder or a chat message arrives**.
3. Under **Which reminders**, choose which ones chime.
Choose **Play the sound** to hear it. Sounds play only while Deskzo is open in a tab, after you have clicked on the page once.

## Q: I don't hear any Deskzo reminder sounds.
A: Deskzo plays sounds only while it is open in a browser tab, and only after you have clicked anywhere on the page once (browsers block sound before that). Check that sounds are on in **Notifications → Preferences → Sounds**, that your computer is not muted, and choose **Play the sound** to test.

## Q: How do I see what permissions I have?
A: Go to **Settings → What I can do**. It lists every permission your account holds and where each one came from, such as your role.

## Q: How do I sign out a device I don't recognise?
A: Open **My profile** and scroll to **Your devices and sign-ins**. Find the session under **Recent sign-ins** and choose **End it**. Then change your password. Your current session is marked **This session** and cannot be ended there — use **Sign out** at the top right.

## Q: Why can't I leave My profile?
A: Deskzo keeps you on **My profile** when your admin requires something first: either you signed in with a temporary password and must choose your own, or your company requires two-factor sign-in and you have not set it up. Finish the step shown in the yellow message, and the rest of the app opens.

## Q: I can't see "Your mailbox" or "Linked workspaces" on My profile.
A: These cards appear only when they apply. **Your mailbox** shows when your company uses document emailing from your own address or the Calendar module. **Linked workspaces** shows only when switching between workspaces is available. They are also hidden while an admin is viewing the app as you. Ask your admin if you expect to see them.

# Glossary: Support, Projects, Renewals and Insights Terms — Deskzo

Plain definitions of Deskzo words used in Tickets, the Customer Portal, Customer feedback, the Customer inbox, Projects, Renewals, Reports, Targets and Incentives.

## Q: What is the helpdesk address in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo helpdesk address is the address in Settings → Support email that your support mailbox forwards to, so each email becomes a ticket.

## Q: What is the Support inbox in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo Support inbox lists support emails that could not be matched to a customer or ticket automatically.

## Q: What is Support load in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo Support load is a page comparing how much support each customer used with what they were billed.

## Q: What is an SLA on a Deskzo ticket?
A: A Deskzo ticket SLA is the fixed deadline for a ticket, set by its priority (4, 24, 72 or 168 hours).

## Q: What is Free Support on a Deskzo ticket?
A: Free Support is a Deskzo ticket that is not linked to anything the customer bought.

## Q: What is Ticket assignment in Deskzo?
A: Ticket assignment is a Deskzo department setting that lets the department's members hold tickets.

## Q: What is the Customer Portal in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo Customer Portal is a private page for each customer, opened with a link instead of a password. A portal link is the private link for one customer contact; it can expire or be revoked.

## Q: What is a customer request in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo customer request is a renewal, more-licences or general question a customer sends from the Customer Portal.

## Q: What is a feedback link in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo feedback link is a one-time link that asks a customer for a 1–5 star score. "About me" is the page showing feedback that names you.

## Q: What is the Customer inbox in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo Customer inbox is an add-on that brings website chat and WhatsApp conversations into one inbox. Each website chat bubble or WhatsApp number is a channel.

## Q: What are set answers and hand over in the Deskzo Customer inbox?
A: Set answers are keyword-matched replies the Deskzo Customer inbox bot gives. Hand over is when the bot passes a conversation to a person.

## Q: What is a saved reply in the Deskzo Customer inbox?
A: A Deskzo saved reply is a team reply you insert by typing "/" and a shortcut.

## Q: What is a WhatsApp template in Deskzo?
A: A WhatsApp template is a Meta-approved message. It is the only kind of WhatsApp message Deskzo can send more than 24 hours after the customer's last message.

## Q: What is a project in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo project is a piece of work delivered for one customer, with milestones, billing stages, a team, risks, documents and credentials. Only the people on the project can see it.

## Q: What is a milestone in Deskzo Projects?
A: A Deskzo milestone is a step in a project. Ticking milestones done gives the project its progress.

## Q: What is a billing stage in Deskzo Projects?
A: A Deskzo billing stage is a part of a project's contract value that is invoiced at a point, such as an advance or on sign-off.

## Q: What is project health in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo project health is On track, At risk or Off track. It is set by hand or by posting an update.

## Q: What is a renewal in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo renewal is a subscription with an expiry date, shown in Renewals. It is the order the subscription was punched on.

## Q: What are renewal stages in Deskzo?
A: Deskzo renewal stages show how far a renewal has got: Not started, Task raised, Contacted, Quoted, Negotiating, On hold, Lost or Renewed.

## Q: What is a renewal manager in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo renewal manager is the person who looks after a customer's renewals. By default it is the account manager.

## Q: What does "Lapsed — not renewed" mean in Deskzo?
A: "Lapsed — not renewed" is a Deskzo subscription that has ended, was not renewed, and nobody marked as Renewed or Lost.

## Q: What is a report in Deskzo Reports?
A: A Deskzo report is a saved set of pages of charts over one data source, such as Invoices or Tickets.

## Q: What is a calculated field in Deskzo Reports?
A: A Deskzo calculated field is a figure you define in a report from other figures, such as a collection rate.

## Q: What is the Explorer in Deskzo Reports?
A: The Deskzo Explorer is a quick tool in Reports to break down any figure by anything, then download or print it.

## Q: What is a target in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo target is a number for a person, team or the company to hit over a month, quarter or financial year. "What counts" on each target card explains what is counted.

## Q: What is pace on a Deskzo target?
A: Deskzo pace compares achievement with an even run rate to date. It shows Ahead of pace, On track, Behind pace or At risk.

## Q: What is an incentive scheme in Deskzo?
A: A Deskzo incentive scheme is the rule that turns a target's achievement into money: a percentage, flat amount, per unit, or bands.

## Q: What is the incentive queue in Deskzo?
A: The Deskzo incentive queue is where incentives are worked out, approved, held, cancelled and marked paid. A one-off award is a one-time incentive given by hand and approved the same way.

# Dashboard, navigation and search — Deskzo

This guide covers moving around Deskzo: the dashboard and its tabs, the sidebar menu, the **Create** button, the search box, keyboard shortcuts, the tool rail on the right (My tasks, Help, What's new and more), the AI copilot, and **Contact Support**. These are part of every Deskzo product.

## Q: What is on the Deskzo dashboard?
A: The dashboard is the first page after you sign in. The top band greets you and shows your company's name and logo. Below it are tabs:
- **Getting Started** — setup steps, shown only until you finish setup.
- **Dashboard** — headline figures and your chart widgets, under the heading **My Dashboard**.
- **Recent Updates** — Deskzo's What's new and your company's news.

## Q: How do I change the date range on my dashboard?
A: On the **Dashboard** tab, choose the date button next to **Add Widget**. Pick **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **This quarter** or **Year to date**. The figures and widgets update for that range.

## Q: How do I add a widget or chart to my dashboard?
A: 1. On the **Dashboard** tab, choose **Add Widget**.
2. Pick a **Data source**, a **Metric** and a **Chart type** (for example Column Chart, Line Chart, Donut Chart, Number, Table or List).
3. Optionally set the **Date range**, a title and the **Width**.
4. Check the **Preview** and choose **Add widget**.
You can only pick data from modules you have access to.

## Q: How do I rearrange or resize dashboard widgets?
A: 1. On the **Dashboard** tab, choose **Customize**.
2. Drag cards to reorder them. On a phone, use the arrows.
3. Use **XS / S / M / L** on a card to change its width.
4. Choose **Save layout**.
To go back to the standard layout, choose **Reset to default**. **Use a layout** offers layouts set up by your admin.

## Q: How do I remove or edit a dashboard widget?
A: On the **Dashboard** tab, open a widget's options (or choose **Customize**). Choose **Edit widget** to change it, or **Remove widget** to take it off. After removing, a message "Widget removed" lets you undo.

## Q: What is the Recent Updates tab?
A: **Recent Updates** shows two lists: **From Deskzo** (Deskzo's What's new — new features and changes) and **Company news** (posts from your own company). A count on the tab shows how many are new. The count clears when you open the tab.

## Q: How is the sidebar menu organised?
A: The sidebar on the left lists the pages you can open. **Dashboard** is at the top, then **My work**. Other sections sit under headings: **CRM** (Sales, Prospecting, Marketing, Support, Renewals, Partners), **Finance** (Accounts), **Operations** (Projects, Field Visits, IT Assets, Front desk), **HR** (People), **Insights** (Reports, Targets & incentives) and **Workspace** (Tools, Automate, Administration). Click a section to open its pages. You only see sections and pages your company uses and your role allows. Some words like "Leads" may be renamed by your admin.

## Q: How do I find a page in the sidebar menu?
A: At the top of the sidebar, type in **Find a page…**. The menu shows only the pages that match. Press Escape to clear it.

## Q: How do I collapse or shrink the sidebar?
A: Choose the **Collapse sidebar** button at the top of the sidebar, next to the logo. The sidebar becomes a narrow rail of icons. Point at a section icon to see its pages. Choose **Expand sidebar** to open it again. Deskzo remembers your choice in that browser. On a phone, the menu opens with the **Open menu** button.

## Q: I can't see a page or module in my menu. Why?
A: A page shows in the sidebar only when all of these are true:
1. The product or module is in your company's plan.
2. An admin has switched it on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. Your role has the permission to open it.
Ask your admin to check. If the sidebar says "No modules assigned", contact your administrator to request access. The page may also have another name, because admins can rename words in **Settings → Wording**.

## Q: What is the Create button?
A: The **Create** button in the header is a quick way to start new records from any page. It can offer **New lead**, **Quick lead**, **Add company**, **New proposal**, **New order**, **New ticket**, **New visit**, **New expense** and **New sticky note**. You only see the choices for modules your company uses and you are allowed to use.

## Q: How do I search in Deskzo?
A: Use the search box at the top left of the header, marked "Search everything ( / )".
1. Type what you are looking for. Results appear after you stop typing.
2. Results show matching menu pages, records from the lists you can open (customers, leads, orders, tickets and more), and help articles.
3. Press Enter to open the full **Search** page with all results.
Press the **/** key anywhere outside a text box to jump to the search box.

## Q: How do I search only leads, or only tickets?
A: In the header search box, choose the magnifier menu ("Search in") and pick one list, such as **Leads**, **Customers**, **Orders**, **Invoices**, **Tickets** or **Items**. The box then says "Search in Leads". Press Enter to open that list filtered by your text. Choose **Everything** to search all again. The choice is remembered in that browser.

## Q: Can I open a record by its reference number?
A: Yes. Type the reference with its prefix in the header search box, for example "LEAD-123". Deskzo offers to open that record straight away.

## Q: Search can't find a record I know exists.
A: Search only shows records you are allowed to see. If a record belongs to another team or person, your role may not include it. The message "Nothing you can see matches" means no record you have access to matches. Ask your admin about your access.

## Q: What keyboard shortcuts does Deskzo have?
A: Press **?** (outside a text box) to open **Keyboard shortcuts**. Useful ones:
- **/** — jump to search.
- **F1** — help for this page.
- **F10** — calculator.
- **Alt + N** — your sticky notes.
- **Ctrl + Alt + M** — main menu, find any page.
- **Alt + K** — lock (sign out).
Deskzo also supports BUSY accounting keys, such as **Alt + B** for balance sheet and **Alt + T** for trial balance. You can switch BUSY's keys off in the shortcuts window. On a Mac, Option is Alt.

## Q: What is the tool rail on the right side?
A: The tool rail is the column of icons on the right edge of the screen (on wide screens). Each icon opens a panel beside your page: **Chat**, **My tasks**, **My calendar**, **Sticky notes**, **GST & margin** calculator, **Pro-rata**, **Currency**, **GSTIN check**, **What's new**, **Help** and **Video walkthroughs**. It may also show **Ask the AI copilot** and **Contact support**. You only see tools your company uses. Press Escape to close a panel.

## Q: How do I see my tasks quickly without leaving the page?
A: Choose **My tasks** on the tool rail on the right. A panel lists your tasks, and you can type in **Add a task…** to add one. The panel stays beside your page while you work.

## Q: How do I get help inside Deskzo?
A: Choose **Help** on the tool rail on the right, or press **F1**. The Help panel shows Deskzo's support contact, **The guide for this page**, and articles **From Deskzo**. Below are guides **From your company**, if your company added its own. Type in **Find a guide or article** to search. **Video walkthroughs** on the rail has videos. **What's new** shows recent changes.

## Q: What are "your company's guides" in the Help panel?
A: Your company's guides are how-tos, SOPs and videos that your own company added. They appear in the Help and Video walkthroughs panels under "From your company", kept apart from Deskzo's help. People with the **Manage your company's guides and news** permission add them in **Settings → Your company's guides**.

## Q: What is the AI copilot?
A: The AI copilot (Deskzo AI) is a chat where you ask questions about your data in plain words, for example "Which of my leads are hot right now?" It can look up companies, leads, orders, renewals, tickets, your tasks and dashboard figures, run reports, and draft tasks, notes, new leads and a change to one lead. Each draft is a card: nothing is saved until you press its button (**Create task**, **Save note**, **Create lead** or **Save changes**). It only sees and does what you already can. Open it with the copilot button in the header or **Ask the AI copilot** on the tool rail.

## Q: I can't use the AI copilot. Why?
A: The copilot shows a reason when it is not available:
- "The copilot is switched off" — an admin can switch it on in **Settings → Deskzo AI**.
- "You don't have access to the AI copilot" — your role needs the **Use the AI copilot** permission.
- "Deskzo AI isn't part of this workspace's plan" or "used all its Deskzo AI credits" — whoever manages billing can buy a credit pack in **Settings → Plan & billing**.
- "You've used today's ... Deskzo AI credits" — your daily limit resets at midnight.

## Q: How do I contact Deskzo support?
A: 1. Choose **Contact support** on the tool rail on the right. On smaller screens, choose **Contact Support** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. In "How can we help you today?", type a **Subject** and **Tell us in detail** what happened.
3. Optionally add a **Mobile number**, choose **How critical is your request?**, add **Attachments** or a **Screen recording**.
4. Tick the box to agree to send the page and device details.
5. Choose **Send**.
You get a request number like "SR-1042", and a copy is emailed to you. Support replies by email. Deskzo support contact details: [CONTACT: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: What files can I attach to a support request?
A: You can attach up to 5 files, 10 MB each: images (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP), PDF, text, log, CSV, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and ZIP files. You can also add one screen recording of up to 5 minutes and 80 MB. Watch the recording before you attach it, because it shows everything on the screen you shared.

## Q: I can't record my screen for support.
A: Screen recording may be blocked. "Your organisation's security settings don't allow screen recording." means your admin turned it off. "Your browser can't record the screen" means your browser does not support it. In both cases, attach a screenshot instead.

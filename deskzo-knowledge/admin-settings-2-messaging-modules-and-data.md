# Settings Tour, Part 2: Customers, Leads, Lists, Module Settings and Data — Deskzo

This part of the Deskzo Settings tour covers the groups Customers & messaging, Leads, Lists & taxonomy, the Module settings section, and Data & continuity. Settings is part of every Deskzo product; a screen for a module only shows when that module is in your plan and switched on.

## Q: What is in the Customers & messaging group of Settings?
A: This group has:
- **Customer portal** — who gets a portal login and what they see (needs **Manage the customer portal**).
- **Mail & messaging** — the providers that send marketing mail and when mail may go out.
- **Customer emails** — every email customers get, with switches for the automatic ones.
- **Email notifications** — which notifications are also emailed, for everyone.
- **Customer feedback** — how long a feedback link lasts and what it says.
- **Support email** — the address forwarded in so every email becomes a ticket.
- **Customer inbox** — website chat and WhatsApp numbers.
- **Renewals** — what happens when a subscription is not renewed.
- **Cloud calling** — connect your cloud telephony so Call buttons ring through your business number.
Except Customer portal, these need the **Change organisation settings** permission.

## Q: How do I stop an automatic email that goes to customers?
A: Go to Settings → Customer emails. The screen lists every email your customers get from Deskzo, where it is sent from, and what stops it. The automatic ones have a switch. Turn off the one you do not want. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission.

## Q: How do I choose which notifications are emailed to staff?
A: Go to Settings → Email notifications. Choose which notifications are emailed as well as shown in the app. The choice is the same for everyone. Each person can still choose their own notices in Settings → Notification preferences. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission and the Notifications module.

## Q: What is in the Leads group of Settings?
A: The Leads group has:
- **Pipeline** — the stages a lead moves through: names, order and colours (needs **Manage the sales pipeline**).
- **Lead assignment** — who a new lead goes to: round robin, fewest open leads, or by brand, designation, source or state.
- **Lead capture API** — keys for your websites to send enquiries in as leads.
- **Developers & API** — keys for the public API, for Zapier, Make or your own systems.
The Leads guide covers the pipeline and lead assignment in detail.

## Q: What is in the Lists & taxonomy group of Settings?
A: This group has:
- **Lists** — industries, company types, contact designations, project types and vault tags.
- **Customer categories** — how customers are grouped, with an icon and colour (needs **Manage customer categories**).
- **Custom fields** — your own fields on companies, contacts, leads, orders and products (needs **Manage custom fields**).
- **PIN directory** — India Post PIN codes used in address forms.
- **World places** — states, cities and postal codes outside India.
Brands and product families are under Items & Inventory, not here.

## Q: How do I add a custom field?
A: Go to Settings → Custom fields. Add a field to companies, contacts, leads, orders or products. Choose its type (text, number, date, dropdown and more), where it appears, whether it is required, and whether it is restricted. Restricted fields are only seen by people with the **See restricted custom fields** permission. You need the **Manage custom fields** permission.

## Q: What is in the Module settings section?
A: Module settings are screens that belong to one part of the app:
- **Purchase**: Vendor codes, Rebate programmes.
- **Accounting**: Payment collection, Chart of accounts, Close the books, Revenue & Close, Month-end checklist.
- **People**: Holidays & leave, Biometric terminals, Attendance locations, Expense approvals, Salary structure, Wins, awards & prizes, TV screens.
- **IT assets & logistics**: Transporters.
- **Reception**: Reception tablets.
- **Marketing**: Templates & journeys, Suppression list.
Some of these live inside their module but are listed in Settings so you can find them. Each module's own guide explains them.

## Q: Who can open the module settings screens?
A: Each screen has its own permission. Examples: Chart of accounts needs **Change the chart of accounts**; Close the books needs **Lock periods and close the year**; Revenue & Close and Month-end checklist need **Run the month-end close**; Holidays & leave, Attendance locations and Expense approvals need **Manage people records**; Salary structure needs **Run payroll**; Wins needs **Run wins, awards and prizes**; TV screens needs **Put Deskzo on a TV**; Reception tablets needs **Set up reception tablets**; Templates & journeys and Suppression list need **Build campaigns**. Payment collection and Vendor codes need **Change organisation settings**.

## Q: What is in the Data & continuity section?
A: This section has:
- **Import & export** — your data in a spreadsheet, and a spreadsheet back in. Opens with any import or export permission.
- **Old CRM migration** — bring an old CRM's data across (super admin only).
- **Backups** — copies of the database (needs **Take database backups**).
- **Maintenance mode** — take the app down for everyone except admins.
- **Yours** — What I can do, My profile, Notification preferences and the Deskzo app.
The Security and Data guide covers import, export and backups.

## Q: How do I put Deskzo into maintenance mode?
A: 1. Go to Settings → Maintenance mode.
2. Choose **Now** or **At a time I choose**.
3. Choose how long: "Until I switch it off" or until a time.
4. Write what people are told, and check the preview.
5. Click **Switch maintenance mode on now** or **Schedule it**.
Everybody except admins sees the maintenance page. A scheduled maintenance shows a banner from a day before. Click **Switch it off — bring the app back** to end it. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission.

## Q: How do I see what permissions I have?
A: Go to Settings → What I can do. Every user can open it. It lists every permission your account holds and where each one came from (your role, or an exception made for you), and which records you can reach.

## Q: Where do I change my own name, photo, password or two-factor?
A: Go to Settings → My profile. Every user can open it. You can change your own name, photo, password and two-factor there.

## Q: How do I choose which notifications I receive?
A: Go to Settings → Notification preferences. Every user can choose which notices reach them and how. This needs the Notifications module to be switched on.

## Q: What is the Deskzo app setting?
A: Settings → Deskzo app lets you pair your phone with the Deskzo app. Calls with customers then log themselves, and Call buttons ring your phone. It appears only when the Calls module is on and the app is available for your workspace.

## Q: How do I set up a TV to show the wins board?
A: Go to Settings → TV screens. Pair a TV with the code it shows, choose its branch, or take it off. A TV shows only the wins board, needs no sign-in and takes no seat. You need the **Put Deskzo on a TV** permission and the Sales Wins module.

## Q: I can't find a setting I need.
A: Use the search box at the top of the Settings page. If the screen is still missing: you may not have its permission, or its module may be switched off in Settings → Modules, or the module may not be in your plan. Some screens, like Old CRM migration and Unmasking logs, are for the super admin only. Ask your workspace admin.

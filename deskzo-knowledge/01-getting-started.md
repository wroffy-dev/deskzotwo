# Getting started with Deskzo — Deskzo

Deskzo is a business suite. A company signs up on the Deskzo website, gets its own workspace at its own web address, and the person who signs up becomes the workspace owner. After the first sign-in, a short "Getting started" wizard and the dashboard's **Getting Started** tab help each person finish setup. This applies to every Deskzo product (Deskzo One, CRM, Books, People and the others).

## Q: How do I sign up my company for Deskzo?
A: You set up a new workspace on the Deskzo website.
1. On the Deskzo website, choose the sign-up button. The page is called **Set up your workspace**.
2. Fill in the form: **Registered business name**, **Your address** (your workspace address), **Your name**, **Work email**, **Password**, **Country**, and optionally **Your kind of business**.
3. If the form asks for an **Invitation code**, enter the code from your invitation.
4. If a Deskzo partner gave you a code, open **Have a partner code?** and type it.
5. Read the privacy notice line and choose **Continue**.
6. Deskzo emails you a 6-digit code. Type it in **Code** and choose **Set up my workspace**.
7. Wait on the page while Deskzo sets up your workspace. It usually takes under a minute. You go straight into your new workspace, already signed in.

## Q: How do I create a new Deskzo account for my business?
A: A new business account in Deskzo is called a workspace. Go to the Deskzo website and choose sign up. Fill in your registered business name, your workspace address, your name, work email, a password of at least 10 characters and your country. Choose **Continue**. Enter the 6-digit code that Deskzo emails you, then choose **Set up my workspace**. You become the owner of the new workspace, with every permission.

## Q: What should I type in "Registered business name" when signing up?
A: Type your business name exactly as it is on your GST registration or certificate of incorporation, for example "Acme Technologies Pvt Ltd". Deskzo makes your workspace address from this name. If the name is empty or too short, the form shows a message under the field, such as "Enter your registered business name — exactly as on your GST registration or certificate of incorporation."

## Q: How do I choose my workspace address when signing up?
A: The workspace address is your company's own web address on Deskzo.
- Deskzo fills in **Your address** from your registered business name. You can change it.
- The address must use at least 8 letters or digits, and it must come from the words of your registered name, in order. Example: for "Acme Technologies Pvt Ltd", "acmetechnologies" works.
- While you type, Deskzo checks the address. A green line says the address "is free". A red line explains the problem, for example "That name is taken."
- If your invitation code was made with an address for you, the address is filled in and locked. The form says "This address was set up for you with your invitation."

## Q: Why does sign-up say my address must come from my registered business name?
A: Deskzo builds workspace addresses from the registered business name, so each company gets an address that matches its real name. The message "Your address must come from your registered business name, in order" means the address you typed uses words that are not in your registered name, or not in the same order. Use the words of the registered name in order, with at least 8 letters or digits in total.

## Q: I did not get the sign-up verification code. What can I do?
A: The 6-digit sign-up code is sent to the work email you typed.
1. Check your spam or junk folder.
2. On the code screen, choose **Send a new code**. The old code stops working.
3. If the email address was wrong, choose **Change your details**. Everything you typed is still there. Fix the email and choose **Continue** again.

## Q: My sign-up code does not work. Why?
A: The sign-up code works for 15 minutes, and you have 5 tries.
- "That code has expired" means more than 15 minutes have passed. Choose **Send a new code**.
- "That isn't the code we sent" means the digits are wrong. Use the code from the latest email.
- After 5 wrong codes the code stops working. Choose **Send a new code**.
- "This signup has expired — start again, and we'll send you a new code" means you need to fill in the form again.

## Q: Why does sign-up say I need an invitation code?
A: At times, setting up a new Deskzo workspace is by invitation only. Then the **Invitation code** field is required. Enter the code from your invitation email or link. If you do not have one, ask Deskzo for one: [CONTACT: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: What is "Your kind of business" on the sign-up form?
A: "Your kind of business" is optional. If you pick an industry, Deskzo starts your workspace with a ready pipeline, order steps, words and fields that suit that business. You can change all of it later, or pick one later in Settings. Choose "Not now — I'll set things up myself" to skip it.

## Q: Can I sign up for only one product, like Deskzo Books?
A: Yes. If you start sign-up from a product's page, the form shows "Your free trial:" with that product's name. To try every product instead, choose **Try every product instead**. Deskzo Books and Deskzo People are sold only in India. If you choose another country with these products, the form says the product is sold only in India and offers to try every product instead.

## Q: I was invited to join my company's Deskzo. How do I accept the invitation?
A: When an admin adds you, Deskzo emails you a setup link with the subject "Set up your account in" followed by your company's workspace name.
1. Open the link in the email. It works once, for three days.
2. On the page "Set up your account: choose a password", type a **New password** of at least 10 characters.
3. Type it again in **The same again**.
4. Choose **Set my password**.
5. Choose **Sign in** and sign in with your email and the new password.
If the link has run out, ask the person who added you to send a new one. Admins see a **Resend setup email** link next to your name.

## Q: My invitation link says it has expired. What do I do?
A: An invitation (setup) link works once, for three days. The message "This link has expired or been used" means you need a new one. Ask your workspace admin to choose **Resend setup email** for you in **Settings → Staff → Users**. The earlier link stops working when a new one is sent.

## Q: What happens the first time I sign in to Deskzo?
A: After the first sign-in you land on the dashboard. If your admin requires two-factor sign-in, or gave you a temporary password, Deskzo first takes you to **My profile** to set those up. Then the **Getting started** wizard opens by itself. It opens once per sign-in until you finish. The dashboard also shows a **Getting Started** tab with the same steps.

## Q: What is the Getting started wizard?
A: The Getting started wizard is a window that walks you through setup, one step at a time. Each step has its own small form. Steps are grouped as **Your company** (done once, for everybody) and **You** (just for your account). Buttons in the wizard:
- **Save and continue** saves the step and moves on.
- **Skip for now** skips an optional step.
- **Finish later** closes the wizard. Your typing is kept while the page stays open.
When all steps are finished, choose **Go to your dashboard**.

## Q: What are the Getting started steps?
A: Company steps (only for people who can change company settings):
- **Fill in your company profile** — legal name, GSTIN, state and registered address. Required.
- **Add your logo** — optional.
- **Bring in your team** — add people and give them roles. Optional.
- **Add what you sell** — products and services with prices and tax rates. Optional, shown only if Items is on.
- **Add your company's own guides** — your own how-tos. Optional.
Personal steps (for everybody):
- **Add your photo** — optional.
- **Turn on two-factor sign-in** — required.

## Q: Which Getting started steps are required?
A: Two steps are required and cannot be skipped: **Fill in your company profile** and **Turn on two-factor sign-in**. All other steps are optional. A skipped step counts as finished, and you can do it any time later. For example, the wizard says "You skipped this — it counts as finished, and you can do it any time from Settings → Branding."

## Q: What do I need to fill in for the company profile step?
A: The company profile step asks for **Legal name**, **GSTIN**, **Registered address**, **Country**, **State or region**, **City** and **Postal code**. In India, state and a 6-digit PIN code are needed, because GST follows the state. The GSTIN is optional — leave it blank if you are not registered for GST. If you type a GSTIN, it must be a valid 15-character GSTIN. You can change these details later in **Settings → Profile**.

## Q: How do I set up two-factor sign-in during Getting started?
A: Two-factor sign-in adds a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone to your password.
1. Install an authenticator app, such as Microsoft Authenticator or Google Authenticator.
2. In the wizard's **Two-factor sign-in** step, or on **My profile**, choose **Set up two-factor**.
3. Type your current password and choose **Continue**.
4. Scan the QR code with the authenticator app. If you cannot scan, type the key shown.
5. Type the 6-digit code from the app and choose **Confirm**.

## Q: Where is the Getting Started tab on the dashboard?
A: The **Getting Started** tab is the first tab on the dashboard, next to **Dashboard** and **Recent Updates**, until you finish setup. It shows your progress and each step with a button to do it. Choose **Continue setup** to open the wizard at the first step still to do. When every step is finished, the Getting Started tab goes away for good.

## Q: How do I reopen the Getting started wizard after I closed it?
A: Go to the dashboard and open the **Getting Started** tab. Choose **Continue setup** (or **Finish setup** when everything is done). The wizard opens at the first step still to do.

## Q: Why is a step already ticked in Getting Started?
A: Getting Started steps are never ticked by hand. Deskzo ticks a step when the data shows it is done, for example when the company profile is saved, a second person is added, or a photo is uploaded. If another admin already did a company step, it shows as **Done** for you too.

## Q: I can't see some Getting Started steps. Why?
A: Getting Started shows only the steps you can act on. The company steps (profile, logo, team, items) appear only for people with the **Change organisation settings** permission. The guides step needs the **Manage your company's guides and news** permission. The "Add what you sell" step appears only when the Items module is on. Everybody sees the photo and two-factor steps.

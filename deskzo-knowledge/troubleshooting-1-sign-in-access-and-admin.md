# Troubleshooting: Sign-in, Access and Admin — Deskzo

Common Deskzo problems with signing in, missing menus, permissions, seats, plans and the AI copilot. Each answer gives the message you see, what it means, and how to fix it.

## Q: I can't sign in to Deskzo. What should I check?
A: 1. Check you are on your company's own Deskzo workspace address.
2. Check your email and password. Use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page if needed.
3. If your company uses Microsoft, Google or Zoho sign-in, use the **Sign in with …** button.
4. If Deskzo asks for a two-factor code, use a fresh code from your authenticator app.
5. If it still fails, ask your Deskzo admin to check your account is switched on.

## Q: Deskzo says "Too many attempts. Try again in N minutes." What does it mean?
A: After 8 failed Deskzo sign-ins within 10 minutes, sign-in is locked for that account or network for 15 minutes. Wait and try again, or use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. Setting a new password clears the lock.

## Q: Deskzo says "Invalid email, password, or authenticator code."
A: The email, password or two-factor code is wrong. Check your password. For the two-factor code, make sure your phone's time is set to automatic and use a fresh 6-digit code. If you lost your phone, ask your Deskzo admin to reset your two-factor sign-in.

## Q: Deskzo says "That code didn't match. Check the time on your phone and try again."
A: This appears while setting up two-factor sign-in in Deskzo. The code from your authenticator app was wrong, usually because the phone clock is off. Set your phone's date and time to automatic, then type the newest code.

## Q: Deskzo says "This link has expired or been used. Ask for a new one from the sign-in page."
A: A Deskzo password reset link works for 1 hour and only once. A setup link for a new user works for 3 days. Request a new reset link from the sign-in page. If you are a new user and your setup email expired, ask your admin to resend the setup email.

## Q: Deskzo says "Your account signs in with Microsoft — use that button above."
A: Your Deskzo account is not allowed to sign in with a password. Your admin requires single sign-on. Use the **Sign in with Microsoft** (or Google or Zoho) button on the sign-in page.

## Q: Deskzo says "Waiting for this device to be approved", "This network is blocked" or "Your access is locked".
A: Your Deskzo admin has set access rules in Settings → Security, such as approved devices or allowed networks. Ask your admin to approve your device or network, or to unlock your access. Then choose **Check again**.

## Q: I can't see a module or page in my Deskzo menu. Why?
A: A Deskzo menu link only shows when all three are true:
1. The module is in your company's plan.
2. An admin has switched the module on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. Your role has the permission for it in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**.
Ask your Deskzo admin to check. Also, your company may have renamed the menu label in Settings → Wording.

## Q: Deskzo says "<Module> isn't part of this workspace's plan."

## Q: Deskzo says "This module is currently disabled. Ask an admin to enable it under Settings."
A: The Deskzo module is in your plan but switched off. An admin can switch it on in **Settings → Modules**.

## Q: A Deskzo button is missing or greyed out for me. Why?
A: Most Deskzo actions need a permission, such as approving, deleting or exporting. If a button is missing or greyed out, your role probably does not have that permission. You can see your own permissions from Settings. Ask your Deskzo admin to give your role the permission in Settings → Roles & Permissions.

## Q: Deskzo says "This workspace's plan includes N users, and every seat is taken."
A: Every Deskzo seat on your plan is in use, so a new user cannot be added or switched on. Fix: switch off a user who has left (Settings → Users → the person → Login & Access → **Switch off**), or ask the workspace owner to add seats in Settings → Plan & billing. A switched-off user does not use a seat. On a free trial the message says the trial includes N users.

## Q: Deskzo says "A user with this email already exists."
A: A Deskzo user with that email is already in the workspace, maybe switched off. Find the existing user in Settings → Users and switch the account on instead of adding a new one.

## Q: Deskzo says "Only a super admin can change somebody's role."
A: In Deskzo, only the super admin (the workspace owner's account) can change a person's role. Ask the super admin.

## Q: Deskzo says "You don't hold this yourself, so you can't change it for a role."
A: In Deskzo, an admin cannot give a role a permission the admin does not have. Ask someone who holds that permission, or the super admin, to make the change.

## Q: Deskzo says "<Name> holds permissions you don't, so you can't view the app as them. A super admin can."
A: Deskzo's View as user only works for people whose permissions you also hold. Ask the super admin to view the app as that person.

## Q: Deskzo says "The workspace is held until a plan is paid for. Nothing has been deleted — paying opens it again at once."
A: The Deskzo free trial ended or a payment failed. Your data is safe. The workspace owner or billing manager pays in **Settings → Plan & billing**, and the workspace opens again at once.

## Q: Deskzo says "That file is over 25MB. Split it and import in batches." or "Use a .csv or .xlsx file."
A: Deskzo imports accept .csv and .xlsx files up to 25 MB. Save your file in one of those formats, or split it into smaller files and import them one by one.

## Q: Deskzo says "That image is …KB — keep it under 96KB."
A: Your Deskzo profile photo is too large. Crop it square and compress it so it is under 96 KB, then upload it again on My profile.

## Q: Deskzo says "The copilot is switched off. An admin can switch it on in Settings → Deskzo AI."
A: Deskzo AI (the AI copilot) is turned off for your workspace. An admin can turn it on in **Settings → Deskzo AI**.

## Q: Deskzo says "You don't have access to the AI copilot."
A: Your Deskzo role does not have the **Use the AI copilot** permission. Ask your admin to add it in Settings → Roles & Permissions.

## Q: Deskzo says "This workspace has used all its Deskzo AI credits."
A: Your Deskzo workspace has used its AI credits for now. Whoever manages billing can buy a credit pack in **Settings → Plan & billing**. For credit prices, see [PRICING: to be filled by Deskzo team].

## Q: Deskzo says "Only workspace admins can use Automate for now."
A: Deskzo Automate is currently open only to people with the **Change organisation settings** permission. Ask your Deskzo admin to build the workflow, or to give you that permission.

## Q: Deskzo is not loading or a page shows an error. What should I do?
A: 1. Refresh the page.
2. Check your internet connection. Deskzo needs to be online.
3. Sign out and sign in again.
4. Try another browser.

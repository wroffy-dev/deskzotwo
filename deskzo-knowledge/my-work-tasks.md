# Tasks — Deskzo

Tasks in Deskzo are to-dos that you give to a person, with a due date, a priority and a status. You can see Tasks as a list or as a board, and add checklists, files, time logged and comments. Tasks is part of every Deskzo product and sits in the sidebar under My work → Tasks.

## Q: What is the Tasks feature in Deskzo?
A: Tasks is where you write down work that somebody has to do, give it to a person and follow it until it is done. Each Deskzo task has a status (To do, In progress, Need help, Complete or Cancelled), a priority (Low, Medium, High or Urgent) and a due date. A task can also have a start date, an estimate, a team, a checklist, files, time logged and comments. A task can be about a company, a lead or a ticket. Then the task also shows on that record's page.

## Q: How do I create a task?
A: To create a task in Deskzo:
1. Go to **My work → Tasks**.
2. Click **New task**.
3. Type the **Task name**. You can add a **Description** of what "done" looks like.
4. Choose who it is **Assigned to**, or leave it as **Nobody yet**.
5. Set the **Due date**, **Priority** and **Status**. Add a **Team** or **Challenge** if needed.
6. Optional: open **Start & estimate**, **XP & badge**, **Repeat**, **Reminder**, **About** or **Checklist** to add more details. In **Checklist**, type one step per line.
7. Click **Create task**.
The person you assigned gets a notification.

## Q: How do I assign a task to a colleague?
A: In Deskzo, open **My work → Tasks** and click **New task**. Type the **Task name**, then pick the colleague in **Assigned to**. Set the **Due date** and **Priority**, then click **Create task**. The colleague gets a notification. Everybody in Deskzo can create tasks and give them to anyone, unless an admin has limited it for their role.

## Q: Can I add a task from a customer, lead or ticket?
A: Yes. A company, lead or ticket page in Deskzo has a Tasks card. Add the task there and it is linked to that record. The task then shows on the record's page and in **My work → Tasks**. You can also link a task to a record from the **About** part of the task form. You can also add a task from My tasks in the side rail.

## Q: How do I change the status of a task?
A: To move a Deskzo task along:
1. Open the task in **My work → Tasks**.
2. Choose To do, In progress, Need help, Complete or Cancelled from the status menu at the top.
Or switch to the board view (the **Board** button next to the **List** button) and drag the task into another column. On a ticket, company or lead page, use the **Status** menu on the task's row in its Tasks section.

## Q: Why does Deskzo ask for a note when I choose Need help or Cancelled?
A: In Deskzo Tasks, Need help and Cancelled always need a note. Need help asks "What do you need help with?". Cancelled asks "Why is it cancelled?". The note is saved as a comment on the task. The person who set the task and the person it is assigned to are both told. If you leave the note empty, Deskzo shows "Say what help you need." or "Say why it's cancelled."

## Q: What does Upcoming mean on a task?
A: A Deskzo task shows as Upcoming when its start date is still in the future. You set the start date in the **Start & estimate** part of the task form. On the start date the task shows normally again. A task cannot start after it is due; Deskzo shows "A task can't start after it's due."

## Q: How do I add a checklist, file, time or comment to a task?
A: Open the task in **My work → Tasks**. Inside the task you can:
- Tick off **Checklist** steps, or type in **Add a step** to add a new one.
- Click **Attach a file**. Each file can be up to 10 MB.
- Log time: enter the **Minutes**, the day and what it was spent on, then click **Log time**. You can log between 1 minute and 24 hours at a time, on a day that has already happened.
- Write a comment and click **Comment**.
- Star the task, so you can filter to your starred tasks later.

## Q: What files can I attach to a task?
A: A Deskzo task accepts pictures, PDFs, office documents, text files and zip files. Each file can be up to 10 MB. If the file is too big, Deskzo shows "Files on a task can be up to 10 MB." Only the person who attached a file can remove it.

## Q: How do I set a reminder on a task?
A: To get reminded about a Deskzo task:
1. Open the task.
2. Under **Reminder**, pick a time in **Remind me at**.
3. Choose **How often**: **Once**, or every hour, every day or every week until it's done.
4. Click **Remind me**.
Task reminders are only for you. They come as a notification, with a sound if your sounds are on. A closed task never reminds you. The time must be in the future, or Deskzo shows "Set the reminder for a time still to come."

## Q: How do I make a task repeat every week or month?
A: In the Deskzo task form, open **Repeat**. Choose **Every day**, **Every week** or **Every month**. You can also set **Until (optional)** to stop repeating on a date. When the task is marked Complete, Deskzo makes the next task a day, week or month later, with its checklist unticked.

## Q: What is XP on a task?
A: XP is points a person earns by finishing a Deskzo task on or before its due date. To set it, open **XP & badge** in the task form and set **Earned XP** and, if you like, a **Badge**. XP earned shows on the Task XP board in **Reports → Performance**. Nobody can set XP on a task assigned to themselves, so nobody awards themselves points.

## Q: I finished a task but did not get its XP. Why?
A: In Deskzo, XP is earned only when all of these are true: the task is marked Complete on or before its due date, it is completed by the person it is assigned to, and somebody other than that person set the XP. If a task is moved back from Complete, its XP is taken back.

## Q: Why can't I set XP on my own task?
A: Deskzo does not let anyone set XP on a task assigned to themselves. The form shows "It's your task — its XP is for somebody else to set." Ask the person who gave you the task, or your manager, to set the XP.

## Q: What are Challenges in Tasks?
A: A Challenge groups Deskzo tasks under one shared goal. To make one:
1. In **My work → Tasks**, click **Challenges**, then **New challenge**.
2. Give it a **Name**, a **Team**, **Starts** and **Ends** dates, and **What it's for**.
3. Save it, then pick it as the **Challenge** in each task.
Each challenge shows how many of its tasks are done and how much XP has been earned. Only the person who made a challenge can change or archive it.

## Q: How do I find a task?
A: **My work → Tasks** opens on the tasks assigned to you. Change **Assigned to** to **Everyone**, **Set by me**, **Unassigned** or a person. You can also filter by **Status**, **Priority**, **Team**, **Challenge**, **Starred** and the due date. Use the search box to search tasks by name.

## Q: Why can't I edit a task that is assigned to me?
A: In Deskzo, a task given to you by somebody who does not report to you (for example your manager or a peer) is yours to work on, not to rewrite. You can still change its status, tick checklist steps, comment, attach files and log time. Only the person who set the task can change its details or delete it. Ask them to make the change.

## Q: Why can't I delete a task?
A: In Deskzo you can delete tasks you created. To delete somebody else's task you need the **Delete any task** permission, which the Management role has by default. A task given to you by your manager or a peer stays theirs to delete. Without the permission, Deskzo shows "You don't have permission to delete this task." Deleting a task also deletes its checklist, files, time and comments.

## Q: Who can see and change tasks in Deskzo?
A: By default, everybody in Deskzo can see and change the company's tasks and delete their own. An admin can narrow whose tasks a role can reach. Go to **Settings → Roles & Permissions**, open the role, and use the **Record Access** tab. Deleting other people's tasks needs the **Delete any task** permission.

## Q: Do tasks show in my calendar?
A: Yes. Open Deskzo tasks that are assigned to you and have a due date show on **My work → Calendar**, next to your meetings. They are read-only there. Open the task in **My work → Tasks** to change it.

## Q: Is there anything to set up before using Tasks?
A: No. Deskzo Tasks is ready as soon as the module is on in **Settings → Modules**. Two things help:
- The task **Team** list is your departments. An admin sets these up in **Settings → Departments**.
- Who reports to whom comes from each person's reporting manager in **Settings → Users**. This decides who can edit a task given to them.

## Q: Should I use a task or a sticky note?
A: Use a Deskzo task when somebody has to do something by a date. Use a sticky note (**My work → Sticky Notes**) for something worth remembering that is nobody's job. A sticky note has no due date and no assignee.

## Q: I can't see Tasks in my menu. What should I do?
A: If **My work → Tasks** is missing in Deskzo, check three things with your admin:
1. Tasks must be in your company's Deskzo plan.
2. The Tasks module must be switched on in **Settings → Modules**.
3. Your role must have the **My work › Tasks** section permission ticked in **Settings → Roles & Permissions**. Every role has it unless an admin unticked it.

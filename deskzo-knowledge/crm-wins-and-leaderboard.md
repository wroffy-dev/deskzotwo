# Sales Wins and Leaderboard — Deskzo

Sales Wins celebrates good news automatically: a big deal won, a target reached, a new customer's first order and the month's top performer. It includes a monthly leaderboard, fortnightly "most active" awards, prizes, a hall of fame and a TV screen for the sales floor. Sales Wins is part of Deskzo CRM and Deskzo One, and appears in the sidebar as Sales → Wins & leaderboard.

## Q: What is Sales Wins?
A: Sales Wins in Deskzo makes good news visible without anybody announcing it. When a big deal is won, a target is reached or a new customer places their first order, Deskzo celebrates it with a full-screen splash and confetti for everybody, or for the winner alone with a line in everybody else's greeting strip.

## Q: What does the wins wall show?
A: Go to Sales → Wins & leaderboard. The wall shows the month so far: what is booked against the target, deals won, new customers, targets reached, this month's **Leaderboard** and the **Recent wins** of the last 30 days. The leaderboard ranks orders booked this month at the selling price, before GST.

## Q: Who can see the wins wall?
A: Everybody can open the wins wall, the **Most active** tab and the **Hall of fame**. The point is that the whole team sees the win. On Most active, everybody sees their own points; the full ranking needs the **View team performance** permission.

## Q: I can't see Wins & leaderboard in my menu. Why?
A: Check these with your admin:
1. Sales Wins must be in your company's plan (Deskzo CRM or Deskzo One).
2. The Sales Wins module must be switched on in Settings → Modules.
3. The **Sales › Sales Wins** section must be ticked for your role in Settings → Roles & Permissions.

## Q: Why don't I see the Prizes & settings tab?
A: The **Prizes & settings** tab is only for people with the **Run wins, awards and prizes** permission (Management by default). Everybody else sees the wall, Most active and Hall of fame.

## Q: How do I choose what gets celebrated?
A: You need the **Run wins, awards and prizes** permission.
1. Go to Sales → Wins & leaderboard and click **Prizes & settings**.
2. Under **What gets celebrated**, tick the moments: **A big deal won** (and the smallest deal worth celebrating), **A target reached** (and which targets), **A new customer's first order** and **Top performer of the month** (and how many to name, 1 to 10).
3. For each, choose **Splash for everybody** or **Splash for the winner, strip for everybody else**.
4. Click **Save**.
Each win is celebrated once, for two days (three for the top performer). Click **Look for wins now** to check straight away.

## Q: How do I set up celebrations for big deals?
A: Go to Sales → Wins & leaderboard → **Prizes & settings**. Under **What gets celebrated**, tick **A big deal won** and enter the smallest deal amount worth celebrating. Choose how loud the splash is and click **Save**. A deal counts at its latest proposal value (or its estimate if there is no proposal), before GST.

## Q: A big deal was won but nothing was celebrated. Why?
A: Check that **A big deal won** is ticked in Prizes & settings and the deal was worth at least the smallest amount set. The value is the deal's latest proposal, or its estimate when there is no proposal, before GST. The deal must also be in a pipeline that counts as sales. Switching a moment on never celebrates anything older than two days. Use **Look for wins now** to check at once.

## Q: Can I hide rupee amounts on the wall and TV?
A: Yes. In Prizes & settings, switch off **Show rupee amounts on the wall and the TV**. The wall and TV then show only ranks and progress to target. This is useful when the sales floor should not see each other's numbers.

## Q: What are the fortnightly most-active awards?
A: Every fortnight Deskzo names the most active people, counted from records, calls, visits, tickets and deals. Awards are announced on the 1st and the 16th, from 9 am, for the fortnight just finished. The **Most active** tab shows your points so far, past fortnights and how points work. Work done while an admin is viewing as somebody earns no points.

## Q: How do I turn on the most-active awards?
A: In Sales → Wins & leaderboard → **Prizes & settings**, under **Most active of the fortnight**:
1. Tick **Announce the most active every fortnight**.
2. Choose **Who hears about it**: **Everybody**, **Managers only** or **Only the winners**.
3. Set how many to name overall (1 to 10), and whether there is a **Full-screen splash with confetti**.
4. Click **Save**.
A change applies from the next announcement.

## Q: Why can I only see my own points on Most active?
A: The full ranking as it builds up is only for people with the **View team performance** permission. Everybody sees their own points, and the winners once they are announced (if the awards are told to everybody).

## Q: How do I set prizes?
A: In **Prizes & settings**, find the prizes section for top sellers of the month or most active of the fortnight.
1. Leave **Standing — every month** (or fortnight) for a prize given every time, or pick one month or fortnight for a special prize that replaces the standing one.
2. For each place, type the prize, add a note if you like, use **Add a picture**, and click **Save**.
Everybody is told automatically what is up for grabs when each period opens. If you change prizes mid-way, click **Announce now**. A period that is over cannot change its prizes.

## Q: How do I mark a prize as handed over?
A: Open the **Hall of fame** tab. It lists every month's top sellers and every fortnight's most active, with what they won. Click **Mark handed over** beside the winner. Everybody then sees **Handed over** and the date instead of **On its way**. You need the **Run wins, awards and prizes** permission.

## Q: How do I show the wins board on a TV?
A: You need the **Put Deskzo on a TV** permission.
1. On the TV's browser, open your workspace's web address followed by /tv. The TV shows a six-digit code and a QR code.
2. In Settings → TV screens (or click **Put on a TV** on the wins wall), type the code in **Code on the TV**, or scan the QR code with your phone.
3. Give the screen a **Name**, choose **Shows the wins of**: **The whole company** or one branch, and click **Pair TV**.
The TV opens the board by itself and refreshes every minute. Nobody signs in on it and it uses no seat.

## Q: The TV shows a code again instead of the wins board.
A: The TV was removed in Settings → TV screens. Pair it again with the code it now shows. If the TV says the wins board is switched off, the Sales Wins module has been switched off for your company.

## Q: How do I show the board on a meeting-room screen where I'm signed in?
A: On the wins wall, click **Open the TV screen**. The board opens full screen in a new browser tab.

## Q: Where does the target figure on the wall come from?
A: From Targets. Set order value targets for your salespeople in Targets. "A target reached" is measured exactly as Targets measures it, for a person, a team or the company.

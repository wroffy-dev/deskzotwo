# Troubleshooting: People, My Work and Tools — Deskzo

Common Deskzo messages in Attendance, Leave, Hiring, Visitors, IT Assets, Tasks, Chat, Sticky Notes, Calendar, Automate, Renewals, Reports, Incentives, the Credential Vault and Email Signatures.

## Q: Deskzo says "You're already clocked in today."
A: You have already clocked in on Deskzo today. If your attendance is wrong, ask for an attendance correction from People → My HR.

## Q: Deskzo says "You're on approved leave today. Cancel the leave first if you're working."
A: Deskzo blocks clock-in on a day of approved leave. If you are working, withdraw the leave first (only before it starts), or ask HR to correct it.

## Q: Deskzo says "You are outside the permitted office area."
A: Deskzo GPS attendance only allows clock-in inside your office area. Move inside the area, or choose **Ask for an exception** so your manager can approve it.

## Q: Deskzo says "Your location accuracy is insufficient. Please try again in an open area."
A: Your phone's location is not accurate enough for Deskzo attendance. Move to an open area and try again. If it still fails, choose **Ask for an exception**.

## Q: Deskzo says "Your phone reports a simulated location."
A: A location-changing (mock location) app is on. Turn it off, then clock in on Deskzo again.

## Q: Deskzo says "That's N day(s) but you have M left of <leave type>".
A: You do not have enough Deskzo leave balance. Leave requests still pending also count against your balance. If the balance looks wrong, ask HR.

## Q: Deskzo says "That leave has already started — ask HR to correct it."
A: In Deskzo, you can only withdraw leave before it starts. Ask HR to correct leave that has already started.

## Q: Deskzo says "Mark the offer accepted first".
A: In Deskzo Hiring, only a candidate who accepted the offer can become an employee. Select **Offer accepted**, then **Convert to employee**.

## Q: The reception tablet shows "This tablet isn't set up. Please ask at the desk."
A: The Deskzo reception tablet link is out of service, deleted or replaced. Open the current link from **Settings → Reception tablets** on the tablet.

## Q: Deskzo says "This needs an e-way bill before it moves."
A: In Deskzo IT Assets, the consignment needs an e-way bill before dispatch. Raise an e-way bill, or enter its number, then dispatch.

## Q: Deskzo says "You don't have permission to delete this task."
A: Somebody else created the Deskzo task. Only the creator, or someone with the **Delete any task** permission, can delete it. Ask the creator.

## Q: Deskzo says "Files on a task can be up to 10 MB."
A: Each file on a Deskzo task can be up to 10 MB. Compress or split the file.

## Q: Deskzo says "Messages can be edited for 15 minutes after sending."
A: In Deskzo Chat, you can edit a message for 15 minutes and delete it for everyone for an hour after sending. After that it cannot be changed.

## Q: Deskzo says "Their account is switched off, so a new chat can't be started with them."
A: The colleague's Deskzo account is switched off. You can still read your old chat with them, but you cannot start a new one.

## Q: Deskzo says "You can't put a note on everybody's board."
A: Posting a Deskzo sticky note to Everyone needs the **Post a note to everyone** permission. Share it with **My team** instead, or ask your admin.

## Q: Deskzo says "A meeting runs between five minutes and a day."
A: A Deskzo meeting must be at least five minutes and at most one day long. Change the start or end time.

## Q: Deskzo says "The time to decide has run out; the workflow went the way it was set to go."
A: A Deskzo Automate approval step timed out, so the workflow took its default path. Nothing more is needed from you.

## Q: Deskzo says "Already renewed by <order>. Cancel that first if it was wrong."
A: A renewal order already exists for this Deskzo renewal. If that order was a mistake, cancel it first, then punch the renewal again.

## Q: Deskzo says "You can read this report but not download it."
A: Downloading financial or customer data from Deskzo Reports needs **Export financial data** or **Export customer data**. Ask your admin for the permission.

## Q: Deskzo says "Somebody saved this report after you opened it."
A: Two people edited the same Deskzo report. Reload the page to see their changes, then redo your own changes.

## Q: Deskzo says "This scheme pays once it's all collected."
A: The Deskzo incentive scheme only pays when invoices are fully collected. Wait until the money is in, then mark the incentive paid.

## Q: Deskzo says "You can't decide your own incentive — somebody else has to."
A: In Deskzo, nobody can approve their own incentive. Another approver must decide it.

## Q: Deskzo says "That password isn't right." in the Credential Vault.
A: To open a Deskzo vault record, type your own Deskzo sign-in password, not the stored password. If you sign in only with Microsoft, Google or Zoho, you may see "This account has no password set" — ask an admin to set a password for your account.

## Q: Deskzo says "The picture is over 1 MB." for an email signature banner.
A: A Deskzo email signature banner is sent in every email, so it must be under 1 MB (a few hundred KB is best). Compress the PNG, JPEG or GIF image.

# Settings Tour, Part 1: Company, Staff, Security and Taxes — Deskzo

Settings is where a Deskzo workspace admin sets up the company: its details, branding, wording, modules, staff, security and tax settings. Settings is part of every Deskzo product. This part covers the "Organisation settings" groups Company, Staff, Security, and Taxes & compliance.

## Q: How do I open Settings in Deskzo?
A: Click Administration → Settings in the sidebar. The Settings page is a map of every setting, grouped under headings. Use the search box ("Search settings — mail, GST, roles, branding…") to find a screen by typing. A sidebar beside every settings page lists the same screens. You only see the screens you are allowed to open, and only screens for modules that are switched on.

## Q: Why does Settings say "There are no settings you can change"?
A: Every settings screen needs its own permission. Most general screens need the **Change organisation settings** permission. If you hold none of the permissions, Settings shows "There are no settings you can change." A screen you cannot open shows a "not found" page. Ask your workspace admin. Any user can still open Settings → What I can do and their own profile.

## Q: How are the Settings screens organised?
A: Settings has three sections:
- **Organisation settings**: Company, Staff, Security, Taxes & compliance, Customers & messaging, Leads, and Lists & taxonomy.
- **Module settings**: settings that belong to one part of the app — Purchase, Accounting, People, IT assets & logistics, Reception and Marketing.
- **Data & continuity**: Import & export, Backups, Maintenance mode, and "Yours" (your own access, profile, notifications and phone app).

## Q: What is in the Company group of Settings?
A: The Company group has:
- **Profile** — legal name, PAN, registered office, head-office GSTIN and bank accounts.
- **Branding** — the app's name, logo, colour and default theme.
- **Wording** — your own words for leads, customers, orders and more.
- **Industry templates** — a starting setup for your kind of business.
- **Letterhead** — logo and signature block on printed documents.
- **Plan & billing** — your plan, payments and invoices.
- **Custom domain** — reach the workspace at your own web address.
- **Modules** — which parts of the app are switched on.
- **Deskzo AI** — the AI copilot and its credits.
- **Your company's guides** and **Company news**.
- **Document emails** — the message sent with emailed proposals and invoices.

## Q: How do I change the company name, PAN, GSTIN or bank details?
A: Go to Settings → Profile. This screen holds the legal name, PAN, registered office, head-office GSTIN and bank accounts. These details print on every proposal, invoice and purchase order, unless a branch has its own. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission.

## Q: How do I change the logo and colours of Deskzo?
A: Go to Settings → Branding. You can change the app's own name, logo, colour and default theme. Changes apply everywhere as soon as you save. The logo on printed invoices is separate: set it in Settings → Letterhead. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission.

## Q: How do I rename "Leads" to "Enquiries" or "Customers" to "Students"?
A: Go to Settings → Wording. For each word (Lead, Company, Customer, Contact, Order, Item, Ticket, Vendor, Visit, Renewal) enter your own singular, plural and "a/an". You can also rename the order statuses. Click **Save the wording**. The menu, page titles and buttons then use your words. Everything works the same. To undo, use the button that goes back to the app's own words. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission.

## Q: What are industry templates?
A: Settings → Industry templates gives you a ready setup for your kind of business, such as system integrators, real estate, IT services, manufacturing or software resellers. A template applies a pipeline, order steps, wording and custom fields in one go. It adds and renames things; it never deletes. You can change every part afterwards.

## Q: How do I switch a module on or off?
A: 1. Go to Settings → Modules.
2. Find the module in the list.
3. Use the switch beside it to turn it on or off.
Switching a module off hides its pages and its place in the sidebar for everyone. The data stays, so switching it back on loses nothing. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission.

## Q: Why can't I switch on a module in Settings → Modules?
A: There are two reasons. A module marked **Not in your plan** is not part of your workspace's plan, so its switch is locked off. Only a plan change adds it; ask the workspace owner to upgrade in Settings → Plan & billing. A module marked **Core — always on**, such as Companies & Contacts, cannot be switched off.

## Q: A page says "This module is currently disabled". What does it mean?
A: The module is in your plan but an admin has switched it off. Ask an admin to switch it on in Settings → Modules. If the page says the module "isn't part of this workspace's plan", the workspace owner must upgrade the plan instead.

## Q: How do I use my own web address for Deskzo?
A: Go to Settings → Custom domain. Add an address such as erp.yourcompany.com, create the DNS records the screen shows, and click **Check now**. You can make a custom address the primary one or remove it. Only the workspace owner manages the custom domain. Others see "Only the workspace owner manages its custom domain."

## Q: How do I control Deskzo AI?
A: Go to Settings → Deskzo AI. You can switch the copilot on or off, switch drafting workflows with AI on or off, and set a daily credit cap for each person. The screen shows this month's credits and who used how many. People also need the **Use the AI copilot** permission. Credit packs are bought by whoever manages the plan and billing. ## Q: How do I add my company's own help guides and news?
A: Go to Settings → Your company's guides to add your own how-tos, SOPs and training videos to the help rail. Go to Settings → Company news to post news for everybody, now or at a time you choose. Both need the **Manage your company's guides and news** permission. Deskzo's own help and What's new are separate and cannot be changed here.

## Q: What is in the Staff group of Settings?
A: The Staff group has **Roles & Permissions**, **Users**, **Departments**, **Branches**, **Unmasking logs** (super admin only) and **Audit logs** (the Activity log). Roles, Users and Departments need the **Review who can do what** permission. Branches needs **Change organisation settings**. See the Users and Roles guide for details.

## Q: How do I set up branches and GST registrations?
A: Go to Settings → Branches. Add your offices, shops and warehouses. Each branch has the GSTIN it bills under, its address and bank. Mark which one is the head office, and set where each person works. Every document is raised from a branch and prints that branch's details. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission.

## Q: What is in the Security group of Settings?
A: The Security group has:
- **Security & data protection** — sign-in rules, two-factor, and copy and screenshot deterrents.
- **Devices, networks & sign-ins** — who may sign in on which device and from where.
- **Account locks** — lock one person (or, as super admin, the whole company) out behind a notice.
- **Chat** — whether chat files can be saved and how long messages are kept.
- **Word filter** — words kept out of chat and tickets.
See the Security and Data guide for details.

## Q: What is in the Taxes & compliance group of Settings?
A: This group has **e-Invoicing** (the IRP connection for IRN and QR code), **e-Way bills** (portal connection and in-state threshold), **Document numbering** (prefix and running number), **Document templates** (how each document looks on paper) and **Document approvals** (which documents need sign-off and who signs). They need the **Change organisation settings** permission. Numbering, templates and approvals appear only when Sales Documents or Purchase Documents is switched on. The Accounts and Sales Documents guides cover these screens in detail.

## Q: How do I change the letterhead on printed invoices?
A: Go to Settings → Letterhead. Set the logo and signature block at the top and bottom of printed documents. This is separate from Branding: the letterhead logo is your company's, while Branding changes the app itself. You need the **Change organisation settings** permission.

## Q: I can't see Settings in my menu.
A: The Settings link in Administration needs the **Change organisation settings** permission. Other admin links (Users, Roles & Permissions, Security & DLP, Import & export) have their own permissions. Also, a screen for a module only shows if that module is in your plan and switched on in Settings → Modules. Ask your workspace admin.

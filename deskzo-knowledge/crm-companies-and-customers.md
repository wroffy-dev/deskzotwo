# Companies, Customers and Contacts — Deskzo

Companies & Contacts is the Deskzo address book: every organisation you sell to, the people there, their offices and GST details, and one page per company with everything that happened with them. It is in every Deskzo workspace and cannot be switched off. The sidebar shows it under Sales as Companies, Customer, Duplicate companies and Mail log.

## Q: What is the difference between Companies and Customer in the menu?
A: Both lists show company records. Sales → Companies holds companies that have never ordered: prospects, companies with a lead, and won deals still waiting for their first order. Sales → Customer holds companies that have bought from you (at least one order on file, active or expired). A company moves from Companies to Customer by itself when its first order is placed. Your admin may have renamed "Companies" or "Customer" in Settings → Wording.

## Q: How do I add a new company?
A: To add a company in Deskzo:
1. Go to Sales → Companies and click **New company**.
2. Type the **Company name**. Companies with similar names are listed under the field. If it is the same company, open that one instead.
3. Fill in what you know: Industry, Company type, Number of employees, Website, Payment terms, Source and Tags.
4. Under **Primary location**, enter the office address. In India also add the **GST number** and **GST treatment**.
5. Under **Contacts**, add the first person and tick **Primary contact**. Click **+ Add contact** for more people.
6. Click **Save company**.
The company page opens, and you become its account manager.

## Q: How do I add a new customer or client?
A: In Deskzo a customer is a company. Go to Sales → Companies, click **New company**, type the **Company name**, add the address under **Primary location** (with GST number and GST treatment in India), add the first contact, and click **Save company**. The company moves to the Customer list by itself when its first order is placed.

## Q: What do Prospect, Lead and Awaiting Order mean on the Companies list?
A: These are company stages in Deskzo. A new company starts as **Prospect**. It becomes **Lead** when somebody opens a lead for it. It shows **Awaiting Order** when one of its leads is won but no order is placed yet. After the first order it moves to the Customer list. On Sales → Companies you can filter with the chips Prospects, Leads, Awaiting Order and Disqualified.

## Q: Why does a company we won still say Awaiting Order?
A: In Deskzo a won deal only becomes a customer once an order is on file. Until then the company stays on Sales → Companies with the stage Awaiting Order. When its first order is placed, it moves to Sales → Customer.

## Q: Who can see which companies?
A: In Deskzo, with the **See every account, not just your own** permission you see every company. Without it, you see the companies where you are account manager, and the accounts of anyone who reports to you. A company with no account manager is seen only by people who see every account. Leads, orders, payments, tickets and contacts under a company follow the same rule. Sales does not have "See every account" by default.

## Q: I added a company and now I can't find it, or I can't see a colleague's company.
A: If you do not have the **See every account, not just your own** permission, you only see companies where you (or your team) are account manager. Ask someone who can reassign accounts to make you the account manager, or ask your admin about your access.

## Q: It says "A company with this name already exists", but I can't find it.
A: Company names are unique across the whole Deskzo workspace, including vendors, resellers and commission parties. The existing company probably belongs to somebody else's accounts. Ask your admin to give you access to it instead of creating a second one.

## Q: I can't see Companies, Duplicate companies or Mail log in my menu.
A: Companies & Contacts is in every Deskzo plan and cannot be switched off in Settings → Modules. But:
- An admin can hide the **Sales › Companies & Contacts** section from your role in Settings → Roles & Permissions.
- **Duplicate companies** needs the **Merge duplicate companies** permission.
- **Mail log** needs the **View emails sent to customers** permission.
Without these, the links are not shown. Ask your admin.

## Q: How do I add or edit a contact person at a company?
A: Open the company and go to its **Contacts** tab.
1. Click **+ Add contact**.
2. Fill in Name, Designation, Email, Phone and LinkedIn URL.
3. Tick **Receives invoices & quotes** for people who should be sent documents, and **Primary contact** for the main person.
4. Click **Save contact**.
To change a person, click the pencil (**Edit contact**) on their row. You need the **View contacts** permission to see and edit contacts.

## Q: How do I check if a contact's email address is valid?
A: Open the company's **Contacts** tab and click the **Check ... addresses** button (it shows how many are not checked yet, for example "Check 3 addresses"). Deskzo checks each address against its domain's mail records and gives a verdict. The address itself is never changed.

## Q: How do I record that a contact has left the company?
A: On the company's **Contacts** tab, click the "has left" icon on the person's row. If you know where they went, tick **They've joined another company**, pick the company and add their new details. Then click **They've left** (or **Record the move**). The person stays on the company with their history, under **Left the company**. They are no longer primary contact, are not sent invoices or campaigns, and are not offered for meetings, calls or forms.

## Q: Why can't I delete a contact?
A: You see "This contact is linked to a lead and can't be deleted." A contact linked to a lead cannot be deleted in Deskzo. If the person has moved on, mark them as left on the company's Contacts tab instead.

## Q: How do I add another office, branch or GST registration for a company?
A: Open the company's **Locations** tab and click **+ Add location**. Give it a Label (for example the city), the address, and in India its GST number and GST treatment. Tick **Bill to this address** and/or **Ship to this address**. Click **Add location**. A company must keep at least one location, and a location with orders on it cannot be deleted ("This location has orders attached to it and can't be deleted.").

## Q: How do I change the account manager or caller of a company?
A: Open the company. At the top, click **Account manager** (who looks after the account) or **Caller** (who works it for lead generation). Pick the person and click **Save**. You need the **Reassign accounts and leads** permission, or you must be the current holder with the **Hand off your own accounts and leads** permission. The change is logged and the new person is notified.

## Q: How do I see everything that happened with a company?
A: Open the company and click **Activity** at the top. A panel opens on the right with everything, newest first: edits, leads, calls, notes, meetings, orders, documents, payments, tickets, tasks, visits and emails. Filter by kind, person or dates, and click **Show older** to go further back. You only see the kinds of records you are allowed to see elsewhere.

## Q: How do I change many companies at once?
A: On Sales → Companies or Sales → Customer, tick the rows. Then assign a caller (if you may change callers), or type in **Add tags (comma separated)**, and click **Apply**. Tags are added to the existing ones, never replaced.

## Q: How do I merge duplicate companies?
A: You need the **Merge duplicate companies** permission (Management by default).
1. Go to Sales → Duplicate companies. Pairs that look like the same company are marked **Very likely** or **Possibly**, with the reason (same GSTIN or PAN, same name, shared email domain or phone).
2. Click **Review merge** on a pair, or **Not duplicates** to stop it being offered again. You can also open the company that should stay and use **Merge a duplicate into this company**.
3. Check which company **Stays**, choose which details to keep, and untick anyone under **People in both** who is really two people.
4. Type the duplicate's name to confirm and click **Merge into**.
Warning: a merge moves everything (contacts, leads, orders, invoices, payments, tickets) and removes the duplicate. It cannot be undone.

## Q: How do I delete a company?
A: Normally you cannot. If the company is a duplicate, merge it into the company that stays (Sales → Duplicate companies). Only the workspace's super admin can delete a company, and only one with no history. Otherwise Deskzo says the company "can't be deleted".

## Q: One of our vendors also buys from us. Do I add them again?
A: No. One company can be both a vendor and a customer. When the name you type is already a vendor, click **Add as a customer too**. Or open the vendor and use **Add as a customer too** under Roles on its Details tab. It then appears in both lists and keeps one set of contacts, addresses and GSTIN. A customer you also buy from works the same way with **Add as a vendor too**.

## Q: Why are a customer's email and phone hidden?
A: The company is a reseller's end customer. You contact them through the reseller, so their details are hidden unless you have the **View reseller-managed contact details** permission.

## Q: How do I see what emails were sent to a customer?
A: Go to Sales → Mail log for all customers, or open a customer's **Emails** tab for one. Filter by status (Delivered, Opened, Bounced or failed, Held back and others), by Notices or Marketing, by channel, or by the date Sent. Open a row to see who sent it, what happened, and the email as sent. You need the **View emails sent to customers** permission.

## Q: How do I set up industries, customer categories and custom fields?
A: An admin fills these in once:
- Settings → Lists: industries and contact designations.
- Settings → Customer categories: groups of customers with an icon, colour and handling note (needs **Manage customer categories**).
- Settings → Custom fields: your own fields on companies and contacts.

## Q: How do I import or export companies and contacts?
A: Go to Settings → Import & export. Importing needs the **Import customer data** permission. Exporting needs the **Export customer data** permission, and an export holds only the accounts you can already see. No built-in role has these by default; admins do.
